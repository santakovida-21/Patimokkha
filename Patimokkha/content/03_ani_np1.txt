## කථා වස්තුව
@ සැවැත් නුවර · ජේතවනාරාමය
**උදායී** තෙරුන් තමාගේ උපස්ථායක පවුලක, විවාහ වී ගිය තරුණ කාන්තාවක සමඟ ==රහසිගත, වැසුණු ආසනයක තනිව හිඳ කථා කරමින් සිටියහ==. ආරාධනාවකට එම නිවසට ගිය **විශාඛා** මහා උපාසිකාව එය දැක, "ස්වාමීනි, මෙය නුසුදුසුයි. ඔබට ඒ කාරණයෙන් ප්‍රයෝජනයක් නැතත්, අප්‍රසන්න මිනිසුන් පහදවා ගැනීම අපහසුය" යි කීවා ය. තෙරුන් එය නොසැලකූ බැවින් ඇය භික්ෂූන්ට කීවා ය. භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ ශික්ෂාපදය පැනවූහ.
## (අ) පද විවරණය
**ඉමෙ ඛො පන** — "**ආයුෂ්මතුනි, මේ අනියත ධර්ම දෙක උද්දේශයට එයි**" යන නිදානයේ — එනම් **මෙසේ උද්දේශ කරනු ලබන** අනියත දෙක.
**මාතුගාමෙන** — එදින උපන් වුව ද ජීවමාන මනුෂ්‍ය ස්ත්‍රියක සමඟ.
**එකො එකාය** — එක් භික්ෂුවක් එක් ස්ත්‍රියක සමඟ.
**රහො** — **ඇසට රහස්** වූ තැන. පාළියේ කනට රහස ද කියා ඇතත්, මෙහි අදහස් කෙරෙන්නේ ඇසට රහස පමණි.
**පටිච්ඡන්නෙ ආසනෙ** — බිත්ති ආදියෙන් වැසුණු ස්ථානයක.
**අලංකම්මනියෙ** — කර්මයට සුදුසු, එනම් **අනාචාරයේ යෙදීමට හැකි** තරම් වූ තැනක.
**නිසජ්ජං කප්පෙය්‍ය** — හිඳී. **සැතපීම ද** හිඳීමට ම ඇතුළත් ය.
**සද්ධෙය්‍යවචසා** — විශ්වාස කළ යුතු වචන ඇති, **ආර්‍ය ශ්‍රාවිකාවක්**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
Ven. **Udāyī** ==sat alone in private, on a secluded seat, talking with a young married woman== of a family that supported him. The great lay follower **Visākhā**, who had gone to that house on invitation, saw this and said, “Venerable sir, this is not proper. Even if you have no interest in that, it is hard to convince people without faith.” He paid no attention, so she told the monks, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Ime kho pana** — in “**these two undetermined matters, venerables, come up for recitation**” — i.e. **the two undetermined rules recited here**.
**Mātugāmena** — with a living human woman, even one born that day.
**Eko ekāya** — one monk with one woman.
**Raho** — **private to the eye**. Although the Pāḷi also mentions privacy to the ear, only privacy to the eye is meant here.
**Paṭicchanne āsane** — in a place screened by walls and the like.
**Alaṃkammaniye** — suitable for the act, i.e. where **misconduct could take place**.
**Nisajjaṃ kappeyya** — sits. **Lying down** is included under sitting.
**Saddheyyavacasā** — whose word is to be trusted: **a noble disciple**.
## (B) Who removes the privacy?
|! Person present | Is privacy removed?
| A discerning man sitting at the door of a closed room (unable to see) | No
| A sighted man sitting within 12 cubits where he can see — even if distracted or nodding off | Yes — no offence
| A blind man standing nearby | No
| A sighted man lying down asleep | No
| Even a hundred women | No
## (C) “Aniyato” — dealt with by one of three
**Nisajjaṃ bhikkhu paṭijānamāno** — even when such a lay follower has seen and reports it, the monk is to be dealt with by one of the three — pārājika, saṅghādisesa, pācittiya — **only if he acknowledges the sitting**; not if he does not.
**Yena vā sā** — whatever she alleges along with the sitting — intercourse and so on — he is to be dealt with accordingly only if he acknowledges it; **not on her word alone**, since what is seen may be so or otherwise.
**Ayaṃ dhammo aniyato** — **undetermined**, because he is to be dealt with according to whichever offence or matter he acknowledges.
## (D) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning Ven. Udāyī, on the matter of sitting with a woman on such a seat. It is **not a common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (E) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Every effort — from putting on eye ointment onwards — to go to the woman, out of the enjoyment of privacy, a defilement bound up with sex | DK
| Having gone, whether she sits while he is seated, or he sits while she is seated, or both sit at once — through the sitting of both (Pācittiya 44) | PC
| If he admits bodily contact | SD
| If he admits intercourse | PR
| Lying down is treated the same way | —
## (F) No offence
# When a **discerning man** of the kind described — not lying asleep, not blind — is **within range**.
# For one **standing** (not sitting).
# For one **not seeking privacy**.
# For one whose **mind is on other things** — no offence by reason of sitting.
# For the insane and so on — **no offence of any of the three**.
## (G) Factors
The factors are to be understood **according to the offence admitted** — those of Pārājika 1 if intercourse is admitted, of Saṅghādisesa 2 if bodily contact, of Pācittiya 44 if only the sitting.
## (H) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**First-pārājika origin** (body–mind), **by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily action, unwholesome mind, two feelings** (pleasant, neutral). Depending on the offence admitted it may be **failure in virtue or failure in conduct**.

## (A) Explanation of terms

භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ **තුන් සිවුරු** අනුදැන වදාළ පසු, **ෂඩ්වර්ගීය භික්ෂූහු** "සිවුරු තුනක් අනුදැන ඇත" යි ==ගමට යන සිවුරු තුනක්, විහාරයේ සිටින සිවුරු තුනක්, නාන සිවුරු තුනක් යනුවෙන් අතිරේක සිවුරු දැරූහ==. භික්ෂූහු දොස් කීහ; භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ ශික්ෂාපදය පැනවූහ.
පසුව **ආනන්ද** තෙරුන්ට ලැබුණු අතිරේක සිවුරක් සාකේතයේ වැඩ සිටි **සාරිපුත්ත** තෙරුන්ට දීමට කැමති වූ කල්හි, "කවදා පැමිණේ ද?" යි ඇසූ භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ "නව දිනකින් හෝ දස දිනකින්" යි ඇසූ හෙයින් **දින දහයක් දක්වා** අතිරේක සිවුරක් තබා ගැනීමට අනුදැන "දසාහපරමං" අනුප්‍රඥප්තිය එක් කළහ.
## (අ) "නිට්ඨිතචීවරස්මිං උබ්භතස්මිං කථිනෙ"
**නිට්ඨිතචීවරස්මිං** — ඉඳිකටු වැඩ අවසන් වීමෙන් හෝ, "නැති විය, විනාශ විය, පිළිස්සිණි, සිවුරු බලාපොරොත්තුව සිඳුණි" යන ආකාරවලින් එකකින් සිවුර නිම වූ කල්හි; එනම් **සිවුරු පලිබෝධය** සිඳුණු කල්හි. කඨිනය අතුරා ඇති භික්ෂුවකට මේ ආකාරවලින් සිවුරු පලිබෝධය නොසිඳෙන තාක් කඨිනානිසංස ලැබේ.
**උබ්භතස්මිං කථිනෙ** — සංඝයා අතුරා ඇති කඨිනය ද උද්ධාරණය වූ කල්හි (කඨින පලිබෝධය ද සිඳුණු කල්හි).
## (ආ) කඨිනය අතිරීම හා උද්ධාරණය

## (ඇ) "අතිරෙකචීවරං" — සිවුරු වර්ග හා ප්‍රමාණය
**අතිරෙකචීවරං** — අධිෂ්ඨාන කළ හෝ විකප්පනය කළ දෙයට ඇතුළත් නොවූ බැවින් අතිරේක සිවුරයි.
**චීවරං** — ජාතිය අනුව **ඛෝම** (හණ/ලිනන්), **කප්පාසික** (කපු), **කෝසෙය්‍ය** (සේද), **කම්බල** (ලොම්), **සාණ** (හණ), **භංග** (මිශ්‍ර) යන සයෙන් හෝ ඒවාට අනුලෝම දෙයකින් එකකි. ප්‍රමාණය අනුව **විකප්පනයට සුදුසු අවම ප්‍රමාණය** — සුගත අඟලින් **දිග අඟල් 8, පළල අඟල් 4**.

## (ඈ) අධිෂ්ඨානය හා එය බිඳීම
අධිෂ්ඨානය **කයින්** (අතින් ගෙන සිතින් සලකා) හෝ **වචනයෙන්** කළ හැකිය. පැරණි සඟළ සිවුර "ඉමං සඞ්ඝාටිං පච්චුද්ධරාමි" යි පච්චුද්ධරණය කොට, නව සිවුර **හත්ථපාසයේ** ඇත්නම් "ඉමං සඞ්ඝාටිං අධිට්ඨාමි" යි ද, කාමරයක හෝ ළඟ විහාරයක ඇත්නම් තැබූ තැන සලකා "එතං සඞ්ඝාටිං අධිට්ඨාමි" යි ද කිව යුතුය. උතුරු සළුව, අඳනය ද තම තමන්ගේ නමින් ම ය.
**අධිෂ්ඨානය බිඳෙන කරුණු අට** — අනුන්ට දීම, පැහැර ගැනීම, විශ්වාසයෙන් ගැනීම, හීන බවට පෙරළීම (සිවුරු හැරීම), ශික්ෂාව ප්‍රතික්ෂේප කිරීම, මරණය, ලිංගය පෙරළීම, පච්චුද්ධරණය.
තුන් සිවුරේ **සුළඟිලි නියපොත්ත තරම් සිදුරක්** — එක නූලක් වත් නොකැඩී නොමැති නම් — ද අධිෂ්ඨානය බිඳියි (සඟළ සිවුරේ හා උතුරු සළුවේ දිග කෙළවරින් වියතක, පළල කෙළවරින් අඟල් අටක ඇතුළත; අඳනයේ පළල කෙළවරින් අඟල් සතරක ඇතුළත). එවිට එය අතිරේක සිවුරක් වේ; මසා නැවත අධිෂ්ඨාන කළ යුතුය.
## (ඉ) "දසාහපරමං" හා නිස්සජ්ජන විධිය
**දසාහපරමං** — දින දහයක් උපරිම සීමාව කොට. සිවුර ලැබුණු දිනයේ අරුණ ඒ දිනයට අයත් බැවින්, ලැබුණු දිනය සමඟ **එකොළොස්වන අරුණ නැගීමේදී** දින දහය ඉක්මවූයේ වේ.
**නිස්සග්ගියං පාචිත්තියං** — ඒ සිවුර **අත්හළ යුතු** (නිස්සග්ගිය) වේ; ඔහුට **පාචිත්තිය** ඇවතක් ද වේ. හෝ — "නිස්සජ්ජනය" යනු පළමුව කළ යුතු විනය කර්මයයි; එය සහිත පාචිත්තියයි.

| සිවුර ආපසු දීම | ඤත්තියෙන් "සඞ්ඝො ඉමං චීවරං ඉත්ථන්නාමස්ස භික්ඛුනො දදෙය්‍ය" යි ආපසු දිය යුතුය
ගණයකට ("ආයස්මන්තානං") හෝ පුද්ගලයෙකුට ("ආයස්මතො") ද නිස්සජ්ජනය කළ හැකිය. **මෙය සියලු නිස්සග්ගියවල විධියයි**; චීවරය, පාත්‍රය, නිසීදනය යනාදී වස්තුව පමණක් වෙනස් වේ. ආපසු නොදෙන භික්ෂුවට දුක්කටයි; ලේශයකින් පැහැර ගන්නේ නම් වටිනාකම අනුව විනිශ්චය කළ යුතුය.
## (ඊ) ප්‍රඥප්තිය හා ආණත්තිය
**වේසාලියේදී** ෂඩ්වර්ගීය භික්ෂූන් අරබයා අතිරේක සිවුරු දැරීමේ වස්තුවෙහි පැනවිණි. "දසාහපරමං" **අනුප්‍රඥප්තියයි**. **සාධාරණ ප්‍රඥප්තියකි**. **අනාණත්තිකයි**.

## The Story
@ Vesālī · Gotamaka shrine
After the Blessed One allowed **three robes**, **the group-of-six monks**, saying “Three robes are allowed,” ==kept extra sets: three robes for going into the village, three for staying in the monastery, three for bathing==. The monks complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
Later, **Ven. Ānanda** received an extra robe and wished to give it to **Ven. Sāriputta**, then at Sāketa. The Blessed One asked when he would come; told “in nine or ten days,” he allowed an extra robe to be kept **for up to ten days**, adding “ten days at most.”
## (A) “Niṭṭhitacīvarasmiṃ ubbhatasmiṃ kathine”
**Niṭṭhitacīvarasmiṃ** — when the robe is finished, either by completing the needlework or in one of the ways “it is lost, destroyed, burnt, the expectation of cloth is cut off”; i.e. when the **robe-impediment** is cut. A monk who has spread the kathina enjoys its privileges as long as the robe-impediment is not cut in these ways.
**Ubbhatasmiṃ kathine** — and when the kathina spread by the Saṅgha has been removed (the kathina-impediment also cut).
## (B) Spreading and removing the kathina
**Spreading the kathina** is allowed for those who completed the first rains; it requires **at least five** monks. If four or fewer completed the first rains, those of the later rains may make up the quorum, but they receive none of the benefits.
When a layman or monk righteously gives cloth — “Spread the kathina with this” — it is given by **a motion and one proclamation** to a monk fit to spread it. **That very day** he must cut it into five or more panels and make an outer robe, upper robe or lower robe, the other monks helping. Uncut, unstitched cloth is not allowed. He relinquishes the old robe, determines the new one, spreads the kathina — “I spread the kathina with this outer robe” — and goes to the monks within the boundary who completed the first rains, saying, “Venerable sirs, the Saṅgha's kathina has been spread; the spreading is lawful; rejoice in it.” **Only for those who rejoice** is the kathina spread.
|! The five benefits | Meaning
| Going without taking leave | Freedom from Pācittiya 46
| Not taking all three robes | Freedom from Nissaggiya 2
| As many robes as needed | Freedom from Nissaggiya 1
| Group meals | Freedom from Pācittiya 32
| Robe-cloth that arises | Cloth given to the Saṅgha in that residence
The kathina is removed by one of **eight grounds**: by departure, by completion, by resolution, by loss, by hearing, by the cutting off of hope, by crossing the boundary, by removal together (with the Saṅgha).
## (C) “Atirekacīvaraṃ” — kinds and size of cloth
**Atirekacīvaraṃ** — an extra robe, being outside what is determined or assigned.
**Cīvaraṃ** — by material one of six or an equivalent: **linen**, **cotton**, **silk**, **wool**, **hemp**, **mixed**. By size, **the smallest that can be assigned** — **8 by 4 sugata fingerbreadths**.
|! Requisite | Determining / assigning
| The three robes | To be determined; not assigned
| Rains-bathing cloth | Determined for the four rainy months; assigned afterwards
| Sitting cloth | Determined; not assigned — only one
| Spread | Determined; no limit of size or number
| Itch-cover | Determined while the illness lasts; assigned afterwards — only one
| Face-wiping cloth | Determined; no limit of number
| Requisite cloth | Determined; any number — bags and water strainers likewise
**Size of the three robes** — at most, less than a sugata robe; at least, the outer and upper robes **five fists** long and **three fists** wide; the lower robe five fists long and **two cubits** wide. What is larger or smaller is to be determined as “requisite cloth.” They are to be dyed and **marked** before determining.
## (D) Determining and its lapse
Determining is done **by body** (taking it in the hand with mental attention) or **by speech**. Having relinquished the old outer robe — “I relinquish this outer robe” — if the new one is **within arm's reach** he says, “I determine this outer robe”; if it is in a room or a nearby monastery, noting where it lies, “I determine that outer robe.” The upper and lower robes are determined by their own names.
**Determining lapses in eight ways** — giving it to another, its being snatched, its being taken on trust, reverting to a lower state (disrobing), renouncing the training, death, change of sex, relinquishing.
For the three robes, **a hole the size of the little fingernail** — with not even a thread left across — also breaks the determination (within a span of the long edges and eight fingerbreadths of the short edges of the outer and upper robes; within four fingerbreadths of the short edge of the lower robe). It then counts as an extra robe; it must be mended and determined again.
## (E) “Dasāhaparamaṃ” and the procedure of forfeiture
**Dasāhaparamaṃ** — with ten days as the limit. Since the dawn of the day the cloth arises belongs to that day, the ten days are exceeded **at the eleventh dawn**, counting the day it arose.
**Nissaggiyaṃ pācittiyaṃ** — the cloth is **to be forfeited**, and he has an offence of **pācittiya**. Or: “forfeiture” is the Vinaya act to be done first; it is a pācittiya together with that.
|! Step | Done before the Saṅgha
| Forfeiting | “Venerable sirs, this robe of mine, kept beyond ten days, is to be forfeited; I forfeit it to the Saṅgha.”
| Confessing | “Venerable sirs, I have fallen into one nissaggiya pācittiya; I confess it.” — “two” if two, “many” if more
| Receiving the confession | A competent monk informs the Saṅgha (“…I would receive the offence of so-and-so”); “Do you see it?” — “Yes, I see it”; “Restrain yourself in future” — “Well indeed, I will restrain myself.”
| Returning the robe | By motion: “The Saṅgha should give this robe to so-and-so.”
It may also be forfeited to a group (“to the Venerables”) or to one person (“to the Venerable”). **This is the procedure for all the nissaggiyas**; only the object — robe, bowl, sitting cloth and so on — differs. A monk who does not give it back has a dukkaṭa; one who appropriates it by a pretext is dealt with by its value.
## (F) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Vesālī** concerning the group-of-six monks, on the matter of keeping extra robes. “Ten days at most” is the **supplementary rule**. It is a **common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (G) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Ten days exceeded — whether he perceives it as exceeded, not exceeded, or is in doubt | NP
| Exceeding ten days with an undetermined, unassigned, ungiven, unlost… robe perceived as determined and so on | NP
| Not exceeded, but perceiving it as exceeded or in doubt | DK
| Using it without forfeiting (**in every nissaggiya**) | DK
## (H) No offence
Within the ten days —
# When it is **determined**, **assigned**, **given away**.
# When it is **lost**, **destroyed**, **burnt**, **snatched**.
# When it is **taken on trust**.
# For the insane and so on, and for the first offenders.
## (I) Factors (= pañca — five)
# A robe of proper material and size **belonging to him**.
# It **counts** (not determined or assigned).
# **The impediments cut** (robe and kathina).
# It is **an extra robe**.
# **Ten days exceeded**.
## (J) Origin, the rest, and failure
**The kathina origin** (body and speech); **by not doing** (not determining or assigning); **not freed by perception; without intention; blameworthy by rule; bodily and verbal action; three kinds of mind; three feelings**.
**Failure** — in both Pātimokkhas the pārājikas and saṅghādisesas are **failure in virtue**; all the other offences are **failure in conduct**. There is no offence that is itself failure in livelihood or in view; but **because of failure in livelihood** six classes of offence (all except dubbhāsita) are laid down, and **because of failure in view** two classes (pācittiya and dukkaṭa).

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**A number of monks** ==left their outer robes with fellow monks and set out on tour wearing only the lower and upper robes==. The robes left behind lay so long that they became mouldy, and the monks looking after them put them out in the sun. Learning of this, the Blessed One laid down the rule.
Later at Kosambī a sick monk, invited by his relatives to be nursed, could not carry all three robes; so the Blessed One allowed **an authorisation not to be apart** for a sick monk, adding “except with the authorisation of the monks.”
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Niṭṭhitacīvarasmiṃ bhikkhunā** — here the instrumental is to be taken **in the possessive sense**: “when the monk's robe is finished” (there is no act to be done by the monk here). The monk whose impediments are cut by the robe being finished and the kathina removed —
**Ekarattampi ce bhikkhu ticīvarena vippavaseyya** — if he dwells apart from **any one** of his outer robe and the rest, determined as the triple robe, **even for one night**.
**Vippavaseyya** — leaving the robe in one of the **fifteen places** listed in the Pāḷi, he lets dawn rise **beyond arm's reach** of it.
## (B) The fifteen places
|! Place | Description
| Village (incl. town) | Of one family or of several; fenced or unfenced
| House | A dwelling
| Storehouse | A store
| Watchtower | A tower
| Round house | A circular building
| Mansion | A palace
| Gabled house | A multi-storey house with a roof
| Boat | A ship or boat
| Caravan | A travelling caravan
| Field | A cultivated field
| Threshing floor | A threshing place
| Park | A garden
| Monastery | A monastery
| Foot of a tree | Under a tree
| Open air | In the open
Each is detailed in the Samantapāsādikā as of one precinct or several, fenced or unfenced, and so on.
## (C) “Aññatra bhikkhusammutiyā”
Except for the **authorisation not to be apart from the triple robe**, given by the Saṅgha to **a sick monk**, a monk without it who is apart for one night has a nissaggiya pācittiya. The forfeiture formula: “Venerable sirs, this robe of mine, apart for a night without the authorisation of the monks, is to be forfeited…”
One with the authorisation should return **when the illness subsides**, or relinquish the robe where he is. If the same or another illness flares up again, the earlier authorisation still holds.
## (D) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning a number of monks, on the matter of setting out on tour with only lower and upper robes. “Except with the authorisation of the monks” is the **supplementary rule**. It is a **common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (E) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Being apart — whether he perceives it as apart, not apart, or is in doubt | NP
| A robe not relinquished, not given… perceived as relinquished and so on | NP
| Not apart, but perceiving it as apart or in doubt | DK
## (F) No offence
# **Relinquished before dawn**.
# **Given away, lost, destroyed, burnt, snatched, taken on trust**.
# Being apart **with the monks' authorisation**.
# For the insane and so on, and for the first offenders.
## (G) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# **A determined robe**.
# **The kathina not spread**.
# **No authorisation**.
# **Being apart for a night** (dawn rising).
## (H) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Kaṭhina origin** (body–speech; body–speech–mind), **by not doing** (**not relinquishing**), **no release by perception, without intention, blameworthy by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of mind, three feelings**.

එක් භික්ෂුවකට අකාල සිවුරක් ලැබුණු නමුත් එය සිවුරක් කිරීමට නොසෑහුණි. ඔහු ==එය නැවත නැවත දිග හරිමින්, රැලි හරිමින් තබා ගත්තේය==. භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ කරුණ විමසා, **අඩුව පිරවීමේ බලාපොරොත්තුවක්** ඇත්නම් අකාල සිවුර තබා ගැනීමට අනුදැන්නහ. **බොහෝ භික්ෂූහු** "අනුදැන ඇත" යි සිතා එය මසකට වඩා තබා ගත් කල්හි ශික්ෂාපදය පැනවූහ.
## (අ) "අකාලචීවරං" යන්නෙහි අර්ථය
**චීවර කාලය** — කඨිනය නොඅතුළු විට **වස්සානයේ අවසන් මාසය**; කඨිනය අතුළු විට **මාස පහ**.
**අකාලචීවරං** — ඒ කාලය හැර අන් කාලයක ලැබුණු දෙය; හෝ කාලය තුළ වුව ද සංඝයාට "මෙය අකාල සිවුරකි" යි, හෝ පුද්ගලයෙකුට "මෙය ඔබට දෙමි" යි ආදී ලෙස දුන් දෙය.
## (ආ) "උප්පජ්ජෙය්‍ය" — සිවුරු ලැබෙන මාතිකා අට
තමාගේ කොටසක් ලෙස සංඝයාගෙන්, ගණයකින්, ඥාතියෙකුගෙන්, මිත්‍රයෙකුගෙන්, පංසුකූලයක් ලෙස, තමාගේ ධනයෙන්, හෝ මේ **මාතිකා අටෙන්** එකකින් ලැබේ:
|! මාතිකාව | පැහැදිලි කිරීම
| සීමාය දෙති | "සීමාවට දෙමි" — සීමා පහළොවෙන් කුමන සීමාව ද යන්න අනුව බෙදේ (උදා: උපචාර සීමාවට දුන් දෙය එහි ඇතුළත ඛණ්ඩ සීමාවල සිටින්නන්ට ද ලැබේ). කුමන සීමාව දැයි නොදන්නේ නම් උපචාර සීමාවේ සිටින අය බෙදා ගනිති.
| කතිකාය දෙති | "සමාන ලාභ කතිකාවක්" කළ විහාර දෙකක් — එකක දෙන දෙය අනෙකේ සිටින්නාට ද ලැබේ.
| භික්ඛාපඤ්ඤත්තියා දෙති | "මගේ ධුවකාර (නිත්‍ය දාන) පවත්වන තැනට" — දායකයාගේ පරිත්‍යාගය පවත්වන තැන්.
| සඞ්ඝස්ස දෙති | උපචාර සීමාවට ආ සියලු දෙනාට හා ඔවුන් හා එකාබද්ධ අයට.
| උභතොසඞ්ඝස්ස දෙති | භාගයක් භික්ෂූන්ට, භාගයක් භික්ෂුණීන්ට — එක් අයෙකු පමණක් සිටියත්.
| වස්සංවුට්ඨසඞ්ඝස්ස දෙති | එම විහාරයේ වස් කැඩීමකින් තොරව පුරිම වස් වැසූ අයට.
| ආදිස්ස දෙති | නියම කොට — උදා: "මාගේ කැඳ පානය කළ අයට."
| පුග්ගලස්ස දෙති | "මෙය අසවලාට දෙමි" — පරම්මුඛව හෝ පාමුල තබා සම්මුඛව.
## (ඇ) "සතියා පච්චාසාය" — මාසයක් තබා ගැනීම
**ආකඞ්ඛමානෙන** — කැමති නම් පිළිගත හැකිය. **ඛිප්පමෙව කාරෙතබ්බං** — **දින දහය ඇතුළත** ම සිවුර කරවිය යුතුය.
**නො චස්ස පාරිපූරි** — අධිෂ්ඨාන සිවුරක් කිරීමට ප්‍රමාණවත් නොවේ නම්.
**සතියා පච්චාසාය** — "අසවල් දිනයේ සංඝයාට සිවුරු ලැබේ, එයින් මට ලැබේ", "පංසුකූලයක් ලැබේ", "මේ කැප දෙයින් සිවුරක් ගනිමි" ආදී ලෙස **සිවුරු බලාපොරොත්තුවක් ඇති** කල්හි — **උපරිම මාසයක්** තබා ගත හැකිය.
|! තත්ත්වය | කළ යුතු දෙය
| මුල් සිවුර සිනිඳු, බලාපොරොත්තු වූ සිවුර රළු — එක් කළ නොහැකි, මාසය තවම නොපිරුණු | අකැමැත්තෙන් සිවුර නොකළ යුතුය; කාලය තුළ තවත් බලාපොරොත්තු සිවුරක් ලබා කළ යුතුය; නොලැබේ නම් පච්චාසා සිවුර ද පරික්ඛාරචෝළ ලෙස අධිෂ්ඨාන කළ යුතුය.
| මුල් සිවුර රළු, බලාපොරොත්තු වූ සිවුර සිනිඳු | මුල් සිවුර පරික්ඛාරචෝළ ලෙස අධිෂ්ඨාන කොට, පච්චාසා සිවුර ම **මුල් සිවුර** ලෙස තැබිය හැකිය — එයට නැවත මාසයක් ලැබේ.
**තතො චෙ උත්තරි** — මාසය ඉක්මවා තබයි නම් නිස්සග්ගියයි. නිස්සජ්ජන වචනය: "ඉදං මෙ, භන්තෙ, අකාලචීවරං මාසාතික්කන්තං නිස්සග්ගියං…"
## (ඈ) ප්‍රඥප්තිය හා ආණත්තිය
**සැවැත් නුවරදී** බොහෝ භික්ෂූන් අරබයා අකාල සිවුරු මසකට වඩා තබා ගැනීමේ වස්තුවෙහි පැනවිණි. **සාධාරණ ප්‍රඥප්තියකි**. **අනාණත්තිකයි**.
## (ඉ) ආපත්ති භේදය — කඞ්ඛාවිතරණී අනුව
==**මාසය** ඉක්මවූ කල්හි== නිස්සග්ගිය වේ:
|! ආකාරය | ඇවැත
| මාසය ඉක්මවූ කල්හි — ඕනෑම සංඥාවකින් | NP
| මාසය නොඉක්මවූ නමුත් ඉක්මවූ යැයි සිතීම හෝ සැක කිරීම | DK
| නිස්සජ්ජනය නොකොට පරිභෝග කිරීම | DK
## (ඊ) අනාපත්ති
මාසය ඇතුළත —
# **අධිෂ්ඨාන**, **විකප්පනය**, **දීම**.
# **නැති වීම, විනාශ වීම, පිළිස්සීම, පැහැර ගැනීම, විශ්වාසයෙන් ගැනීම**.
# උමතු ආදීන්ට හා ආදිකම්මිකයන්ට.
## (උ) අංග (= පඤ්ච — පහකි)
# ජාතිය හා ප්‍රමාණයෙන් යුත් **අකාල සිවුරක්** තමා සතු වීම.
# **ගණනට ඇතුළත්** වීම.
# **පලිබෝධ සිඳුණු** බව.
# **අතිරේක** බව.
# **මාසය ඉක්මවීම**.
## (ඌ) සමුට්ඨානාදිය
**කථින සමුට්ඨානයි, අකිරියයි, නෝසඤ්ඤාවිමොක්ඛයි, අචිත්තකයි, පණ්ණත්තිවජ්ජයි, කාය-වචීකම්මයි, තිචිත්තයි, තිවේදනයි**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
A monk received out-of-season cloth, but it was not enough to make a robe. He ==kept it, unfolding it and smoothing it out again and again==. The Blessed One asked the reason and allowed out-of-season cloth to be kept **in expectation of making up the shortfall**. **A number of monks**, thinking “It is allowed,” kept such cloth for more than a month, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) The meaning of “akālacīvaraṃ”
**The robe season** — **the last month of the rains** when the kathina has not been spread; **five months** when it has.
**Akālacīvaraṃ** — what arises outside that season; or, even within it, what is given to the Saṅgha as “out-of-season cloth,” or to an individual with “I give this to you” and the like.
## (B) “Uppajjeyya” — the eight grounds for the arising of cloth
It may come as his share from the Saṅgha, a group, a relative, a friend, as rag-cloth, with his own funds, or by one of **eight grounds**:
|! Ground | Explanation
| Given to a boundary | “I give to the boundary” — shared according to which of the fifteen boundaries is meant (e.g. what is given to the precinct boundary reaches those in minor boundaries within it). If the donor does not know, those within the precinct boundary share it.
| Given by agreement | Two monasteries with an “equal gains” agreement — what is given in one reaches one living in the other.
| Given where alms are arranged | “Where my regular offerings are made” — wherever the donor's giving takes place.
| Given to the Saṅgha | To all within the precinct boundary and those joined with them.
| Given to both Saṅghas | Half to the monks, half to the nuns — even if only one is present.
| Given to the Saṅgha that spent the rains | To those who completed the first rains there without breaking it.
| Given by specifying | E.g. “To those who drank my rice gruel.”
| Given to an individual | “I give this to so-and-so” — in his absence, or face to face, placing it at his feet.
## (C) “Satiyā paccāsāya” — keeping it for a month
**Ākaṅkhamānena** — he may accept it if he wishes. **Khippameva kāretabbaṃ** — the robe is to be made **within ten days**.
**No cassa pāripūri** — if it is not enough to make a determined robe.
**Satiyā paccāsāya** — when there is **an expectation of cloth** — “On such a day the Saṅgha will receive robes and I will get one,” “I will get rag-cloth,” “I will buy cloth with this allowable item” — he may keep it **for a month at most**.
|! Situation | What to do
| Original cloth fine, expected cloth coarse — they cannot be joined, the month not yet over | He should not make the robe unwillingly; he should obtain other expected cloth within the time; failing that, he should determine the expected cloth as requisite cloth.
| Original cloth coarse, expected cloth fine | He may determine the original as requisite cloth and keep the expected cloth as the **new original** — it then gets a fresh month.
**Tato ce uttari** — if he keeps it beyond the month, it is to be forfeited. Formula: “Venerable sirs, this out-of-season robe-cloth of mine, kept beyond a month, is to be forfeited…”
## (D) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning a number of monks, on the matter of keeping out-of-season cloth for more than a month. It is a **common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (E) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
It becomes nissaggiya ==when **the month** is exceeded==:
|! Case | Offence
| The month exceeded — whatever his perception | NP
| Not exceeded, but perceiving it as exceeded or in doubt | DK
| Using it without forfeiting | DK
## (F) No offence
Within the month —
# **Determined**, **assigned**, **given away**.
# **Lost, destroyed, burnt, snatched, taken on trust**.
# For the insane and so on, and for the first offenders.
## (G) Factors (= pañca — five)
# **Out-of-season cloth** of proper material and size belonging to him.
# It **counts**.
# **The impediments cut**.
# It is **extra**.
# **The month exceeded**.
## (H) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**The kathina origin; by not doing; not freed by perception; without intention; blameworthy by rule; bodily and verbal action; three kinds of mind; three feelings**.

## කථා වස්තුව
@ සැවැත් නුවර · ජේතවනාරාමය
**උදායී** තෙරුන්ගේ ගිහි කාලයේ බිරිඳ භික්ෂුණියක් ව සිටියා ය. ඇය නිතර උන්වහන්සේ වෙත පැමිණියා ය. දිනක තෙරුන්ගේ අසූචියෙන් ==අපිරිසිදු වූ අඳනය සෝදන්නට ගත් ඇය== එයින් කොටසක් ගෙන ගැබ් ගත්තා ය. භික්ෂුණීහු "මේ භික්ෂුණිය බඹසර නොහැසිරේ" යි කීහ; ඇය කරුණ කීවා ය. භික්ෂූහු "භික්ෂුණියක ලවා පරණ සිවුරක් සෝදවන්නේ කෙසේ ද?" යි දොස් කීහ. භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ ශික්ෂාපදය පැනවූහ.
## (අ) පද විවරණය
**අඤ්ඤාතිකාය** — ඥාති නොවන; **මවගේ හෝ පියාගේ පැත්තෙන් සත්වන පරම්පරාව** දක්වා කිසිදු ආකාරයකින් සම්බන්ධ නොවූ. (ඉදිරියේ සියලු තැන මෙලෙස ම ය.)
**භික්ඛුනියා** — ශාක්‍ය කුමරියන් මෙන් **ශුද්ධ භික්ෂු සංඝයා තුළ** හෝ **උභතෝ සංඝයා තුළ** උපසම්පදාව ලත්.
**පුරාණචීවරං** — පඬු පොවා කප්ප බින්දුව තබා **එක් වරක් වුව ද** හැඳ හෝ පොරවා ඇති; අවම වශයෙන් පරිභෝග වශයෙන් **උරහිසේ හෝ හිසේ තබාගෙන** මඟ ගිය, හෝ **කොට්ටයක් කොට** සැතපුණු සිවුර ද පුරාණ සිවුරකි.
**ධොවාපෙය්‍ය** — "සෝදන්න" යි වචනයෙන් කීම, කාය චලනයක් කිරීම, අතට අතින් දීම, පාමුල තැබීම, රියන් 12 ඇතුළත සිට උඩින් විසි කිරීම, හෝ අනුන් අත යැවීම — ඇය සේදුවොත් සෝදවූයේ ම වේ. **රජාපෙය්‍ය** (පඬු පෙවීම) හා **ආකොටාපෙය්‍ය** (තළා මැදීම) ද මෙලෙස ම ය.
ශික්ෂමානා, සාමණේරී හෝ උපාසිකාවකට දුන් සිවුර ඇය **උපසම්පදාව ලබා** සේදුවොත් ද, උපාසකයෙකුට, සාමණේරයෙකුට හෝ තරුණ භික්ෂුවකට දුන් සිවුර ඔහු **ලිංගය පෙරළී** උපසම්පන්න වී සේදුවොත් ද නිස්සග්ගිය පාචිත්තියයි.
## (ආ) ප්‍රඥප්තිය හා ආණත්තිය
**සැවැත් නුවරදී** උදායී ස්ථවිරයන් අරබයා පරණ සිවුරක් සෝදවීමේ වස්තුවෙහි පැනවිණි. **අසාධාරණ ප්‍රඥප්තියකි**. "සෝදන්න" ආදී අණ කිරීමෙන් සිදු වන බැවින් **සාණත්තිකයි**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
Ven. **Udāyī**'s former wife had become a nun, and she often visited him. One day ==she took his lower robe, soiled with semen, to wash it==, took some of it and conceived. The nuns said, “This nun is not celibate”; she explained. The monks complained, “How can he have a nun wash an old robe?” and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Aññātikāya** — unrelated: not connected in any way **through mother or father back to the seventh generation**. (Likewise everywhere hereafter.)
**Bhikkhuniyā** — one fully ordained, like the Sakyan women, **in the monks' Saṅgha alone** or **in both Saṅghas**.
**Purāṇacīvaraṃ** — a robe dyed, marked and **worn even once**, as lower or upper robe; even one carried **on the shoulder or head** on a journey as a way of using it, or **used as a pillow**, is an old robe.
**Dhovāpeyya** — saying “Wash it,” making a gesture, handing it over, placing it at her feet, tossing it to her from within 12 cubits, or sending it by another — if she washes it, he has had it washed. **Rajāpeyya** (having it dyed) and **ākoṭāpeyya** (having it beaten) are the same.
If a robe given to a trainee nun, novice nun or laywoman is washed by her **after she is ordained**; or one given to a layman, novice or young monk is washed by him **after a change of sex** and ordination as a nun — it is still a nissaggiya pācittiya.
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning Ven. Udāyī, on the matter of having an old robe washed. It is **not a common rule**. As it arises by command — “Wash it” and so on — it is **sāṇattika**.
## (C) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| For the monk, in every effort of the nun so commanded — setting up the oven and so on | DK
| The moment it is washed and lifted out, dyed, or beaten | NP
| Having all three or two done — washing, dyeing, beating — one is nissaggiya, the rest dukkaṭa | NP + DK
| Told “Wash it,” she does all three — only by reason of the washing | NP
| With one utterance, “Do whatever needs doing to this robe” — a pācittiya with two dukkaṭas | NP + DK
| An unrelated nun — perceived as related or in doubt (**triple pācittiya**) | NP
| A related nun perceived as unrelated, or in doubt | DK
| Having it washed by one ordained only on the nuns' side | DK
| Having another's robe, sitting cloth or spread washed | DK
**Triple pācittiya** — hereafter in such places “triple pācittiya” means: concerning an unrelated person, it is a nissaggiya pācittiya **whichever of the three perceptions** — unrelated, related, or doubtful.
## (D) No offence
# When **an unrelated companion of a related nun** washes it unasked.
# When **an unused robe** or **another requisite** is washed.
# When **a trainee nun or novice nun** washes it.
# For the insane and so on, and for the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **An old robe**.
# **Commanding an unrelated nun**, standing within range.
# Her **washing and so on**.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**The go-between origin; by doing; not freed by perception; without intention; blameworthy by rule; bodily and verbal action; three kinds of mind; three feelings**.

## The Story
@ Rājagaha · Veḷuvana
The arahant nun **Uppalavaṇṇā**, living at Sāvatthī, brought meat that thieves had offered her to the Veḷuvana at Rājagaha, handed it to Ven. Udāyī for the Blessed One, and as she was about to leave, he ==asked for her lower robe==. She said, “Cloth is hard for women to get; this is my fifth robe,” but as he kept asking, she gave it and returned to her residence with four robes. The nuns found out and complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
Later, monks were anxious about receiving robes from nuns even **in exchange**, so “except in exchange” was added.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Cīvaraṃ** — any of the six kinds of cloth **large enough to be assigned**. (So in all rules about robes.)
**Paṭiggaṇheyya** — whether handed to him, placed at his feet, or tossed to him from beyond range while he is teaching, **if he consents** it is accepted.
It is allowable to take what is **sent by an unordained person**. What is left on a rubbish heap “so that he may take it as rag-cloth” may be taken **as rag-cloth**.
**Aññatra pārivattakā** — except an exchange, made by giving **even a piece of myrobalan**, or with the intention “I will give,” taking **even a cloth water-strainer** of assignable size is nissaggiya.
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Rājagaha** concerning Ven. Udāyī, on the matter of accepting a robe. “Except in exchange” is the **supplementary rule**. It is **not a common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.

# **In exchange**.
# Taking **on trust**; taking **temporarily**.
# **Requisites not to be determined**, such as a bowl-bag.
# Taking from **a trainee nun or novice nun**.
# For the insane and so on, and for the first offender.

# **A robe large enough to be assigned**.
# **Not an exchange**.

## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Matchmaking (go-between) origin** (six origins — body; speech; body–speech; body–mind; speech–mind; body–speech–mind), **by doing and not doing** (taking is doing, not giving in exchange is not doing), **no release by perception, without intention, blameworthy by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of mind, three feelings**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
When Ven. **Upananda the Sakyan** taught a merchant's son, the young man invited him: “Venerable sir, tell me anything we can give.” Upananda ==asked for the costly cloth he was wearing==. He offered to send it from home, but Upananda insisted, so he gave it and went home in a single garment. People complained, “The recluses won't even let us go about clothed,” and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
Later, monks **robbed of their robes** on the road from Sāketa to Sāvatthī, anxious about asking, went on naked; so “except at the proper occasion” was added.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Aññātakaṃ** — unrelated. **Gahapatiṃ** — for monks, **a man not gone forth**; **gahapatāniṃ** — for nuns, a woman not gone forth. (So in all rules about householders.)
**Viññāpeyya** — asks, or has another ask.
**Aññatra samayā** — except at the occasion when a monk's robe has been **snatched** or **lost**.
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning Ven. Upananda, on the matter of asking for robes. “Except at the proper occasion” is the **supplementary rule**. It is a **common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (C) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| The effort of asking, other than at the proper occasion | DK
| On receiving (**triple pācittiya**) | NP
| A relative perceived as unrelated, or in doubt | DK
## (D) No offence
# Asking **at the proper occasion** (robe snatched or lost).
# Asking **relatives** or **those who have invited** him.
# Asking **for another**, from that person's own relatives or inviters.
# Obtaining it **with his own funds**.
# For the insane and so on, and for the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# **A robe large enough to be assigned**.
# **Not the proper occasion**.
# **Asking an unrelated person**.
# **Receiving it** by that asking.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
The go-between origin; by doing; not freed by perception; without intention; blameworthy by rule; bodily and verbal action; three kinds of mind; three feelings.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six monks** went to monks whose robes had been stolen, saying, “We will get you robes,” and going to people ==asked for far more robes than were needed, keeping some for themselves==. When people learned that the robbed monks had not been given them, they complained, “Are the recluses trading in cloth?” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Tañce** — that monk whose robe has been snatched or lost.
**Abhihaṭṭhuṃ pavāreyya** — invites him to take — physically bringing it and setting it before him, or saying, “Take as much as you like from our cloth store.”
**Santaruttaraparamaṃ** — with **a lower robe and an upper robe** as the most; no more should be taken.
## (B) How much may be taken
|! Robes lost | May take
| All three (determined robes) | **Two** — one to wear, one to cover; the third to be sought from friendly monks
| Two | **One**
| Both (for one who normally goes with only lower and upper robes) | **Two**
| One of three | **None**
| One of two | **One**
| He had only one, and it is lost | **Two**
| A nun — five lost / four / three or fewer | Two / one / none
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six monks, on the matter of asking for many robes. It is a **common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Efforts of asking beyond the lower-and-upper-robe limit | DK
| On receiving (**triple pācittiya**) | NP
| A relative perceived as unrelated, or in doubt | DK
## (E) No offence
# Making two robes and taking the cloth saying, **“I will bring back the rest.”**
# When told, **“Let the rest be yours.”**
# Taking what is given **not on account of snatching or loss**.
# Asking **relatives** or **those who have invited** him.
# Obtaining it **with his own funds**.
# For the insane and so on, and for the first offenders.
## (F) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# **More than that** (beyond the limit).
# **Because of the snatching** or other cause.
# **Asking an unrelated person**.
# **Receiving it** by that asking.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Matchmaking (go-between) origin** (six origins — body; speech; body–speech; body–mind; speech–mind; body–speech–mind), **by doing**, **no release by perception, without intention, blameworthy by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of mind, three feelings**.

## (අ) පද විවරණය
**භික්ඛුං පනෙව උද්දිස්ස** — "අසවල් භික්ෂුවට දෙමි" යි නියම කොට.
**චීවරචෙතාපන්නං** — රන් ආදී **සිවුරු මිල**. **උපක්ඛටං හොති** — සූදානම් කර, එකතු කර තබා ඇත.
**චෙතාපෙත්වා** — හුවමාරු කොට, කරවා හෝ මිලට ගෙන.
**චීවරෙන අච්ඡාදෙස්සාමි** — ව්‍යවහාර වචනයකි; "අසවල් භික්ෂුවට දෙමි" යන්න අර්ථයි.
**පුබ්බෙ අප්පවාරිතො** — පෙර පවාරණය නොකරන ලද භික්ෂුව එහි ගොස්.
**විකප්පං ආපජ්ජෙය්‍ය** — **විශේෂ නියමයකට** පැමිණේ — "සාධු වත මං ආයස්මා…" (යාච්ඤාවකි; "වත" — සිතීමක්) — **එවරූපං වා එවරූපං වා** — දිග, පළල ආදී එකක් කියමින්.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
A monk on almsround heard a man tell his wife, “Let us present a robe to Master **Upananda**,” and told Upananda. Upananda went to the man and ==specified: “If you are going to give me a robe, give me one of such length, such width, such thickness, such fineness.”== The man complained, “These recluses are greedy; one can't even give them a robe easily.” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Bhikkhuṃ paneva uddissa** — designating, “I will give to such-and-such a monk.”
**Cīvaracetāpannaṃ** — **a robe fund**, gold and the like. **Upakkhaṭaṃ hoti** — has been prepared, set aside.
**Cetāpetvā** — having exchanged, had it made or bought.
**Cīvarena acchādessāmi** — a manner of speaking; the meaning is “I will give to such-and-such a monk.”
**Pubbe appavārito** — the monk, not previously invited, goes there.
**Vikappaṃ āpajjeyya** — makes **a special stipulation** — “sādhu vata maṃ āyasmā…” (“sādhu” a request, “vata” a reflection) — **evarūpaṃ vā evarūpaṃ vā**, naming length, width and the like.
**Kalyāṇakamyataṃ upādāya** — wanting something fine, special.
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning Ven. Upananda, on the matter of making stipulations about a robe. It is a **common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (C) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| In the efforts of the donor who, at his word, raises the price first intended and buys a finer robe | DK
| On receiving (**triple pācittiya**) | NP
| A relative perceived as unrelated, or in doubt | DK
Formula: “Venerable sirs, this robe of mine, obtained by approaching an unrelated householder without prior invitation and making a stipulation, is to be forfeited…”
## (D) No offence
# Asking one who wants to buy a **costly** robe for a **cheaper** one.
# Saying, “Give me another of this kind,” **at the same price**.
# From **relatives** or **those who have invited** him.
# Obtaining it **with his own funds**.
# For the insane and so on, and for the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Wanting something more** in the robe.
# **Asking an unrelated person**.
# **Receiving it** by that asking.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Matchmaking (go-between) origin** (six origins — body; speech; body–speech; body–mind; speech–mind; body–speech–mind), **by doing**, **no release by perception, without intention, blameworthy by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of mind, three feelings**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
Learning that **two** men had each set aside funds to give him a robe, Ven. **Upananda** went to them and said, ==“If you are going to give me robes, give me one robe of such-and-such a kind, the two of you together.”== They complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Ubhinnaṃ aññātakānaṃ gahapatīnaṃ** — **of two householders who are not relatives**.
**Paccekacīvaracetāpannāni** — **robe funds** (gold and the like) prepared **separately** by each. **Upakkhaṭāni honti** — have been prepared, set aside.
**Cīvarehi acchādessāma** — a manner of speaking; the meaning is “**we will give to such-and-such a monk**.”
**Pubbe appavārito** — the monk, **not previously invited**, goes there.
**Vikappaṃ āpajjeyya** — makes **a special stipulation of length, width and the like**: “**Together, buy me one robe**.”
**Kalyāṇakamyataṃ upādāya** — **wanting something fine, special**.
This rule is **like a supplement to the first robe-fund rule**.
|! Point | First robe-fund | Second robe-fund
| Who is pressed | **One** donor | **Two** (or more)
| What is asked | A special robe by raising the price | One special robe by **combining the funds**
Pressing **many** donors in the same way is also an offence.
Formula: “Venerable sirs, this robe of mine, obtained by approaching unrelated householders without prior invitation and making a stipulation, is to be forfeited…”
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning Ven. Upananda. It is a **common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (C) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| In the efforts of the donors who, at his word, combine their funds and buy a special robe | DK
| On receiving (**triple pācittiya**) | NP
| Relatives perceived as unrelated, or in doubt | DK
## (D) No offence
# Asking those who would buy a costly robe for a **cheaper** one; asking for another robe at the same price.
# From **relatives** or **those who have invited** him.
# Obtaining it **with his own funds**.
# For the insane and so on, and for the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Wanting something more** in the robe.
# **Asking unrelated people**.
# **Receiving it** by that asking.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Matchmaking (go-between) origin** (six origins — body; speech; body–speech; body–mind; speech–mind; body–speech–mind), **by doing**, **no release by perception, without intention, blameworthy by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of mind, three feelings**.

## කථා වස්තුව
@ සැවැත් නුවර · ජේතවනාරාමය
**උපනන්ද** තෙරුන්ගේ උපස්ථායක මහාමාත්‍යයෙක් දූතයෙකු අත සිවුරු මිලක් යවා, "මෙයින් සිවුරක් ගෙන උපනන්ද ස්වාමීන්ට පුදන්න" යි උපාසකයෙකුට භාර දෙන්නට සැලැස්වීය. උපාසකයා "කැමති විට පැමිණෙන්න" යි කීවේය. එදින **නගර සභා රැස්වීමක්** ඇති බැවින් "අද එක් දිනක් ඉවසන්න" යි කීවත්, තෙරුන් =="අද ම සිවුර දිය යුතුය" යි බල කොට ඔහු නවතා ගත්හ==. රැස්වීමට ප්‍රමාද වූ ඔහුට **කහවණු පනහක දඩයක්** නියම විය. මිනිස්සු දොස් කීහ. භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ ශික්ෂාපදය පැනවූහ.
## (අ) පද විවරණය
**රාජභොග්ගො** — රජුගෙන් භෝග ලබන තැනැත්තා (රාජ ආශ්‍රිතයා).
**චීවරචෙතාපන්නං** — රන් ආදී **අකැප** සිවුරු මිල. **පහිණෙය්‍ය** — යවයි.
**ඉමිනා චීවරචෙතාපන්නෙන…** — **පැමිණීමේ පිරිසිදු බව** දැක්වීමට කියන ලදී. "මෙය අසවල් භික්ෂුවට දෙන්න" යි එව්වේ නම්, පැමිණීම අපිරිසිදු බැවින් අකැප වස්තුවක් අරබයා කැප කාරකයෙකු වත් පෙන්විය නොහැකි වේ.
## (ආ) අකැප වස්තු හා භික්ෂුවගේ පිළිතුර
**න ඛො මයං, ආවුසො, චීවරචෙතාපන්නං පටිග්ගණ්හාම** — කැප ලෙස ගෙනා සිවුරු මිල වුව ද මෙබඳු දූත වචනයෙන් අකැප වන බැවින් ප්‍රතික්ෂේප කළ යුතුය.
|! වස්තු | ස්වභාවය
| රන්, රිදී, කහවණු, මාසක | **නිස්සග්ගිය වස්තු** (නිස්සග්ගිය 18)
| මුතු, මැණික්, වෛඩූර්‍ය, සක්, ගල්, පබළු, ලෝහිතංක, මසාරගල්ල, ධාන්‍ය සත, දාසි-දාස, කෙත්-වතු, මල් උයන්, පලතුරු උයන් ආදිය | **දුක්කට වස්තු**
මේවා තමා, චෛත්‍යය, සංඝයා, ගණයා හෝ පුද්ගලයෙකු සඳහා වත් පිළිගැනීම නොවටී.
**චීවරඤ්ච ඛො මයං පටිග්ගණ්හාම කාලෙන කප්පියං** — තමා උදෙසා ගෙනා බැවින් මෙසේ කීම වටී: අවශ්‍ය වූ කල්හි කැප සිවුරක් පිළිගනිමු.
**වෙය්‍යාවච්චකරො** — **කැප කාරකයා**. "ආයුෂ්මතුන්ට වෙය්‍යාවච්චකරයෙක් සිටී ද?" යි කැප වචනයෙන් ඇසූ බැවින් පෙන්වීම අනුදත්තේ ය. "කවුද මෙය ගන්නේ?", "කාටද දෙන්නේ?" යි ඇසුවොත් පෙන්විය යුතු නැත. සහධාර්මිකයන් පස් දෙනා හැර ඕනෑම අයෙක් කැප කාරකයා විය හැකිය. "**එසො ඛො, ආවුසො, භික්ඛූනං වෙය්‍යාවච්චකරො**" යි පමණක් කිව යුතුය; "මොහුට දෙන්න" ආදිය නොකිව යුතුය.
**සඤ්ඤත්තො සො මයා** — "ඔබට සිවුරක් අවශ්‍ය විට දෙනු ඇතැයි මා ඔහුට කීවෙමි" යි දූතයා දැන්වූ විට ම පමණක් ඔහුට ඉල්ලීම වටී.
## (ඇ) චෝදනා තුන හා සිටීම් සය
**අත්ථො මෙ, ආවුසො, චීවරෙන** — චෝදනාවේ ලක්ෂණයයි. ඕනෑම භාෂාවකින් මෙය හෝ මෙහි අර්ථය කිව හැකිය; "**මට දෙන්න, මට ගෙනෙන්න**" ආදී ලෙස නොකිව යුතුය.
**ඡක්ඛත්තුපරමං තුණ්හීභූතෙන උද්දිස්ස ඨාතබ්බං** — සිවුර උදෙසා **නිහඬව සිටිය යුතුය**: නොහිඳිය යුතුය, ආමිස නොපිළිගත යුතුය, ධර්මය නොකිය යුතුය. "කුමට පැමිණියේ ද?" යි ඇසුවොත් "**ආවුසො, දැනගන්න**" යි පමණක් කිව යුතුය.

## (ඈ) "සාමීචි" — නොලැබුණු විට

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
A high official who supported Ven. **Upananda** sent a robe fund by messenger, arranging that a lay follower should buy a robe with it and present it to Upananda. The lay follower said, “Come whenever you wish.” That day there was **a town council meeting**, and he asked, “Wait just one day.” But Upananda ==insisted, “The robe must be given today,” and held him back==. Arriving late, he was **fined fifty kahāpaṇas**. People complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Rājabhoggo** — one who enjoys an income from the king (a royal dependant).
**Cīvaracetāpannaṃ** — **an unallowable** robe fund, gold and the like. **Pahiṇeyya** — sends.
**Iminā cīvaracetāpannena…** — said to show **the purity of its coming**. Had he sent it saying, “Give this to such-and-such a monk,” its coming would be impure, and the monk could not even point out a steward with regard to an unallowable object.
## (B) Unallowable objects and the monk's reply
**Na kho mayaṃ, āvuso, cīvaracetāpannaṃ paṭiggaṇhāma** — even a robe fund brought in an allowable way becomes unallowable through such words of the messenger, so it must be refused.
|! Objects | Status
| Gold, silver, kahāpaṇas, māsakas | **Objects to be forfeited** (Nissaggiya 18)
| Pearls, gems, beryl, conch, stone, coral, ruby, cat's-eye, the seven grains, slaves, fields and land, flower gardens, orchards and the like | **Objects entailing dukkaṭa**
None of these may be accepted for oneself, a shrine, the Saṅgha, a group or an individual.
**Cīvarañca kho mayaṃ paṭiggaṇhāma kālena kappiyaṃ** — since it was brought for him, he may say this: when we need it, we accept an allowable robe.
**Veyyāvaccakaro** — **a steward** (kappiyakāraka). He is allowed to point one out because the messenger asked in allowable words, “Does the Venerable have a steward?” If asked “Who will take this?” or “To whom shall I give it?” he should not point one out. Anyone except the five co-religionists may serve as steward. He should say only, “**That, friend, is the monks' steward**” — not “Give it to him” and the like.
**Saññatto so mayā** — only when the messenger has reported, “I have instructed him to give you a robe when you need one,” may he press the steward.
## (C) Three requests and six standings
**Attho me, āvuso, cīvarena** — the mark of a request. This, or its meaning, may be said in any language; but not “**Give me, bring me**” and the like.
**Chakkhattuparamaṃ tuṇhībhūtena uddissa ṭhātabbaṃ** — he is to **stand in silence** for the robe: he should not sit, accept food, or teach Dhamma. If asked, “Why have you come?” he should say only, “**Know it, friend.**”
|! Method | At most
| Only asking | **Six requests**
| Only standing | **Twelve standings**
| Both | One request **counts as two standings** — 3 requests + 6 standings
Whether he goes repeatedly in one day or goes once and says it six times, or stands twelve times, all requests and standings are used up. Stewards to whom the donor himself went and pointed out may be pressed **even a hundred times**. A steward pointed out by neither party is **not to be told anything**.
## (D) “Sāmīci” — when it is not obtained
**Yatassa cīvaracetāpannaṃ ābhataṃ, tattha sāmaṃ vā gantabbaṃ dūto vā pāhetabbo** — he should go himself, or send a messenger, to the king or royal dependant who sent the fund and say: “**The robe fund you sent has been of no use to that monk; let the Venerable recover his property; may your property not be lost.**” **Ayaṃ tattha sāmīci** — this is the proper duty there; one who does not do it has **a dukkaṭa for breach of duty**.
## (E) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning Ven. Upananda, who would not wait when asked, “Wait just one day.” It is a **common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (F) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Every effort beyond the limit of requests and standings | DK
| Obtaining it thereby (**triple pācittiya**) | NP
| Not beyond, but perceiving it as beyond or in doubt | DK
| Not informing the donor when it is not obtained (breach of duty) | DK
Formula: “Venerable sirs, this robe of mine, obtained by asking more than three times and standing more than six times, is to be forfeited…”
## (G) No offence
# Obtaining it **without asking**.
# **The owners themselves** pressing the steward and giving it.
# For the insane and so on, and for the first offender.
## (H) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# **A steward pointed out** by the monk.
# **Entrusted** by the messenger.
# **Effort beyond the limit**.
# **Obtaining it** by that effort.
## (I) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Matchmaking (go-between) origin** (six origins — body; speech; body–speech; body–mind; speech–mind; body–speech–mind), **by doing**, **no release by perception, without intention, blameworthy by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of mind, three feelings**.
