## කථා වස්තුව
@ සැවැත් නුවර · ජේතවනාරාමය
**ෂඩ්වර්ගීය භික්ෂූහු** ==**අසම්මුඛව (නැති තැන)** සිටි භික්ෂූන්ට **තජ්ජනීය, නියස්ස, පබ්බාජනීය, පටිසාරණීය, උක්ඛේපනීය** කර්ම කළහ==. අල්පේච්ඡ භික්ෂූහු දොස් කීහ. භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ "**අසම්මුඛව කළ කර්ම අධර්ම කර්ම ය**" යි වදාරා, අධිකරණ **සම්මුඛව** විසඳිය යුතු බව වදාළහ (චූළවග්ග, සමථක්ඛන්ධකය).
## (අ) අධිකරණ සතර — කඞ්ඛාවිතරණී අනුව
**සත්ත** — සමථවල **ගණන** යි. **අධිකරණසමථා** — **අධිකරණ සංසිඳවන, සන්සුන් කරන** බැවින් එසේ කියනු ලැබේ.
|! අධිකරණය | අර්ථය
| **විවාදාධිකරණය** | "**ධර්මය ය**" හෝ "**අධර්මය ය**" ආදී **වස්තු දහඅටකින්** විවාද කරන භික්ෂූන්ගේ **විවාදය**
| **අනුවාදාධිකරණය** | **සීල විපත්තියෙන්**, හෝ **ආචාර, දිට්ඨි, ආජීව විපත්තියෙන්** චෝදනා කරන අයගේ **අනුවාදය — උපවාදය හා චෝදනාව**
| **ආපත්තාධිකරණය** | මාතිකාවේ ආ **පහ** හා විභංගයේ ආ **දෙක** යන **ආපත්ති ස්කන්ධ සත** ම
| **කිච්චාධිකරණය** | සංඝයාගේ **අපලෝකන ආදී කර්ම සතර** කිරීම
## (ආ) සම්මුඛතා සතර
සම්මුඛා විනය නම් අධිකරණය සංසිඳෙන විට පවතින මේ **සම්මුඛතා සතර** යි:
|! සම්මුඛතාව | අර්ථය — කඞ්ඛාවිතරණී අනුව
| **සංඝ සම්මුඛතා** | කර්මය කරන සංඝයා **සමගිව, සම්මුඛව** සිටීම
| **ධම්ම සම්මුඛතා** | සංසිඳවිය යුතු **වස්තුව සත්‍ය (භූත)** වීම
| **විනය සම්මුඛතා** | සංසිඳවිය යුතු **ආකාරයට ම** සංසිඳවීම
| **පුග්ගල සම්මුඛතා** | **විවාද කරන්නා හා විවාද කරනු ලබන්නා** — දෙපක්ෂය ම **සම්මුඛව** සිටීම
==**උබ්බාහිකාවකින්** සංසිඳවන විට **සංඝ සම්මුඛතාව** නැත==; **පුද්ගලයෙකු හෝ ගණයෙකු ඉදිරියේ** ඇවැත් දෙසන විට ද **සංඝ සම්මුඛතාව** නැත.
## (ඇ) සංසිඳවන අධිකරණ
==**අධිකරණ සතර ම** සම්මුඛා විනය සමඟ සංසිඳේ==; **කිච්චාධිකරණය සම්මුඛා විනයෙන් පමණක්** සංසිඳේ.
|! අධිකරණය | සම්මුඛා විනයෙන් සංසිඳෙන ආකාරය
| **විවාදාධිකරණය** | **උපන් විහාරයේ ම**, හෝ වෙනත් තැනකට **යන අතරමඟ**, හෝ **ගෙන ගොස් භාර දුන් තැන සංඝයා** විසින්, නොහැකි නම් **එහි ම උබ්බාහිකාවට සම්මත භික්ෂූන්** විසින් විනිශ්චය කිරීම
| **අනුවාදාධිකරණය** | **චෝදනා කරන්නාගේ හා චෝදනා ලබන්නාගේ** වචන අසා, **ඇවතක් නැත්නම් දෙදෙනාට ම සමා කරවා**, **ඇත්නම් "මෙහි මේ ඇවත ය"** යි විනිශ්චය කිරීම
| **ආපත්තාධිකරණය** | සම්මුඛා විනයෙන් **පමණක්** නොසංසිඳේ — **පටිඤ්ඤාතකරණය** හෝ **තිණවත්ථාරකය** සමඟ ය
| **කිච්චාධිකරණය** | **සම්මුඛා විනයෙන් ම**
## (ඈ) විනිශ්චය කරන තැන — පිළිවෙළ
# පළමුව **අධිකරණය උපන් ආවාසයේ ම** සංඝයා සංසිඳවීමට උත්සාහ කරයි.
# නොහැකි නම් **වඩා බොහෝ භික්ෂූන් සිටින ආවාසයකට** ගෙන යයි; **අතරමඟ දී** ද සංසිඳවිය හැකිය.
# එහි දී **අනන්ත කථා** ඇති වී **කිසිවකුගේ කීමේ අර්ථය නොතේරේ** නම්, **අංග දහයකින් යුත්** භික්ෂූන් **උබ්බාහිකාවට** සම්මත කොට ඔවුන් ලවා විනිශ්චය කරවයි.
**උබ්බාහිකාවට සම්මත කළ යුතු භික්ෂුවගේ අංග දහය (චූළවග්ගය):**
# **සිල්වත්** ය — පාතිමොක්ඛ සංවරයෙන් සංවෘත, ආචාර ගෝචර සම්පන්න, අණුමාත්‍ර වරදෙහි ද භය දක්නා.
# **බහුශ්‍රැත** ය — ඇසූ දේ දරන, රැස් කරන.
# **ප්‍රාතිමෝක්ෂ දෙක ම** විස්තර වශයෙන් **මනාව උගත්, මනාව විභජනය කළ, සූත්‍ර හා අනුව්‍යඤ්ජන වශයෙන් මනාව විනිශ්චය කළ**.
# **විනයයේ ස්ථිර, නොසැලෙන**.
# **දෙපක්ෂයට ම** හඟවන්නට, දක්වන්නට, බලන්නට, පහදවන්නට **සමර්ථ**.
# **අධිකරණය උපදවීමට හා සංසිඳවීමට දක්ෂ**.
# **අධිකරණය දන්නා**.
# **අධිකරණයේ හේතුව (සමුදය) දන්නා**.
# **අධිකරණයේ නිරෝධය දන්නා**.
# **අධිකරණ නිරෝධයට යන මඟ දන්නා**.
## (ඉ) ධාර්මික හා අධාර්මික සම්මුඛා විනය

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==carried out **censure, guidance, banishment, reconciliation and suspension** acts against monks **who were not present**==. Monks of few wishes complained. The Blessed One declared “**Acts done in absence are illegitimate**,” and taught that issues must be settled **face to face** (Cūḷavagga, Samatha-khandhaka).
## (A) The four kinds of issue — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
**Satta** — **the number** of the settlements. **Adhikaraṇasamathā** — so called because they **settle and calm issues**.
|! Issue | Meaning
| **dispute (vivāda)** | the **dispute** of monks arguing on **the eighteen grounds**, such as “**this is Dhamma**” or “**this is not Dhamma**”
| **accusation (anuvāda)** | the **accusation — reproach and charge** — of those accusing of **failure in morality**, or **in conduct, view or livelihood**
| **offence (āpatti)** | **all seven classes of offence** — **five** in the Mātikā and **two** in the Vibhaṅga
| **duty (kicca)** | carrying out **the four Saṅgha acts**, by announcement and the rest
## (B) The four kinds of presence
A face-to-face verdict is **these four kinds of presence** when an issue is settled:
|! Presence | Meaning — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
| **of the Saṅgha** | the Saṅgha doing the act is **present and united**
| **of the Dhamma** | the matter to be settled **is real (bhūta)**
| **of the Vinaya** | it is settled **just as it should be settled**
| **of the individuals** | **the one disputing and the one disputed with** — both parties **present**
==When it is settled **by a referral committee**, **presence of the Saṅgha** is lacking==; when an offence is confessed **before an individual or a group**, **presence of the Saṅgha** is also lacking.
## (C) Issues it settles
==**All four issues** are settled together with the face-to-face verdict==; **duties are settled by the face-to-face verdict alone**.
|! Issue | How it is settled face to face
| **dispute** | decided **in the monastery where it arose**, or **on the way** to another place, or **by the Saṅgha to which it was handed over**, or failing that, **by monks authorised there as a referral committee**
| **accusation** | hearing **the accuser and the accused**, **if there is no offence, having both forgive each other**, **if there is, deciding “this is the offence here”**
| **offence** | **not** by the face-to-face verdict **alone** — together with **acting on acknowledgement** or **covering over with grass**
| **duty** | **by the face-to-face verdict alone**
## (D) Where it is decided — in order
# First the Saṅgha **in the residence where the issue arose** tries to settle it.
# If it cannot, the issue is taken **to a residence with more monks**; it may also be settled **on the way**.
# If there **endless talk** arises and **the meaning of what anyone says cannot be understood**, monks **with ten qualities** are authorised as **a referral committee (ubbāhikā)** to decide it.
**The ten qualities of a monk for the referral committee (Cūḷavagga):**
# **Virtuous** — restrained by the Pātimokkha, of good conduct and resort, seeing danger in the slightest fault.
# **Learned** — retaining and storing what he has heard.
# Has **both Pātimokkhas** **well learnt in detail, well analysed, well decided by rule and detail**.
# **Firm in the Vinaya, not to be shaken**.
# **Able** to make **both parties** understand, see, look and be reconciled.
# **Skilled at raising and settling issues**.
# **Knows the issue**.
# **Knows its origin**.
# **Knows its cessation**.
# **Knows the way to its cessation**.
## (E) Legitimate and illegitimate face-to-face settlement
|! Who persuades whom | Result
| **one who speaks against the Dhamma** — an individual / several / a Saṅgha — persuades **one who speaks Dhamma** — an individual / several / a Saṅgha — “**This is Dhamma, this is Vinaya, this is the Teacher’s instruction; take this, approve this**,” and settles it (**the dark nine**) | settled **illegitimately** — **a counterfeit of the face-to-face verdict**
| **one who speaks Dhamma** — an individual / several / a Saṅgha — persuades **one who speaks against the Dhamma** thus and settles it (**the bright nine**) | settled **legitimately** — **by the face-to-face verdict**
## (F) Reopening
Knowingly reopening an issue legitimately settled, saying “**it must be done again**,” is **pācittiya 63 (reopening)**.

## The Story
@ Rājagaha · Veḷuvana, the squirrels' feeding place
Ven. **Dabba the Mallian** **became an arahant at seven years of age**. Wishing to serve the Saṅgha, he was **authorised by the Saṅgha as assigner of lodgings and allocator of meals**. For monks arriving at night he showed the lodgings **by the light of his blazing finger (attaining the fire element)**.
**The Mettiya-Bhūmajaka monks**, being **junior and of little merit**, received **the Saṅgha’s poorer lodgings and poorer meals**. On the day they expected **the householder Kalyāṇabhattika’s** fine meal, they again received a poor one, and ==in anger had **the nun Mettiyā** accuse Dabba — “**He has violated me**” — with **a groundless failure in morality**==.
The Blessed One convened the Saṅgha and asked, “**Dabba, do you remember having done what this nun says?**” He replied, “**Venerable sir, the Blessed One knows me**,” and, “**Venerable sir, I do not recall indulging in sexual intercourse even in a dream.**” The nun was **expelled**; the Mettiya-Bhūmajakas admitted they had incited her. The Blessed One then allowed **a verdict of mindfulness** to be given to Dabba, **who had reached fullness of mindfulness (sativepullappatta)**.
## (A) Explanation — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
==When **one whose taints are destroyed (a pure monk such as an arahant)** is accused **with a groundless failure in morality**==, and he **asks for a verdict of mindfulness**, the Saṅgha gives it **by a motion-and-three-announcements act**.
==Once the verdict of mindfulness is given, **no one’s accusation against that person takes hold**==.

## The Story
@ Rājagaha · Veḷuvana, the squirrels' feeding place
A monk named **Gagga** ==**went insane, his mind deranged**, and did and said much **unbecoming to an ascetic**==. After he recovered, monks accused him: “**Venerable, do you remember committing such an offence?**” Though he said, “**Friends, I was insane; I did that in my insanity; I don’t remember it**,” they kept accusing him. The Blessed One allowed **a verdict of past insanity** to be given to Gagga, **now no longer deluded (amūḷha)**.
## (A) Explanation — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
==**An insane monk** is accused, “**Do you remember?**”, about **unbecoming conduct done during his insanity**==. Though he says, “**Friends, I did that when insane; I don’t remember it**,” if **the accusations continue**, he asks for a verdict of past insanity **so that he will not be accused again**. The Saṅgha gives it **by a motion-and-three-announcements act**.
==Once it is given, **no one’s accusation against him on that account takes hold**==.
## (B) Issues it settles
|! Issue | How
| **accusation** | settled **by the face-to-face verdict and the verdict of past insanity**
## (C) How it is given
# The monk goes to the Saṅgha, **arranges his upper robe over one shoulder**, **pays respect at the feet of the senior monks**, **squats**, **raises his joined palms** and asks **three times**: “**Venerable sirs, I was insane; in my insanity I did much that was unbecoming; the monks accuse me; being no longer deluded, I ask the Saṅgha for a verdict of past insanity.**”
# **A competent, capable** monk gives it **by a motion-and-three-announcements act**.
## (D) Legitimate and illegitimate grants — Cūḷavagga
|! The monk’s state | What he says | Ruling
| has committed an offence, **remembering it** | “**Friends, I don’t remember such an offence**” | **illegitimate**
| has committed an offence, **remembering it** | “**I remember it as in a dream**” | **illegitimate**
| has committed an offence, **sane but acting insane** | “**I do this, you do this; it’s allowable for me, allowable for you**” | **illegitimate**
| **really was insane**, **does not remember** what he did | “**Friends, I don’t remember it**” | **legitimate**
| **really was insane** | “**I remember it as in a dream**” | **legitimate**
| **really was insane** | “**I do this … allowable for you**” (because of the insanity) | **legitimate**

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
Monks **in the midst of the Saṅgha had fallen into strife, quarrels and disputes**, ==**stabbing one another with the weapons of their tongues**==, and could not settle the issue. The Blessed One said, “**Monks, I allow such an issue to be settled by majority decision**,” and allowed **a distributor of voting tickets** to be authorised.
## (A) Explanation — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
If a dispute is not settled **by the face-to-face verdict alone**, **the monks authorised as a referral committee** hand it back **to the Saṅgha**, saying, “**We cannot settle it.**” The Saṅgha then authorises a monk **with five qualities** as **ticket distributor**, has him **distribute tickets**, and settles it ==according to **the majority of those who speak Dhamma** in the assembly, **as they say**==.
==**Carrying out the majority-decision act** is called “majority decision”==.

| **dispute** | settled **by the face-to-face verdict and majority decision**
## (C) Five qualities of the ticket distributor
# Does not go astray **through desire**.
# Does not go astray **through hatred**.
# Does not go astray **through delusion**.
# Does not go astray **through fear**.
# Knows **which tickets have been taken and which not**.
He is authorised **by a motion-and-one-announcement act**.

|! Way | How it is done
| **secret (gūḷhaka)** | separating the two kinds of ticket, he tells each monk, “**This is for those who say this, this for those who say that; take whichever you like**,” adding “**Don’t show anyone**.” If he finds **those against the Dhamma are the majority**, he **takes them back** as “**badly taken**”; if **those of the Dhamma are the majority**, he **announces it** as “**well taken**”
| **whispering in the ear (sakaṇṇajappaka)** | he says **into each monk’s ear**, “**This is the ticket for those who say this … take whichever you like; don’t tell anyone**.” Then as with the secret way — **if those against the Dhamma are the majority he takes them back**, **if those of the Dhamma are, he announces it**
| **open (vivaṭaka)** | if he knows **those of the Dhamma are the majority**, he has the tickets taken **openly**

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
A monk named **Upavāḷa**, ==**being questioned about an offence** in the midst of the Saṅgha, **denied and then admitted, admitted and then denied**, **evaded one question with another**, and **told deliberate lies**==. Monks of few wishes complained. The Blessed One allowed **a further-punishment act** to be carried out against Upavāḷa.
## (A) Explanation — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
==For **a wicked person, wicked from the abundance of his wrongdoing**, who is accused **of a pārājika or something close to a pārājika** and **evades one question with another**==, the Saṅgha — thinking, “**If his root is not cut, he will behave properly and gain reinstatement; if it is cut, he will destroy himself**” — carries out the further-punishment act **by a motion-and-three-announcements act**.
## (B) Issues it settles
|! Issue | How
| **accusation** | settled **by the face-to-face verdict and further punishment**
## (C) Five factors for doing it legitimately
# He is **impure**.
# He is **shameless**.
# He is **under accusation**.
# The Saṅgha does it **legitimately**.
# The Saṅgha does it **in unity**.
## (D) Legitimate and illegitimate acts
|! Illegitimate (with three factors) | Legitimate (with three factors)
| done **in his absence** | done **in his presence**
| done **without questioning** | done **after questioning**
| done **without acknowledgement** | done **with acknowledgement**
| done to one **without an offence** | done to one **with an offence**
| for an offence **not to be confessed** | for an offence **to be confessed**
| for an offence **already confessed** | for an offence **not yet confessed**
| done **without accusing** | done **after accusing**
| done **without reminding** | done **after reminding**
| done **without charging the offence** | done **after charging the offence**
| done **illegitimately** | done **legitimately**
| done **as a faction** | done **in unity**
## (E) Whom the Saṅgha may act against if it wishes — three factors
# One who **makes strife, quarrels, disputes and contention**, raising **issues** in the Saṅgha.
# One who is **foolish, incompetent, full of offences, without limit**.
# One who lives **in unsuitable association with householders**.
## (F) The eighteen duties of one subjected to the act
# He should not **give full ordination**.
# He should not **give dependence**.
# He should not **have a novice attend on him**.
# He should not **accept authorisation to admonish nuns**.
# Even if authorised, he should not **admonish nuns**.
# He should not commit **the offence for which the act was done**.
# He should not commit **a similar offence**.
# He should not commit **a worse one**.
# He should not **criticise the act**.
# He should not **criticise those who did the act**.
# He should not **cancel a regular monk’s Uposatha**.
# He should not **cancel his Pavāraṇā**.
# He should not **give orders**.
# He should not **take the lead in accusing**.
# He should not **ask leave (to accuse)**.
# He should not **accuse**.
# He should not **remind (of an offence)**.
# He should not **make monks quarrel**.
## (G) The purpose of the act
==If **his root is not cut**, he behaves properly and gains **reinstatement**; if **his root is cut (a pārājika)**, **he destroys himself**== (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī).

**ආරවුල්, කලහ, විවාදවලට පැමිණ** වාසය කළ භික්ෂූන් විසින් ==**ශ්‍රමණයෙකුට නොගැළපෙන බොහෝ දේ කියන ලදී, කරන ලදී**==. පසුව ඔවුන්ට මෙසේ සිතිණ: "**අපි මේ ඇවැත්වලින් එකිනෙකා ලවා කරවන්නෙමු නම්, ඒ අධිකරණය දරුණු බවට, කර්කශ බවට, භේදයට හේතු විය හැකිය; අප කුමක් කළ යුතු ද?**" භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ **තිණවත්ථාරකයෙන්** එබඳු අධිකරණය සංසිඳවීමට අනුදැන වදාළහ.
## (අ) විස්තරය — කඞ්ඛාවිතරණී අනුව
==**දෙපක්ෂයට බෙදී ආරවුල් කළ** භික්ෂූහු **බොහෝ ශ්‍රමණයෙකුට නොගැළපෙන හැසිරීම්** කොට, පසුව **ලැජ්ජා ධර්මය උපන්** කල්හි==, එකිනෙකා ලවා ඇවැත් කරවීමේ **දෝෂය** දැක තිණවත්ථාරක කර්මය කරති.
**"තිණවත්ථාරක"** — ==**අසූචියක් තණකොළයෙන් වසා දමන්නාක් මෙන්**== ඇවැත් **එකවර වසා** සංසිඳවීම.
## (ආ) සංසිඳවන අධිකරණ
|! අධිකරණය | ආකාරය
| **ආපත්තාධිකරණය** | **සම්මුඛා විනයෙන් හා තිණවත්ථාරකයෙන්** සංසිඳේ
## (ඇ) කරන ආකාරය — චූළවග්ගය
# **සියල්ලන් ම එක් තැනක රැස්** විය යුතුය.
# **ව්‍යක්ත, ප්‍රතිබල** භික්ෂුවක් **සංඝයාට** ඤත්තියක් තබයි: "**අප ආරවුල් කරද්දී බොහෝ නොගැළපෙන දේ කළෙමු... සංඝයාට සුදුසු නම්, සංඝයා මේ අධිකරණය තිණවත්ථාරකයෙන් සංසිඳවන්නේය — ථුල්ලවජ්ජ හා ගිහි සම්බන්ධ ඇවැත් හැර.**"
# **එක් එක් පක්ෂයේ** ව්‍යක්ත භික්ෂුවක් **තම පක්ෂයට** දන්වයි: "**ආයුෂ්මතුනි, ඔබට සුදුසු නම්, ඔබගේ ද මාගේ ද ඇවැත්, ඔබගේ ද මාගේ ද යහපත පිණිස, සංඝයා මැද තිණවත්ථාරකයෙන් දෙසන්නෙමි — ථුල්ලවජ්ජ හා ගිහි සම්බන්ධ ඇවැත් හැර.**"
# ඉන්පසු ඔහු **සංඝයාට ඤත්තිදුතිය කර්මයෙන්** දන්වා දෙසයි; "**අපගේ මේ ඇවැත් සංඝයා මැද තිණවත්ථාරකයෙන් දෙසන ලදී**" යි ධාරණය කරයි.
# **අනෙක් පක්ෂය ද** එසේ ම කරයි.

| **ථුල්ලවජ්ජ** — **පාරාජික, සංඝාදිසේස** වැනි **බරපතල** ඇවැත් | තිණවත්ථාරකයෙන් නොවසේ
| **ගිහි සම්බන්ධ** — ගිහියන්ට නින්දා කිරීම ආදී ඇවැත් | තිණවත්ථාරකයෙන් නොවසේ
| **දිට්ඨාවිකම්ම** කළ අය — "**මෙය මට නොරුචිය**" යි **විරුද්ධත්වය පළ කළ** අය | ඔවුන්ගේ ඇවැත් නොමිදේ

==**හත්ථපාසයට පැමිණ** "**මෙය මට නොරුචිය**" යි විරුද්ධත්වය පළ නොකළ සියල්ලන්ගේ — **නින්දට ගිය අයගේ පවා** — **ථුල්ලවජ්ජ හා ගිහි සම්බන්ධ ඇවැත් හැර සියලු ඇවැත්** නැගිටෙයි== (කඞ්ඛාවිතරණී).

## (ඊ) ප්‍රාතිමෝක්ෂ නිගමනය
**"කච්චිත්ථ පරිසුද්ධා"** — "**මේ සමථ සතෙහි ඔබ පිරිසිදු ද? සමථවලින් සංසිඳවිය යුතු කිසිවක් ඔබට නැද්ද?**" යි ඇසීමයි; මෙයින් **සියලු ඇවැත්වලින් පිරිසිදු බව** අසනු ලැබේ.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
While monks **lived in strife, quarrels and disputes**, ==**much unbecoming to ascetics was said and done**==. Later they thought: “**If we make one another answer for these offences, the issue may lead to harshness, violence and schism; what should we do?**” The Blessed One allowed such an issue to be settled **by covering over with grass**.
## (A) Explanation — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
==Monks **split into two factions and quarrelling** did **much unbecoming conduct**; later, **when a sense of shame arose**==, seeing **the danger** of making one another answer for the offences, they carry out the covering-over-with-grass act.
**“Tiṇavatthāraka”** — settling by ==**covering the offences all at once, as one covers filth with grass**==.
## (B) Issues it settles
|! Issue | How
| **offence** | settled **by the face-to-face verdict and covering over with grass**
## (C) How it is done — Cūḷavagga
# **All must assemble in one place**.
# **A competent, capable** monk sets a motion **before the Saṅgha**: “**While quarrelling we did much that was unbecoming… if it is fitting for the Saṅgha, let it settle this issue by covering over with grass — except for grave faults and those connected with householders.**”
# A competent monk **of each faction** informs **his own faction**: “**Venerables, if it suits you, I will confess in the midst of the Saṅgha, by covering over with grass, your offences and mine, for your good and mine — except grave faults and those connected with householders.**”
# He then informs the Saṅgha **by a motion-and-one-announcement act** and confesses; it is recorded: “**These offences of ours have been confessed in the midst of the Saṅgha by covering over with grass.**”
# **The other faction** does the same.
## (D) What is not released
|! Offences / persons not released | Why
| **grave faults (thullavajja)** — **heavy** offences such as **pārājika and saṅghādisesa** | not covered by this act
| **connected with householders** — offences such as reviling laypeople | not covered by this act
| those who **voiced dissent** — “**This does not please me**” | their offences are not released
| those **not present** | their offences are not released
==For all **within arm’s reach** who did not voice dissent — **even those who have fallen asleep** — **all offences except grave faults and those connected with householders** are cleared== (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī).
## (E) Issues and settlements — full summary
|! Issue | Settled by
| **dispute** | **two** — face-to-face verdict, majority decision
| **accusation** | **four** — face-to-face verdict, verdict of mindfulness, verdict of past insanity, further punishment
| **offence** | **three** — face-to-face verdict, acting on acknowledgement, covering over with grass
| **duty** | **one** — the face-to-face verdict alone
## (F) The conclusion of the Pātimokkha
**“Kaccittha parisuddhā”** — asks, “**Are you pure in these seven settlements? Is there nothing of yours to be settled by them?**”; by this **purity from all offences** is asked.
**“Ettakaṃ … sikkhitabbaṃ”** — “**This much of the training — handed down in the Blessed One’s text (the Pātimokkha), included in it, coming up for recitation every half-month — is to be trained in, united in body, rejoicing in mind, without dispute**” — **not transgressing each rule, the training in higher virtue is to be fulfilled** (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī).
