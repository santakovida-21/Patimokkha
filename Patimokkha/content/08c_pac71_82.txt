## The Story
@ Kosambī · Ghositārāma
When Ven. **Channa** behaved improperly, the monks said, “**Friend, don't do that; it is not allowable.**” He replied, ==“**I won't train in this rule until I've asked another monk, learned and expert in Vinaya.**”== The monks criticised him, and the Blessed One, declaring, “**A monk in training should understand, ask and investigate,**” laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Sahadhammikaṃ vuccamāno** — being spoken to with **a rule laid down by the Blessed One** in which **the five co-religionists** must train (in the sense given in **the rule on being difficult to admonish** — saṅghādisesa 12).
**Etasmiṃ sikkhāpade** — “**I won't yet train** in what is said in this rule.”
**Pācittiyaṃ** — ==for one who says so **evasively**, **out of fear of the offence of disrespect (Pāc. 54)**, a pācittiya **for each utterance**==.
**Sikkhamānena** — as one who **accepts the admonition respectfully** and wishes to train.
**Aññātabbaṃ** — it should be understood. **Paripucchitabbaṃ** — one should ask, “**What does this mean?**” **Paripañhitabbaṃ** — one should **think it over and weigh it**.
## (B) Compared with the disrespect rule
|! Rule | Manner
| **Pācittiya 54 — disrespect** | showing **disrespect directly** when admonished
| **Pācittiya 71 — co-religionist** | out of fear of that offence, saying **evasively**: “**I won't train until I've asked someone else**”
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Kosambī** concerning **Channa**, on the matter of speaking thus. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Speaking thus when an ordained person admonishes with a rule laid down — perceiving him as ordained, in doubt, or as unordained (**triple pācittiya**) — each utterance | PC
| When **an unordained person** admonishes — in all three perceptions | DK
| Speaking thus when either admonishes with **what was not laid down** (“This does not lead to effacement” etc.) | DK
## (E) No offence
# Saying, “**I will find out, I will train**.”
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= dve — two)
# Words of **an ordained person, based on a rule laid down**.
# Speaking thus **not wishing to train**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Three origins, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

**උද්දිස්සමානෙ** — **ආචාර්‍යයා අන්තේවාසිකයාට** කියන විට, හෝ **සජ්ඣායනය** වශයෙන් නැවත නැවත කියන විට.
**ඛුද්දානුඛුද්දකෙහි** — **සුළු හා අනු-සුළු** ශික්ෂාපදවලින්.
**යාවදෙව** — ඒවායේ ප්‍රතිඵලයේ **සීමාව** දක්වන වචනයකි: "**මේවා උද්දේශ කරන, කරවන, සජ්ඣායනා කරන අයට, 'වටී ද, නොවටී ද' යන කුකුස (විප්පටිසාරය), වෙහෙස, සැකය, මනෝ විලේඛනය ඇති වනතුරු ම පවතී**." නැතහොත් **අතිශය** අර්ථයෙන්: "**කුකුසටත් වෙහෙසටත් විලේඛනයටත් අතිශයින් ම පවතී**."
**සික්ඛාපදවිවණ්ණකෙ** — මෙසේ **ශික්ෂාපදවලට ගැරහීමෙහි** පාචිත්තියයි.

**සැවැත් නුවරදී** ෂඩ්වර්ගීය භික්ෂූන් අරබයා **විනයයට ගැරහීමේ** වස්තුවෙහි පැනවිණි. **සාධාරණ ප්‍රඥප්තියකි**. **අනාණත්තිකයි**. **තික පාචිත්තියකි**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
The Blessed One gave talks on **the Vinaya** in many ways, praising **the Vinaya and the one expert in it**, and praising Ven. **Upāli**. Many monks learnt the Vinaya from Upāli. **The group-of-six**, afraid — “**If they become expert in the Vinaya they'll push us around as they please**” — disparaged it: ==“**What's the use of reciting these lesser and minor rules? They only lead to anxiety, vexation and perplexity.**”== The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Uddissamāne** — while **a teacher recites it to his pupil**, or while it is **being repeated in recitation**.
**Khuddānukhuddakehi** — by the **lesser and minor** rules.
**Yāvadeva** — marks the **limit** of their effect: “**For those who recite them, have them recited or repeat them, they lead only so far as the anxiety and remorse, vexation, doubt and mental perplexity of ‘Is it allowable or not?’**” Or, as intensive: “**They lead exceedingly to anxiety, vexation and perplexity.**”
**Sikkhāpadavivaṇṇake** — **in disparaging the training rules** thus, there is pācittiya.
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of **disparaging the Vinaya**. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (C) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Disparaging the training rules before an ordained person — perceiving him as ordained, in doubt, or as unordained (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| Disparaging before **an unordained person** — in all three perceptions | DK
| Disparaging **Dhamma other than the Vinaya** (suttas, Abhidhamma) before either | DK
## (D) No offence
# **Without wishing to disparage**, saying, “**First learn the suttas, verses or Abhidhamma; later you'll learn the Vinaya**.”
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= dve — two)
# **Wishing to disparage**.
# **Disparaging the training rules before an ordained person**.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Three origins, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

**උද්දිස්සමානෙ** — **උපෝසථය වශයෙන්** උද්දේශ කරන විට.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six**, having behaved improperly and wishing it to be thought “**they offended out of ignorance**,” said while the Pātimokkha was being recited: ==“**Only now do we learn that this rule too is handed down in the text, included in the text, and comes up for recitation every half-month.**”== The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Anvaḍḍhamāsaṃ** — **every half-month in turn**.
**Uddissamāne** — while it is being recited **at the Uposatha**.
**Yañca tattha āpattiṃ āpanno** — whatever offence he committed by that misconduct, about which he speaks this way wishing it to be known that he offended **out of ignorance**.
**Tañca yathādhammo kāretabbo** — ==**there is no release because he offended out of ignorance**==; he must be dealt with according to the Dhamma and Vinaya — made to **confess** an offence cleared by confession, made to **emerge** from one cleared by rehabilitation.
**Uttari cassa moho āropetabbo** — further, after rebuking him with words like “**Friend, it is a loss for you, it is ill-gotten for you…**”, **the charge of feigned ignorance is to be imposed** on him **by a motion-and-one-announcement act**.
**Idaṃ tasmiṃ mohanake pācittiyaṃ** — this pācittiya falls on the one who feigns ignorance **after the charge has been imposed** — not if it has not been imposed.
## (B) “Having heard it in full”
|! Situation | Ruling
| one who has heard the Pātimokkha **in full two or three times** feigns ignorance after the charge is imposed | PC
| feigns ignorance **before the charge is imposed** | DK
| one who has **not heard it in full**, or heard it in full **fewer than two or three times** | no offence
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of feigning ignorance. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Feigning ignorance after the charge was imposed by a legitimate act — perceiving it as legitimate, in doubt, or as illegitimate (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| **An illegitimate act** — in all three perceptions | DK
| Feigning ignorance **before the charge is imposed** | DK
## (E) No offence
# For one who has **not heard it in full**.
# For one who has heard it in full **fewer than two or three times**.
# For one **not wishing to deceive**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# **The charge of feigned ignorance imposed**.
# **Wishing to deceive**.
# **Having heard** the Pātimokkha **in full two or three times**.
# **Feigning ignorance**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Three origins, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six**, ==angry and displeased, **struck** monks of **the group-of-seventeen**==, who **wept**. Asked by the monks, “**Why are you weeping?**”, they told what had happened. The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Kupito anattamano** — displeased, with a mind struck by anger.
**Pahāraṃ dadeyya** — strikes **with the body, something attached to it, or a thrown object** — **even with a lotus petal**.
==If he dies from a blow given **with the intention of striking**, it is **still pācittiya**== (there being no intention to kill).
## (B) Striking and disfiguring
|! Act | Offence
| striking **an ordained person** in anger | PC
| striking **an unordained person** (layman, woman, even an animal) — in all three perceptions | DK
| cutting off **ears, nose** etc., even of an ordained person, **intending to disfigure** | DK
| striking a woman **out of lust** | SD (bodily contact)
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of striking. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Striking an ordained person in anger — perceiving him as ordained, in doubt, or as unordained (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| An unordained person — triple dukkaṭa; cutting off ears etc. to disfigure | DK

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six**, angry, ==**raised their palms** threateningly at monks of **the group-of-seventeen**==, who, used to being struck, **wept in fear**. The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Talasattikaṃ uggireyya** — **showing the gesture of striking**, he **raises** the body (hand, palm etc.) or **something attached to it** (a stick, a bowl etc.).
==Here the pācittiya is **for the raising itself**==.
## (B) If a blow lands after raising
|! Situation | Offence
| **raising** the hand / stick | PC
| having raised it, **a blow lands by mistake** — not given with intent to strike | DK
| even if that blow **breaks a hand** etc. | DK

## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of raising the hand. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==charged monks of **the group-of-seventeen** with **groundless saṅghādisesa** offences==. They wept, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Amūlakena** — **without the grounds** of having **seen, heard or suspected**.
**Anuddhaṃseyya** — **charges** him or **has him charged**.
**Pācittiyaṃ** — if the one charged ==**knows at that very moment, “He is charging me,”**== the accuser incurs a pācittiya.
## (B) Groundless charges compared
|! Charge | Rule | Offence
| a groundless **pārājika** — out of hatred | saṅghādisesa 8 | SD
| a groundless **saṅghādisesa** | **pācittiya 76** | PC
| **failure in conduct** or **failure in view** | — | DK
| against **an unordained person** | — | DK
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of charging with a groundless saṅghādisesa. It is a **common rule**, **sāṇattika** — also by having him charged — and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Charging or having an ordained person charged with a groundless saṅghādisesa — perceiving him as ordained, in doubt, or as unordained (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| Charging with failure in conduct or view; an unordained person (triple dukkaṭa) | DK
## (E) No offence
# Charging him **believing it to be so**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# **An ordained person**.
# The saṅghādisesa **being groundless**.
# **Charging** him.
# **His knowing at that very moment**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Three origins, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==deliberately aroused anxiety in monks of **the group-of-seventeen**==: “**Friends, a rule has been laid down that one under twenty should not be ordained; you were ordained under twenty — aren't you unordained?**” They wept, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Sañcicca** — knowingly, consciously, intentionally.
**Kukkuccaṃ upadaheyya** — **arouses anxiety** by saying, “**I think you were under twenty**,” “**I think you ate at the wrong time**,” “**I think you drank alcohol**,” “**I think you sat in private with a woman**” and the like.
==For one who deliberately arouses anxiety **with no other reason**, a pācittiya **for each utterance**==.
**Itipi'ssa muhuttampi aphāsu bhavissati** — with the thought, “**Let him be uneasy, even for a moment**.”
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of arousing anxiety. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (C) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Deliberately arousing anxiety in an ordained person — perceiving him as ordained, in doubt, or as unordained (**triple pācittiya**) — each utterance | PC
| In **an unordained person** — in all three perceptions | DK
## (D) No offence
# **Without wishing to arouse anxiety**, speaking **only out of concern for his welfare** — “**You seem to be under twenty; find out**.”
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **An ordained person**.
# **Wishing to make him uneasy**.
# **Arousing anxiety**.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Three origins, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six**, **quarrelling** with well-behaved monks, ==**stood eavesdropping** while those monks were talking==, thinking, “**We'll hear what they say and then accuse them, remind them, shame them.**” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Vivādāpannānaṃ** — of monks who have entered into **a dispute that has grown out of strife and quarrels**.
**Upassutiṃ** — **near enough to hear** — he **stands where their words can be heard**.
## (B) How the offences fall
|! Stage | Offence
| **each step** in going there **intending to accuse**, thinking “**I'll listen from there**” | DK
| likewise **hurrying ahead** or **lagging behind** | DK
| **standing and hearing** | PC
| when **they come** to where he stands and talk, **hearing** without letting them know **by coughing or saying “I'm here”** | PC
==If they come to where he is, he must **let them know by coughing or saying “I'm here”**== — otherwise hearing is still pācittiya (hence this is **sometimes by not doing**).
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of eavesdropping. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Each step in going | DK
| Intending to accuse, hearing ordained persons' talk — perceiving them as ordained, in doubt, or as unordained (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| Hearing **unordained persons'** talk — in all three perceptions | DK
## (E) No offence
# Going thinking, “**Having heard them, I'll desist, abstain, calm things down, clear myself**.”
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **An ordained person**.
# **Intending to accuse**.
# **Hearing**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Thieves'-caravan origin** (body–mind; body–speech–mind); this is **sometimes by doing, sometimes by not doing** (not letting them know); **release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six**, behaving improperly, would ==**block**== any act being done against one of them. One day, while they were **busy with robe-making**, the Saṅgha met for some business, and they sent **their consent by one monk**. The Saṅgha said, “**This is one of the group-of-six, and he has come alone; let's do the act against him,**” and did so. When he returned and they heard of it, the group-of-six ==**complained: “The Saṅgha did an illegitimate act.”**== The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Dhammikānaṃ kammānaṃ** — the four Saṅgha acts done **by the Dhamma, the Vinaya and the Teacher's instruction**:
|! Act | How it is done | Applications
| **by announcement (apalokana)** | with the consent of a united Saṅgha, stating the matter and **asking three times** “**Ruccati saṅghassa?**” | **5** — reinstatement, expulsion, shaving permission, the higher penalty, act-defined
| **by motion (ñatti)** | **a single motion** | **9** — reinstatement, expulsion, Uposatha, Pavāraṇā, authorisation, giving, receiving, postponement, act-defined
| **motion and one announcement** | **a motion and one proclamation** | **7** — reinstatement, expulsion, authorisation, giving, lifting, designation, act-defined
| **motion and three announcements** | **a motion and three proclamations** | **7** — reinstatement, expulsion, authorisation, giving, rebuke, formal admonition, act-defined
**Khīyanadhammaṃ āpajjeyya** — having **given consent** to such legitimate acts, he afterwards **complains**: “**It was an illegitimate act, a badly done act**” — ==a pācittiya **for each utterance**==.
## (B) When an act is legitimate
Every act must be done **by its own form** — an announcement act only by announcement, a motion act only by a single motion. Of the motion-and-one-announcement acts, **the six weighty ones** — **authorising a boundary, abolishing a boundary, giving the kaṭhina cloth, ending the kaṭhina, designating a hut site, designating a dwelling site** — may not be done by announcement; the proclamation must be recited. **The lighter authorisations** may also be done by announcement.
Acts done thus, **complete in matter, motion, proclamation, boundary and assembly**, are **legitimate**.
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of complaining. It is a **common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Having given consent to a legitimate act, knowing it legitimate, complaining afterwards — each utterance | PC
| **In doubt** about a legitimate act | DK
| An illegitimate act perceived as legitimate, or in doubt | DK
## (E) No offence
# Perceiving it **as an illegitimate act**.
# Complaining **knowing** that “they are acting **unlawfully**, **as an incomplete assembly**, or **against one not deserving it**.”
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **A legitimate act**.
# **Perceiving it as legitimate**.
# **Complaining after giving consent**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Three origins, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
While the Saṅgha had assembled for some business and **was deliberating**, **a certain monk**, saying, “**Against whom are you doing this act?**”, ==**got up and left without giving his consent**==. Because of him **the act could not be done**. The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Vinicchayakathāya** — “the deliberation is going on” as long as **the matter raised has not been decided**, or **the motion has been set but the proclamation not finished**.
**Chandaṃ adatvā** — without giving consent.
**Uṭṭhāyāsanā pakkameyya** — if a monk in that interval, **wishing to invalidate the act**, leaves **arm's reach of the assembly** —
|! Stage | Offence
| **leaving** arm's reach (first foot) | DK
| **having left** arm's reach | PC
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning a certain monk, on the matter of leaving without giving consent. It is a **common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (C) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| During the deliberation on a legitimate act, leaving arm's reach wishing to invalidate it | PC
| In the act of leaving arm's reach (first foot) | DK
| **In doubt** about a legitimate act; an illegitimate act perceived as legitimate or in doubt | DK
## (D) No offence
# Perceiving it **as an illegitimate act**.
# Knowing “**there will be strife, quarrels, disputes in the Saṅgha**.”
# Knowing “**they will act unlawfully, as an incomplete assembly, or against one not deserving it**.”
# **Being ill**; **on a sick person's business**.
# **Pressed by the call of nature**.
# Going **without wishing to invalidate the act**, intending “**I'll come back**.”
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= cha — six)
# **The deliberation going on**.
# **A legitimate act**.
# **Perceiving it as legitimate**.
# Standing **within the same boundary**.
# Being **in the same communion**.
# **Leaving arm's reach wishing to invalidate it**.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Samanubhāsana origin** (body–speech–mind), **by doing and not doing** (leaving; not giving consent), **release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

## කථා වස්තුව
@ රජගහ නුවර · වේළුවනය කලන්දක නිවාපය
ආයුෂ්මත් **දබ්බ මල්ලපුත්ත** තෙරුන් සංඝයාගේ **සෙනසුන් පනවන්නා හා බත් උද්දේශකයා** ලෙස සේවය කළහ; උන්වහන්සේගේ සිවුර **දුර්වල** විය. එකල සංඝයාට **සිවුරක්** ලැබිණ. සංඝයා එය **දබ්බ තෙරුන්ට දුන්නේය**. **ෂඩ්වර්ගීය භික්ෂූහු** ද සමග්ගව සිවුර දී, පසුව ==**"භික්ෂූහු සංඝ ලාභය මිතුරුකම් අනුව බෙදති"**== යි විරෝධය දැක්වූහ. භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ ශික්ෂාපදය පැනවූහ.
## (අ) පද විවරණය
**සමග්ගෙන සඞ්ඝෙන** — **සමාන සංවාසක**, **සමාන සීමාවේ** සිටින සංඝයා සමඟ **සිවුර දී**.
**යථාසන්ථුතං** — **මිත්‍ර, සන්දිට්ඨ, සම්භත්ත** වශයෙන් යමෙක් යමෙකු හා හුරු ද — ඔහුට.

## The Story
@ Rājagaha · Veḷuvana, the squirrels' feeding place
Ven. **Dabba the Mallian** served the Saṅgha as **assigner of lodgings and allocator of meals**, and his robe was **worn out**. A **robe** came to the Saṅgha, and the Saṅgha **gave it to Ven. Dabba**. **The group-of-six**, having joined in giving it, afterwards ==complained: “**The monks share out the Saṅgha's gains according to friendship.**”== The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Samaggena saṅghena** — having **given the robe** together with a Saṅgha **in the same communion** and **within the same boundary**.
**Yathāsanthutaṃ** — to whoever one is familiar with as **friend, acquaintance or companion**.
**Pācittiyaṃ** — for one who, together with the Saṅgha, **himself** gives a robe to a monk **authorised as assigner of lodgings etc.**, and afterwards complains, ==a pācittiya **for each utterance**==.
## (B) The five kinds of heavy goods
Giving **some requisite other than a robe** that is **distributable and divisible** and afterwards complaining is **dukkaṭa**. “Distributable and divisible” means everything **except the five heavy goods**:
|! Group | Heavy goods
| 1 | **a monastery** and **monastery land**
| 2 | **a dwelling** and **dwelling site**
| 3 | **bed, chair, mattress, pillow**
| 4 | **metal pot, metal jar, metal jug, metal cauldron, adze, axe, hatchet, hoe, digging tool**
| 5 | **creepers, bamboo, muñja and pabbaja grass, thatch, clay, wooden goods, clay goods**
==These five **may not be given away or divided by the Saṅgha, a group or an individual**; even if given away or divided, they **remain Saṅgha property**.== Fixed may be exchanged for fixed, and others exchanged in an allowable way with regard to the Saṅgha's benefit. One who gives them away by authority incurs **a thullaccaya**; one who takes them with intent to steal is judged by their value.
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Rājagaha** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of complaining after giving a robe. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and for a legitimate act a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Having given a robe with the Saṅgha to a monk legitimately authorised, complaining afterwards — each utterance (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| Complaining after giving a distributable requisite other than a robe | DK
| Complaining after giving a robe or other requisite to one **not authorised** by the Saṅgha | DK
| **An unordained person** — in every case | DK
## (E) No offence
# Seeing one who **habitually acts from desire, hatred, delusion or fear**, complaining: “**What's the use of giving to him? He'll waste it and not use it properly.**”
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# **An ordained person**.
# **Legitimately authorised**.
# **Giving a robe of assignable size together with the Saṅgha**.
# **Complaining, wishing to complain afterwards**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Three origins, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
At Sāvatthī a certain **guild** prepared **a meal with robes**: “**We'll feed the Saṅgha and present them with robes.**” **The group-of-six** went to them and ==diverted them — “**Give these robes to such-and-such monks**” — **not to themselves but to other individuals**==. The monks criticised them, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Jānaṃ** — **knowing it himself**, or **told by others**, or **told by the donor**.
**Saṅghikaṃ** — belonging to the Saṅgha in a sense, being **intended** for it **even if not yet handed over**.
**Lābhaṃ** — **robes, almsfood, lodgings, medicines**; even **a lump of bath powder, a tooth stick, a fringe thread**.
**Pariṇataṃ** — **inclined** towards the Saṅgha by **words spoken** (“we will give, we will make”) or **by a gesture**.
**Puggalassa pariṇāmeyya** — diverts it **to another individual**: “**Give it to this monk**.” **Dukkaṭa in the effort** of diverting; **pācittiya when he receives it**.
## (B) Compared with nissaggiya 30
Compared with **nissaggiya 30** (diverting to oneself):
|! Point | Nissaggiya 30 | Pācittiya 82
| diverted to | **oneself** | **another individual**
| offence | **nissaggiya pācittiya** (the item forfeited) | **pure pācittiya**
| one to oneself, one to another | — | **both** offences
| other diversions among Saṅgha / shrine / individual | DK | DK
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of diverting to another individual. It is a **common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| The effort of diverting | DK
| Knowing it was intended for the Saṅgha, diverting it to another individual and its **being received** | PC
| In doubt whether intended; not intended, perceived as intended or in doubt | DK
| Diverting what was intended for a Saṅgha / shrine / individual to another Saṅgha / shrine | DK
## (E) No offence
# Perceiving what was intended **as not intended**.
# When asked “**Where shall we give?**”, saying, “**Give where your gift will be used, kept in repair and last long**, or **where your mind is inspired**.”
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# The gain **intended for the Saṅgha**.
# **Knowing this**, diverting it to another individual.
# **Its being received**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Three origins, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, three feelings**.
