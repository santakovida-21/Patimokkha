## කථා වස්තුව
@ සැවැත් නුවර · ජේතවනාරාමය
එක් **භික්ෂුණියක්** සැවැත් නුවර පිඬු සිඟා ආපසු එන කල්හි, එක් භික්ෂුවක් දැක "**ආර්‍යයාණෙනි, පිණ්ඩපාතය පිළිගන්න**" යි කීවාය. ඔහු "**හොඳයි, නැගණිය**" යි ==**සියල්ල ම** ගත්තේය==. කාලය ළං වූ බැවින් ඇයට නැවත පිඬු සිඟා යා නොහැකි වූයේ **බත් නොලැබ** සිටියාය. **දෙවන දිනයේ ද තෙවන දිනයේ ද** එසේ ම විය.
සිව්වන දිනයේ ඇය **වෙවුලමින්** වීථියේ යද්දී, රථයෙන් ආ **සිටුවරයෙක්** "**ආර්‍යාවෙනි, ඉවත් වන්න**" යි කීය. ඉවත් වන්නට යද්දී ඇය **එතැන ම ඇද වැටුණාය**. සිටුවරයා සමාව ඉල්ලූ කල්හි "**ඔබ මා හෙළුවේ නැත; මම ම දුර්වලයෙමි**" යි කියා කරුණ පැවසුවාය. ඔහු ඇයව ගෙදර ගෙන ගොස් වළඳවා, ==**"ස්ත්‍රීන්ට ලාභ ලැබීම දුෂ්කර ය; භික්ෂුණියකගේ අතින් භික්ෂූන් ආමිස පිළිගන්නේ කෙසේ ද?"**== යි දොස් කීය. භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ "**නෑ නොවන්නා නෑ නොවන්නියකට සුදුසු නුසුදුසු බව නොදනී**" යි වදාරා ශික්ෂාපදය පැනවූහ.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
A **nun**, returning from her almsround in Sāvatthī, saw a monk and said, “**Venerable, accept some almsfood.**” He said, “**Very well, sister,**” and ==took **all of it**==. As the time was near, she could not go for alms again and **went without food**. The same happened **on the second and third days**.
On the fourth day, as she walked down the street **trembling**, **a wealthy merchant** coming in a carriage said, “**Out of the way, lady.**” Trying to step aside, she **collapsed on the spot**. When the merchant asked forgiveness, she said, “**You didn't knock me down; I'm just weak,**” and told him why. He took her home, fed her, and complained: ==“**Women find it hard to get gains; how can the monks accept food from a nun's hand?**”== The Blessed One, saying, “**An unrelated man doesn't know what is suitable or unsuitable for an unrelated woman,**” laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Aññātikā** — **unrelated on the mother's or father's side back to the seventh generation**.
**Bhikkhunī** — one ordained **in both Saṅghas** (nuns' and monks').
**Antaragharaṃ paviṭṭhāya** — standing in **a street, a cul-de-sac, a crossroads or a house**. The same applies in elephant stables and the like.
==The offence depends on **where the nun stands**; **where the monk stands does not matter**==:
|! Nun stands | Monk stands | Offence
| **among the houses** (street etc.) | anywhere (street or another monastery) | PD
| **in another monastery etc.** | even in the street etc. | no offence
**Khādanīyaṃ / bhojanīyaṃ** — **the five foods: rice, porridge, flour, fish, meat**, and anything **to be chewed** other than **juice drinks, seven-day tonics and lifetime medicines** (i.e. food allowed until noon).
**Paṭidesetabbaṃ** — he must confess: “==**Friend, I have fallen into a blameworthy, unsuitable matter that ought to be acknowledged; I acknowledge it**==.”
## (B) How the offences fall
|! Stage | Offence
| **accepting**, thinking “**I'll chew, I'll eat**” | DK
| **each mouthful swallowed** | PD
| **juice drinks, seven-day tonics, lifetime medicines** — accepted and swallowed as food | DK
| when **mixed with food into a single taste** | PD
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning a certain monk, on the matter of **accepting food from an unrelated nun's hand among the houses**. It is a **rule not shared**, and **anāṇattika** — as are the other pāṭidesanīyas. It is a **triple pāṭidesanīya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| From an unrelated nun — perceiving her as unrelated, in doubt, or as related (**triple pāṭidesanīya**) — each mouthful | PD
| Ordinary food from **one ordained only on one side** (in the nuns' Saṅgha only) | DK
| A **related** nun **perceived as unrelated** or **in doubt** | DK
## (E) No offence
# From a **related** nun (perceived as related).
# She **has it given by another** (not giving herself).
# She **places it on the ground** and says, “**Venerable, I give this to you**” — he may accept and eat it after having it **formally offered** by her or someone else.
# Given while standing in **another monastery, a nuns' residence, a sectarians' lodging or a refectory**, or **brought outside the village**.
# **Juice drinks, seven-day tonics or lifetime medicines** given “**to use when there is reason**.”
# Taking from **a probationer** or **a novice nun**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= pañca — five)
# A **fully ordained** nun.
# **Unrelated**.
# **Accepting with one's own hand from her hand while she stands among the houses**.
# **Food allowed until noon**.
# **Swallowing**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Sheep's-wool origin** (body; body–mind), **by doing, no release by perception, without intention, blameworthy by convention, bodily action, three kinds of mind, three feelings**.

**කඨින සමුට්ඨානයි** (කාය-වාචා, කාය-වාචා-චිත්ත), **කිරියාකිරියයි** (පිළිගැනීම කිරියාව; නොවැළැක්වීම අකිරියාව), **නෝසඤ්ඤාවිමොක්ඛයි, අචිත්තකයි, පණ්ණත්තිවජ්ජයි, කාය-වචීකම්මයි, තිචිත්තයි, තිවේදනයි**.

## The Story
@ Rājagaha · Veḷuvana, the squirrels' feeding place
Monks were eating **on invitation at families' homes**. ==**The group-of-six nuns** stood **directing** the serving for the group-of-six monks: “**Give curry here, give rice here.**”== The group-of-six monks ate **as much as they liked**; **the other monks did not get enough**. The monks complained, “**How can they not dismiss the nuns who direct the serving?**” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Kulesu** — in the four kinds of family: **noble, brahmin, merchant, worker**.
**Nimantitā bhuñjanti** — eat having been invited with **one of the five foods**.
**Vosāsantī** — a nun who **directs** “**Give curry here, give rice here**” **according to friendship, acquaintance, intimacy, or sharing the same preceptor or teacher**.
**Apasādetabbā** — the monks eating must **dismiss** her: “==**Step aside, sister, while the monks are eating**==.”
**Ekassa cepi** — if **not even one monk** dismisses her —
|! Stage | Offence
| **accepting**, thinking “**I'll chew, I'll eat**” | DK
| **each mouthful swallowed** | PD
**Paṭidesetabbaṃ** — (in the plural) “==**Friend, we have fallen into a blameworthy, unsuitable matter that ought to be acknowledged; we acknowledge it**==.”
## (B) Allowed ways — compared with the first pāṭidesanīya
|! What the nun does | Under the first pāṭidesanīya | Under this rule
| **has her own food given** by another | — | no offence
| **gives her own food herself** (among the houses) | offence | no offence
| **gives others' food herself** | — | no offence
| **has others' food given** (out of friendship) | — | offence (if not dismissed)
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Rājagaha** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of **not dismissing a nun who directs the serving**. It is a **rule not shared**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pāṭidesanīya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| A fully ordained nun directing — perceiving her as ordained, in doubt, or as unordained — eating without dismissing her (**triple pāṭidesanīya**) | PD
| Not dismissing **one ordained only on one side** | DK
| An unordained woman **perceived as ordained** or **in doubt** | DK
## (E) No offence
# For one **perceiving her as unordained** (when she is unordained).
# She **has her own food given**; she **herself gives others' food**.
# She has given **what was not given**; **where it was not given**; **equally to all**.
# **A probationer** or **a novice nun** directs.
# In everything **other than the five foods**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= pañca — five)
# A **fully ordained** nun.
# **One of the five foods**.
# Directing **among the houses other than in the allowed ways**.
# **Not dismissing her**.
# **Swallowing**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Kaṭhina origin** (body–speech; body–speech–mind), **by doing and not doing** (accepting; not dismissing), **no release by perception, without intention, blameworthy by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of mind, three feelings**.

සැවැත් නුවර එක් කුලයක් **උභතෝපසන්න** විය — **උපාසකයා හා උපාසිකාව දෙදෙනා ම පැහැදුණු** (අට්ඨකථාවට අනුව දෙදෙනා ම **සෝතාපන්නයෝ** ය). ==**ශ්‍රද්ධාවෙන් වැඩුණ, භෝගයෙන් පිරිහුණ** ඒ කුලය, උදෑසන ලැබෙන **සියලු කෑම බීම භික්ෂූන්ට දී** සමහර විට **නිරාහාරව** සිටියේය==. මිනිස්සු "**ශාක්‍යපුත්‍ර ශ්‍රමණයෝ පමණ නොදැන පිළිගනිති**" යි දොස් කීහ. භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ එබඳු කුලයකට **ඤත්තිදුතිය කර්මයෙන් සේඛසම්මුතිය** දීමට අනුදැන ශික්ෂාපදය පැනවූහ.

## (අ) පද විවරණය

**පුබ්බෙ අනිමන්තිතො** — **අද හෝ හෙට** සඳහා **ගෙයි උපචාරයට පිවිසීමට පෙර** ආරාධනා නොලැබූ; ==**ගෙයි උපචාරයට පිවිසෙද්දී** ආරාධනා කළ ද එය "**අනිමන්තිත**" ම ය==.
**අගිලානො** — **පිඬු සිඟා යා හැකි** තැනැත්තා.
**ඛාදනීයං / භොජනීයං** — **ඔදන, කුම්මාස, සත්තු, මාළු, මස්** යන **භෝජන පස** සහ **යාමකාලික, සත්තාහකාලික, යාවජීවික හැර** සෙසු **කෑ යුතු දේ** (යාවකාලික).

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
A family in Sāvatthī was **confident on both sides** — **both the husband and the wife** (per the commentary, both **stream-enterers**). ==**Growing in faith but declining in wealth**, the family **gave all the food it had in the morning to the monks** and sometimes **went hungry**==. People complained, “**The Sakyan ascetics accept without moderation.**” The Blessed One allowed such a family to be given **the designation “in training” by a motion-and-one-announcement act**, and laid down the rule.
> **Why does it decline in wealth?** — Such a family declines **even with eight hundred million**, because **neither husband nor wife guards their wealth** (commentary).
**The two amendments:**
|! # | Incident | Added wording
| 1 | when the family invited the monks **at a festival**, the monks declined out of scruple, and the family complained: “**What use is our life if the venerables won't accept from us?**” | “**not invited beforehand**”
| 2 | **the family's regular monk was ill**; declining out of scruple and unable to go for alms, he **went without food** | “**not ill**”
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Sekkhasammataṃ kulaṃ** — a family **growing in faith but declining in wealth**, **given the designation “in training” by a motion-and-one-announcement act**.
**Pubbe animantito** — not invited, **for today or tomorrow**, **before entering the house precincts**; ==an invitation given **as he is entering the house precincts** still counts as “**not invited**”==.
**Agilāno** — one **able to go for alms**.
**Khādanīyaṃ / bhojanīyaṃ** — **the five foods: rice, porridge, flour, fish, meat**, and anything **to be chewed** other than **juice drinks, seven-day tonics and lifetime medicines** (i.e. food allowed until noon).
## (B) How the offences fall
|! Stage | Offence
| entering the house precincts and **accepting** food | DK
| having taken it, **each mouthful swallowed**, **wherever** he eats it | PD
| **juice drinks** etc. accepted and swallowed as food | DK
## (C) “Brought out of the house” — commentary
|! Situation | Ruling
| brought **to the seating hall** or **the monastery** and given | allowable
| brought out **before the monk arrives**, **placed at the door**, and given when he comes | allowable
| brought out from inside **after seeing the monk** | not allowable (Mahāpaccarī)
## (D) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning a number of monks, on the matter of **accepting without moderation**. There are **two amendments**: “**not invited beforehand, not ill**.” It is a **rule not shared**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pāṭidesanīya**.
## (E) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| From a family designated “in training” — perceiving it as such, in doubt, or as not — uninvited and not ill, accepting and eating (**triple pāṭidesanīya**) — each mouthful | PD
| **Juice drinks** etc. as food | DK
| A family **not designated**, **perceived as designated** or **in doubt** | DK
## (F) No offence
# For one **perceiving it as not designated** (when it is not).
# For one **invited beforehand**; for **one who is ill**.
# Eating **the leftovers** of one invited or ill.
# Taking alms **set out there for others**.
# Food **brought out of the house**, or **brought out beforehand and placed at the door** (before seeing the monk).
# **Regular meals, ticket meals, fortnightly, Uposatha or first-day meals**.
# **Juice drinks** etc. given “**to use when there is reason**.”
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (G) Factors (= pañca — five)
# A family **designated “in training”**.
# **Not invited beforehand**.
# **Not ill**.
# **Entering the house precincts**.
# Taking and **eating** food **other than regular meals and the like**.
## (H) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Sheep's-wool origin, by doing, no release by perception, without intention, blameworthy by convention, bodily action, three kinds of mind, three feelings**.

## කථා වස්තුව
@ කපිලවස්තු · නිග්‍රෝධාරාමය
එකල **ශාක්‍යයන්ගේ දාසයෝ කැරලි ගසා** සිටියහ. **ශාක්‍ය කාන්තාවෝ** ආරණ්‍ය සෙනසුන්වල දන් දීමට කැමති වූහ. එය ඇසූ දාසයෝ **මඟ රැක සිටියහ**. ==ප්‍රණීත කෑම බීම රැගෙන ආරණ්‍ය සෙනසුනට යන ශාක්‍ය කාන්තාවන්, දාසයෝ නික්මී **පැහැරගෙන දූෂණය ද කළහ**==. ශාක්‍යයෝ නික්මී සොරුන් **බඩු සහිතව අල්ලා**, "**ආරාමයේ සොරුන් වසද්දී භදන්තයන් අපට නොදන්වන්නේ කෙසේ ද?**" යි දොස් කීහ. භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ ශික්ෂාපදය පැනවූහ.
**අනුප්‍රඥප්තිය:** ආරණ්‍ය සෙනසුනක **භික්ෂුවක් ගිලන්ව** සිටියදී, මිනිසුන් ගෙනා ආහාර කුකුසින් නොපිළිගෙන, පිඬු සිඟා යා නොහැකිව **බත් නොලැබ** සිටියේය. එවිට "**නොගිලන්**" යන්න එක් කළහ.

## The Story
@ Kapilavatthu · Nigrodhārāma
At that time **the Sakyans' slaves were in revolt**. **Sakyan women** wished to give a meal at the wilderness lodgings. Hearing of it, the slaves **lay in wait on the road**. ==As the Sakyan women went to the wilderness lodging carrying fine food, the slaves came out and **robbed and violated them**==. The Sakyans came out, **caught the thieves with the goods**, and complained: “**How can the venerables not tell us that thieves are living at the monastery?**” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
**The amendment:** **a monk at a wilderness lodging was ill**; declining out of scruple the food people brought, and unable to go for alms, he **went without food**. “**Not ill**” was then added.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Āraññakaṃ senāsanaṃ** — a lodging **at least five hundred bow-lengths** from a village.
|! Term | Sign — in the monastery or its precincts
| **sāsaṅkaṃ** (suspect) | places where thieves **camped, ate, stood, sat or lay down** are seen
| **sappaṭibhayaṃ** (dangerous) | people are seen to have been **killed, robbed or beaten** by thieves
**Pubbe appaṭisaṃviditaṃ** — **not announced beforehand**. **Paṭisaṃviditaṃ** — when **a woman or man, lay or ordained**, comes to the monastery or its precincts and announces, “**Venerable sir, food from such-and-such a family will be brought.**”
**Ajjhārāme** — **inside** a fenced monastery; **in the precincts** of an unfenced one.
**Agilāno** — one **able to go for alms**.
## (B) What counts as “announced”?
|! Situation | Ruling
| bringing **just what was announced** at the monastery or its precincts | announced
| bringing cakes, rice etc. as **accompaniments** to the announced **porridge** etc. | announced
| hearing “**such-and-such a family is taking food after announcing it**,” **other families join in** | announced
| announced by sending **one of the five co-religionists** | **not announced**
| announced **outside** the monastery or precincts — to a monk met on the road or come to the village | **not announced**
==When it is announced, he **must say**, “**It is suspect**,” “**It is dangerous**”== — to be free of the charge “**they don't tell us when thieves are at the monastery**.” If they say, “**Never mind, we'll bring it**,” he should tell **the thieves**, “**People are coming here; go away**” — to be free of the charge “**they have us caught by their supporters**.”
## (C) How the offences fall
|! Stage | Offence
| unannounced food — **even given by travellers passing through the monastery** — **accepted** in the monastery or precincts | DK
| **each mouthful swallowed** | PD
| **juice drinks** etc. as food | DK
If unannounced food is brought, it should be **sent outside the monastery and announced**, then brought in; or monks should **go and receive it on the way**. **Leftovers** of food announced or brought for one ill are **allowable for others too**; any surplus, **kept after giving up storage**, is allowable **the next day** as well.
## (D) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down **in the Sakyan country** concerning a number of monks, on the matter of **not announcing that thieves were living at the monastery**. There is **one amendment**: “**not ill**.” It is a **rule not shared**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pāṭidesanīya**.
## (E) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Unannounced food — perceiving it as unannounced, in doubt, or as announced — not ill, accepting it in the monastery and eating (**triple pāṭidesanīya**) | PD
| **Juice drinks** etc. accepted and swallowed as food | DK
| **Announced** food **perceived as unannounced** or **in doubt** | DK
## (F) No offence
# For one **perceiving it as announced** (when announced).
# For **one who is ill** (even if unannounced).
# Food **brought after announcing**, or **leftovers of food brought for one ill**.
# **Received outside the monastery** and eaten inside.
# Roots, bark, leaves, flowers, fruit etc. **growing right there**.
# **Juice drinks** etc. received “**to use when there is reason**.”
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (G) Factors (= satta — seven)
# **A suspect, dangerous wilderness lodging**.
# The food **not growing there**.
# **Not ill**.
# **Not leftovers of one who is ill**.
# **Not announced**.
# **Accepting inside the monastery**.
# **Swallowing**.
## (H) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Kaṭhina origin**, **by doing and not doing** (accepting; not announcing), **no release by perception, without intention, blameworthy by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of mind, three feelings**.
