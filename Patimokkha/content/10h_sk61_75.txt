## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==taught Dhamma to **people wearing sandals**==. Monks of few wishes complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Pādukārūḷhassa** — to one ==**mounted on sandals (wooden clogs)**==:
|! Term | Meaning
| **akkanta** | just **standing on** them **without putting the strap between the toes**
| **paṭimukka** | **wearing them fastened**
| **omukka** | standing with **the heel strap undone**
==In **all three ways** — even with the strap undone — teaching is **not allowable**==.
**Dhammo** — ==what is **spoken by the Buddha, by disciples, by sages or by deities**, **connected with the meaning and with the Dhamma**==.
|! Way of teaching | Offence
| teaching **word by word** | DK **for each word**
| teaching **syllable by syllable** | DK **for each syllable**
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. There is **one amendment on account of illness** — ==in the rules on teaching Dhamma, “**not ill**” was added so that one may teach **if the listener is ill**==. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (C) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Teaching Dhamma to one wearing sandals who is not ill **out of disrespect** | DK
## (D) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Teaching one wearing sandals who is not ill**.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Dhamma-teaching origin** (speech–mind), **by doing and not doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling** (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī summary).

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==taught Dhamma to **people wearing shoes**==. Monks of few wishes complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Upāhanārūḷhassa** — to one ==**wearing shoes (leather sandals)**==:
|! Term | Meaning
| **akkanta** | just **standing on** them **without putting the strap between the toes**
| **paṭimukka** | **wearing them fastened**
| **omukka** | standing with **the heel strap undone**
==In **all three ways** teaching is **not allowable**==.
**Dhammo** — ==what is **spoken by the Buddha, by disciples, by sages or by deities**, **connected with the meaning and with the Dhamma**==.
|! Way of teaching | Offence
| teaching **word by word** | DK **for each word**
| teaching **syllable by syllable** | DK **for each syllable**
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. There is **one amendment on account of illness** — ==in the rules on teaching Dhamma, “**not ill**” was added so that one may teach **if the listener is ill**==. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (C) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Teaching Dhamma to one wearing shoes who is not ill **out of disrespect** | DK
## (D) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Teaching one wearing shoes who is not ill**.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Dhamma-teaching origin** (speech–mind), **by doing and not doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling** (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī summary).

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==taught Dhamma to **people lying down**==. Monks of few wishes complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Sayanagatassa** — ==to one **lying down**, even **on a mat** or **the bare ground**==, it is **not allowable** to teach standing or sitting, **even on a high bed, chair or ground**.
|! Teacher | Listener | Ruling
| lying — **higher or level** | lying | allowable
| lying | **standing / sitting** | allowable
| sitting | **standing / sitting** | allowable
| standing | **standing** | allowable
| standing or sitting | **lying down** | **not allowable**
**Dhammo** — ==what is **spoken by the Buddha, by disciples, by sages or by deities**, **connected with the meaning and with the Dhamma**==.
|! Way of teaching | Offence
| teaching **word by word** | DK **for each word**
| teaching **syllable by syllable** | DK **for each syllable**
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. There is **one amendment on account of illness** — ==in the rules on teaching Dhamma, “**not ill**” was added so that one may teach **if the listener is ill**==. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (C) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Teaching Dhamma to one lying down who is not ill **out of disrespect** | DK
## (D) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Teaching one lying down who is not ill**.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Dhamma-teaching origin** (speech–mind), **by doing and not doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling** (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī summary).

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==taught Dhamma to **people with wrapped heads (turbans)**==. Monks of few wishes complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Veṭhitasīso** — one whose head is wrapped **with a cloth** or **a topknot etc.** ==**so that the hairline does not show**==.
**Kesantaṃ vivarāpetvā** — teaching ==**after having him uncover the hairline**== is **no offence**.
**Dhammo** — ==what is **spoken by the Buddha, by disciples, by sages or by deities**, **connected with the meaning and with the Dhamma**==.
|! Way of teaching | Offence
| teaching **word by word** | DK **for each word**
| teaching **syllable by syllable** | DK **for each syllable**
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. There is **one amendment on account of illness** — ==in the rules on teaching Dhamma, “**not ill**” was added so that one may teach **if the listener is ill**==. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (C) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Teaching Dhamma to one with a wrapped head who is not ill **out of disrespect** | DK
## (D) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# Teaching **after having him uncover the hairline**.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Teaching one with a wrapped head who is not ill**.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Dhamma-teaching origin** (speech–mind), **by doing and not doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling** (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī summary).

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==**sitting on the ground**, taught Dhamma to **people sitting on seats**==. Monks of few wishes complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Chamāyaṃ nisīditvā** — ==**sitting on the ground**==.
**Āsane nisinnassa** — to one sitting ==**even on a spread cloth or grass**==.
**Dhammo** — ==what is **spoken by the Buddha, by disciples, by sages or by deities**, **connected with the meaning and with the Dhamma**==.
|! Way of teaching | Offence
| teaching **word by word** | DK **for each word**
| teaching **syllable by syllable** | DK **for each syllable**
## (B) Illustration
!dgm: sk-ground **A posture not to be used** — the teacher sits on the ground while the listener, not ill, sits on a seat.
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. There is **one amendment on account of illness** — ==in the rules on teaching Dhamma, “**not ill**” was added so that one may teach **if the listener is ill**==. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (D) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Sitting on the ground and teaching one on a seat who is not ill **out of disrespect** | DK
## (E) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Teaching from the ground to one on a seat**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Samanubhāsana origin** (body–speech–mind), **by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling** (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī summary).

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==**sitting on low seats**, taught Dhamma to **people on high seats**==. Monks of few wishes complained. The Blessed One told **the story of the outcaste (chapaka)** — “**Monks, even then, teaching mantras sitting on a low seat to one on a high seat displeased me; how much more teaching Dhamma so now?**” — and laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Nīce āsane nisīditvā** — **sitting on a low seat**.
**Ucce āsane nisinnassa** — to one sitting ==**on a raised place, even on the ground**== it is not allowable to teach.
**Dhammo** — ==what is **spoken by the Buddha, by disciples, by sages or by deities**, **connected with the meaning and with the Dhamma**==.
|! Way of teaching | Offence
| teaching **word by word** | DK **for each word**
| teaching **syllable by syllable** | DK **for each syllable**
## (B) Illustration
!dgm: sk-lowseat **A posture not to be used** — the teacher on a low seat, the listener on a high one.
## (C) The story of the outcaste
|! Character | What happened
| **the outcaste (chapaka)** | when his pregnant wife craved **mangoes out of season**, he climbed **the king's ever-fruiting mango tree** and hid there
| **the king** | sitting **on a high seat**, learnt **mantras from his brahmin chaplain**
| **the brahmin** | taught the mantras **sitting on a low seat**
| **the outcaste** | thinking, “**The king is unrighteous, the brahmin is unrighteous, and I too, stealing the king's mango for a woman; the world has lost all bounds**,” **dropped down between them** and spoke verses
**Meaning of the verses (per the Samantapāsādikā):** “**He who teaches the mantra and he who learns it unrighteously — neither knows the meaning nor sees the text.**” The brahmin: “**Having eaten the king's fine rice with meat sauces, I do not live by the Dhamma, though I know it.**” The outcaste: “**Shame on gain of wealth and fame that leads to ruin or is had by unrighteousness. Leave, great brahmin — other beings cook and eat too; let not unrighteousness break you as a stone breaks a pot.**”
## (D) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. There is **one amendment on account of illness** — ==in the rules on teaching Dhamma, “**not ill**” was added so that one may teach **if the listener is ill**==. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (E) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Sitting low and teaching one on a high seat who is not ill **out of disrespect** | DK
## (F) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (G) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Teaching from a low seat to one on a high seat**.
## (H) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Samanubhāsana origin** (body–speech–mind), **by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling** (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī summary).

**සමනුභාසන සමුට්ඨානයි** (කාය-වාචා-චිත්ත), **කිරියයි, සඤ්ඤාවිමොක්ඛයි, සචිත්තකයි, ලෝකවජ්ජයි, කාය-වචීකම්මයි, අකුසලචිත්තයි, දුක්ඛවේදනයි** (කඞ්ඛාවිතරණී සාරාංශය).

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==**standing**, taught Dhamma to **people sitting**==. Monks of few wishes complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Na ṭhito nisinnassa** — one should not teach Dhamma **standing to one who is sitting**.
==If **a senior elder seated** asks a question of **a young monk standing in attendance on him**, he should not answer==. Since out of respect one cannot say, “**Please stand up and ask**,” it is **allowable** to answer thinking, “**I will tell the monk standing beside me**.”
**Dhammo** — ==what is **spoken by the Buddha, by disciples, by sages or by deities**, **connected with the meaning and with the Dhamma**==.
|! Way of teaching | Offence
| teaching **word by word** | DK **for each word**
| teaching **syllable by syllable** | DK **for each syllable**
## (B) Illustration
!dgm: sk-standing **A posture not to be used** — the teacher standing, the listener sitting.
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. There is **one amendment on account of illness** — ==in the rules on teaching Dhamma, “**not ill**” was added so that one may teach **if the listener is ill**==. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (D) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Standing and teaching one sitting who is not ill **out of disrespect** | DK
## (E) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Teaching while standing to one sitting**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Samanubhāsana origin** (body–speech–mind), **by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling** (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī summary).

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==**defecated, urinated and spat on green plants**==. People complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Harite** — on **green grass, plants, crops**.
**Kheḷo** — **spittle**; ==**nasal mucus (siṅghāṇikā)** is also included== (Mahāpaccarī).
## (B) What counts as “green” and the exemptions
|! Situation | Ruling
| **roots of a living tree** visible on the ground; **branches touching the ground** | **counts as green** — not allowable
| **sitting on the trunk** and letting it fall on a bare spot | allowable
| it comes out **suddenly while looking for** a bare spot | allowable
| being **in a place for the sick** | allowable
| unable to find a bare spot, done **on a wad of grass or straw**, which **later spreads onto green** | allowable
| done **on a bare spot** that **later spreads onto green** | allowable
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. The rule includes “**not ill**.” It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (D) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Defecating, urinating or spitting on green plants when not ill **out of disrespect** | DK
## (E) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# What was done **on a bare spot** spreading onto green.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Relieving oneself or spitting on green plants when not ill**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**First-pārājika origin, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

==**In all sekhiyas there is no offence on account of illness**; but **in three — heaped almsfood (30), covering curry with rice (36), looking critically (38) — there is no “ill” exemption**== (Samantapāsādikā).
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. The rule includes “**not ill**.” It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (D) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Defecating, urinating or spitting in usable water when not ill **out of disrespect** | DK
## (E) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# What was done **on dry ground** washing into the water.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Relieving oneself or spitting in water when not ill**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
