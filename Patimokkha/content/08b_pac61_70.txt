## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
Ven. **Udāyī** had been **an archer** as a layman. He disliked **crows**. ==He **shot crows, cut off their heads and impaled them on stakes** in a row==. When monks asked, “**Who killed these crows?**”, he said, “**I did; I dislike crows.**” The monks criticised him, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Sañcicca** — **knowing, perceiving, intending, deciding** — with the intention to kill.
**Pāṇo** — here **an animal** is meant.
**Jīvitā voropeyya** — deprives of life — cuts off, destroys the life faculty.
==Killing **a small or a large animal** is **pācittiya alike**==; but in killing a large animal **the unwholesomeness is greater** because **the effort is greater**.
## (B) With the rule on killing a human being
The means (by one's own hand, by command, by missile, by a fixed device, by spells, by psychic power), the rulings on commands etc. — are to be considered **just as for killing a human**.
|! The one killed | Offence
| **a human being** | PR
| **a yakkha, peta, or animal in human form** | TH
| **an animal** — small or large | PC
| **in doubt** about an animal; a non-living thing perceived as living, or in doubt | DK
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning Ven. **Udāyī**, on the matter of depriving animals of life. It is a **common rule**, and **sāṇattika** — there is an offence also by ordering “Kill it.”
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Knowing it is a living being, killing or having an animal killed with intent to kill | PC
| Killing a living being **in doubt** | DK
| A non-living thing perceived as living, or in doubt | DK
## (E) No offence
# Perceiving it **as not a living being**.
# **Not deliberate** (asañcicca) — e.g. treading on it unawares while walking.
# **Not knowing** — not knowing the act would kill it.
# A death caused by something done **without intending death**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= pañca — five)
# **An animal**.
# **Knowing it is a living being**.
# **The intention to kill**.
# **An effort** (upakkama).
# **Death resulting from it**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Three origins** (body–mind, speech–mind, body–speech–mind), **by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==knowingly used **water containing living beings**== — drinking it, bathing in it, washing bowls. The monks of few wishes criticised them, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Jānaṃ** — knowing it himself or being told by others.
**Sappāṇakaṃ** — water with living beings **that die through its use**. (Water with minute creatures that do not die by use is not counted here.)
**Paribhuñjeyya** — uses it by **drinking, bathing, washing a bowl** etc. — ==a pācittiya **for each act**==.
## (B) Compared with the sprinkling rule
Using **water with living beings** in it — **drinking, bathing** etc. — **knowing there are beings** in it, or knowing “**they will die from the use**,” is pācittiya **for each effort**; using it perceiving no beings, not knowing, or knowing they will not die, is no offence. Compared with related rules:
|! Rule | Fault
| **Pācittiya 20** | **pouring** water with living beings **on grass, clay etc.** / having it poured
| **Pācittiya 62** | **using** water with living beings **oneself** (drinking, bathing etc.)
| **Pācittiya 61** | killing animals **with intent to kill**
**The water strainer** — one may **strain** such water, remove the creatures and then use it; the creatures removed must be put **back into water**.
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of using water with living beings. It is a **common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Knowing it contains beings and that they die by use, using it — each act | PC
| Using it **in doubt** whether it contains beings | DK
| Water without beings perceived as having them, or in doubt | DK
## (E) No offence
# **Not knowing** it contains beings.
# Perceiving it **as without beings**.
# Knowing that **the beings will not die** through the use.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# The water **contains living beings**.
# Knowing “**they will die through use**.”
# The water **being so**.
# **Using** it.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Three origins, by doing, release by perception, with intention, an offence by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of consciousness, three feelings**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==agitated to reopen **legal issues settled according to the Dhamma**==, saying, “**The act was not done, was badly done, must be done again, was not completed, was badly completed.**” The monks of few wishes criticised them, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Yathādhammaṃ** — **by the very Dhamma** declared for settling that particular issue.
**Nihatādhikaraṇaṃ** — an issue **settled by the Dhamma the Teacher declared in the Chapter on Settlements**. (How issues are settled is shown under **the seven ways of settling legal issues**.)
**Punakammāya ukkoṭeyya** — going to monk after monk and saying “**The act was not done**” etc., he **stirs it up for re-doing** — **not letting it stand as it is**. Doing so is pācittiya.
## (B) A well-settled issue
==An issue settled by the Dhamma **is well settled**.== However —
|! Situation | What to do
| someone **objects while the act is in progress** | he must be **persuaded** and then the act done
| if not | **the act is invalid**, and those doing it **incur an offence**
| agitating **after the act is complete** | pācittiya **under this rule**
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of agitating. It is a **common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Knowing it was a legitimate act, agitating to reopen it | PC
| **In doubt** about a legitimate act | DK
| An illegitimate act perceived as legitimate, or in doubt | DK
## (E) No offence
# In either case **perceiving it as an illegitimate act**.
# **Knowing** that “the act was done **unlawfully**, **by an incomplete assembly**, or **against one not deserving it**,” and agitating.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# The issue **settled according to the Dhamma**.
# **Knowing** it.
# **Agitating**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Three origins, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
Ven. **Upananda** committed **a saṅghādisesa** — **intentional emission of semen** — told it to a co-resident pupil of his brother, and said, “**Don't tell anyone.**” Similarly **a certain monk**, knowing of such an offence, ==**concealed** it==. Later, when a quarrel broke out among the monks, it came to light, and the monks criticised him: “**Didn't you know of an offence and conceal it?**” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Duṭṭhullaṃ** — here **a saṅghādisesa** offence is meant.
**Paṭicchādeyya** — having **come to know** it by any means, conceals it.
==The moment he **gives up the responsibility**==, thinking, “**Now I won't tell any monk about him**,” it is pācittiya — **even if he tells later**, it is pācittiya; he will tell having already incurred it.
## (B) “The chain” of offences
If, having given up the responsibility, he tells another **only so as to keep it concealed**, and that one tells another, in this way ==**even a hundred ascetics** incur the offence **as long as the chain is not broken**==.
|! Situation | Result
| the offender tells **A**; A tells **B** | the chain continues
| **B tells it back to A** (the third tells the second) | ==**the chain is broken**==
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning a certain monk, on the matter of concealing a serious offence. It is a **rule not shared** (for nuns, concealing is a pārājika), and **anāṇattika**.

## The Story
@ Rājagaha · Veḷuvana, the squirrels' feeding place
In Rājagaha the parents of a boy named **Upāli** thought, “**How could he live at ease?**” and, deciding “**Ascetics live at ease**,” wished him to go forth. Upāli and his **sixteen friends** — **the group-of-seventeen** — went forth, and the monks ==gave them full ordination **though they were not yet twenty**==. Before dawn they cried, “**Give us gruel, give us rice, give us snacks!**” The Blessed One rebuked: “**A person under twenty cannot endure cold, heat, hunger, thirst and painful feelings,**” and laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Ūnavīsativassaṃ** — not having completed twenty years **from conception**.
**Upasampādeyya** — gives full ordination **as preceptor**.
**So ca puggalo anupasampanno** — ==whether ordained knowingly or not, he **remains unordained**==.
**Te ca bhikkhū gārayhā** — **all except the preceptor** (the teacher and the assembly) are blameworthy — they all incur **dukkaṭa**.
**Idaṃ tasmiṃ pācittiyaṃ** — this pācittiya falls **on the preceptor alone**.
## (B) Consequences and sequence of offences
|! Point | Ruling
| the one ordained | is **unordained** — once known, he must be **ordained again**
| as long as he does not know | there is **no obstacle to heaven or liberation** for him
| if after ten years he **ordains another** | if **the quorum is complete without him**, that ordination is **valid**
| the preceptor — seeking the assembly, teacher, bowl, robes, authorising the boundary, fixing a water-sprinkle boundary; **at the motion and the two announcements** | DK
| the preceptor — **at the end of the announcement** | PC
| the teacher and the assembly | DK
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Rājagaha** concerning a number of monks, on the matter of ordaining one under twenty. It is a **rule not shared**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Knowing he is under twenty, ordaining him as preceptor — at the end of the announcement | PC
| **In doubt** about one under twenty; one of full age perceived as under twenty, or in doubt | DK
## (E) No offence
# In either case **perceiving him as of full age**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Under twenty**.
# **Perceiving him as under age**.
# **Giving full ordination**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Three origins, by doing, release by perception, with intention, an offence by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of consciousness, three feelings**.

එක් භික්ෂුවක් **රජගහයේ සිට පටියාලෝකයට** යන **සොර ගැල් කණ්ඩායමක්** සමඟ ගමන් කළේය. ==ඔවුහු **රජුගේ සුංගම් මඟහරින** අය වූහ==. **සුංගම් නිලධාරීහු** ඔවුන් අල්ලා භික්ෂුව ද රජු වෙත ගෙන ගියහ. රජතුමා "**ස්වාමීනි, ඔබ මේ සොරුන් සමඟ යන බව දැන සිටියා ද?**" යි අසා, "**නැත**" යි කී කල්හි "**ශ්‍රමණයෙකු මැරීම නුසුදුසුය; වඩින්න**" යි නිදහස් කළේය. මිනිස්සු "**මේ ශ්‍රමණයෝ සොරුන් සමඟ යති**" යි දොස් කීහ. භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ ශික්ෂාපදය පැනවූහ.
## (අ) පද විවරණය
**ථෙය්‍යසත්ථො** — **රජු රවටා** හෝ **සුංගම් මඟහැරීමට** කැමති, **සොරකම් කළ හෝ නොකළ**, මගට පිළිපන් **සොරුන්ගේ** කණ්ඩායම.
**ජානං** — සොර කණ්ඩායමක් බව **දැනගෙන**.
**සංවිධාය** — **කතිකා කොට** — යන කාලය ගැන සංකේතයක් ඇති කොට.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
A monk travelled **from Rājagaha to Paṭiyāloka** with **a caravan of thieves**, ==people **evading the king's tolls**==. **Customs officers** caught them and took the monk to the king too. The king asked, “**Venerable sir, did you know you were travelling with thieves?**” “**No.**” “**It is not fitting to put an ascetic to death; you may go,**” and he released him. People complained, “**These ascetics travel with thieves!**” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Theyyasattho** — a band of **thieves** on the road, **who have or have not yet committed a theft**, wishing **to cheat the king** or **evade tolls**.
**Jānaṃ** — **knowing** it to be a thieves' caravan.
**Saṃvidhāya** — **having arranged** — made an appointment about the time of going.
## (B) Arranging and travelling
For one who, perceiving it as a thieves' caravan and knowing it, travels with them by arrangement, **the rulings on offences for arranging and travelling** are:
|! Situation | Offence
| arranging | DK
| each village precinct — **the first foot** | DK
| **the second foot** | PC
| a road without villages — **every half-yojana** | PC
| arranging **by himself while the people do not** | DK
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning a certain monk, on the matter of travelling the same road with a thieves' caravan by arrangement. It is a **common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Knowing it is a thieves' caravan, travelling by arrangement into a village precinct / over a half-yojana | PC
| Arranging by himself while the people do not | DK
| **In doubt** about a thieves' caravan | DK
| Not a thieves' caravan, perceived as one or in doubt | DK
## (E) No offence
# Perceiving it **as not a thieves' caravan**.
# Travelling **without arrangement**.
# Travelling **with a change of time**.
# **In emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# **A thieves' caravan**.
# **Knowing** it.
# **Arranging**.
# Travelling **without a change of time**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Thieves'-caravan origin** (body–mind; body–speech–mind), **by doing, release by perception, with intention, an offence by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of consciousness, three feelings**.

## කථා වස්තුව
@ සැවැත් නුවර · ජේතවනාරාමය
එක් භික්ෂුවක් **කොසොල් රටේ සිට සැවැත් නුවරට** යද්දී මගදී එක් ගමක දොරටුව අසල **සැමියා සමඟ කලහ කොට නිවසින් පිටත් වූ කාන්තාවක්** "**ස්වාමීනි, කොහේ යන්නහු ද?**" යි අසා, "**සැවැත් නුවරට**" යි කී කල්හි "**මම ද ආර්‍යයන් සමඟ යමි**" යි ==කතිකා කොට ගියාය==. ඇයගේ **සැමියා** ඇය සොයා පැමිණ භික්ෂුව දැක "**මගේ බිරිඳ ගෙන යන්නේ ඔබ ද?**" යි ==ඔහුට **පහර දුන්නේය**==. භික්ෂුව එය භික්ෂූන්ට කී කල්හි භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ ශික්ෂාපදය පැනවූහ.
## (අ) පද විවරණය
**මාතුගාමෙන** — **මනුෂ්‍ය ස්ත්‍රියක්** — **එදින උපන් දැරියක** වුව ද; විඤ්ඤු, හොඳ නරක දන්නා.
**සංවිධාය** — කතිකා කොට — **යන කාලය ගැන සංකේතයක්** ඇති කොට ("අද යමු", "හෙට යමු").
**එකද්ධානමග්ගං** — එක ම දීර්ඝ මාර්ගයක් — හෝ **එකට** දීර්ඝ මාර්ගයක.
**අන්තමසො ගාමන්තරම්පි** — අවම වශයෙන් **එක් ගමක සිට අනෙක් ගමට** වුව ද.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
A monk was travelling **from Kosala to Sāvatthī**. Near a village gate, **a woman who had quarrelled with her husband and left home** asked, “**Venerable sir, where are you going?**” “**To Sāvatthī.**” “**I'll come with you,**” and ==she went with him by arrangement==. Her **husband**, searching for her, saw the monk: “**Are you taking my wife away?**” — and ==**beat him**==. The monk told the monks, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Mātugāmena** — **a human female** — **even a girl born that day** — who is discerning, knowing good and bad.
**Saṃvidhāya** — by arrangement — **fixing a time to go** (“let's go today,” “let's go tomorrow”).
**Ekaddhānamaggaṃ** — the same long road — or a long road **together**.
**Antamaso gāmantarampi** — **even from one village to the next**.
## (B) Compared with Pācittiya 27
Compared with **Pācittiya 27** (with a nun) —
|! Point | Pācittiya 27 | Pācittiya 67
| companion | **a nun** | **a woman** (a laywoman)
| supplementary rule | “**aññatra samayā**” (a dangerous road) | **none** — no occasion allowed
| rule | not shared | **common** (for nuns, with a man)
| counting | per village precinct / half-yojana | **the same**
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning a certain monk, on the matter of travelling the same road with a woman. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Arranging on unsuitable ground; the first foot into a village precinct | DK
| Having arranged, travelling into a village precinct / over a half-yojana — perceiving her as a woman, in doubt, or as not (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| Arranging **by himself while the woman does not** | DK
| With **a yakkhinī, peta-woman, paṇḍaka, or female animal in human form** | DK
## (E) No offence
# Travelling **without arrangement**.
# **Only the woman** arranges.
# Travelling **with a change of time**.
# **In emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# **A woman**.
# Travelling **by mutual arrangement**.
# **The time not changed** and **no emergency**.
# **Entering a village precinct** or **passing a half-yojana**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Journey origin** (body; body–speech; body–mind; body–speech–mind), **by doing, no release by perception, without intention, an offence by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of consciousness, three feelings**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**Ariṭṭha**, who had been **a vulture-trainer** as a layman, conceived this **evil view**: “**As I understand the Dhamma taught by the Blessed One, those things he calls obstructions are not obstructions for one who engages in them.**” Monks went and admonished him: “**Don't misrepresent the Blessed One; sensual pleasures are like a skeleton…**” but he ==**clung stubbornly to that view**==. The Blessed One summoned him, rebuked him — “**Foolish man, to whom do you know me to have taught the Dhamma so?**” — allowed **formal admonition**, and laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Antarāyikā** — things that **obstruct heaven and liberation** — of five kinds:
|! Obstruction | Meaning
| **of kamma** | **the five heinous deeds** and **violating a nun**
| **of defilement** | **fixed wrong view**
| **of result** | **rootless rebirth**, being a paṇḍaka, an animal etc.
| **of abuse** | **reviling noble ones**
| **of transgressing rules** | **transgressing training rules laid down** (until the offence is confessed)
Ariṭṭha, thinking “**Like the touch of soft rugs, the touch of a woman is also fine**,” **saw no fault in transgressing by intercourse**, and took this view with regard to **obstructions of transgressing rules**.
**Anekapariyāyena** — in many ways: “**Sensual pleasures are like a skeleton, like a lump of meat…**”
## (B) The procedure of formal admonition
|! Step | Description | Offence
| 1. admonition by monks | monks who see or hear tell him **three times**: “**Mā āyasmā evaṃ avaca… alañca pana te paṭisevato antarāyāyā**” | if he does not give it up, **dukkaṭa**; monks who **hear but do not speak** also **dukkaṭa**
| 2. in the midst of the Saṅgha | he is **brought before the Saṅgha** and told the same three times | if he does not give it up, **dukkaṭa**; those who don't speak, **dukkaṭa**
| 3. formal admonition | by a **motion-and-three-announcements act**, up to three times | **dukkaṭa** at the motion and the first two announcements
| 4. end | if he still does not give it up **at the end of the third announcement** | PC
(At the end of the formal rebuke the offence is **pācittiya**.)
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning **Ariṭṭha**, on the matter of not giving up an evil view. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya** (perceiving the act as legitimate, in doubt, or as not).
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Not giving it up when admonished by monks and before the Saṅgha; at the motion and two announcements | DK
| Not giving it up **at the end of the third announcement** of a legitimate formal admonition (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| **An illegitimate act** — in all three perceptions | DK
## (E) No offence
# **Not formally admonished**.
# **Giving it up**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **A legitimate act**.
# **Formal admonition**.
# **Not giving it up**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Samanubhāsana origin** (body–speech–mind), **by not doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**Ariṭṭha**, who would not give up his evil view, was **suspended** by the Saṅgha **by an act of suspension**. **The group-of-six**, ==knowing this, **shared food** with him, **communed** with him and **slept under one roof** with him==. The monks of few wishes criticised them, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Tathāvādinā** — one who speaks thus: “**As I understand the Dhamma taught by the Blessed One…**”
**Akatānudhammena** — “**anudhamma**” is **the reinstatement (osāraṇa)** granted after seeing the proper conduct of one **suspended by the Dhamma and Vinaya** for not seeing an offence, not making amends, or not giving up an evil view. One for whom this **has not been done** is “akatānudhamma.”
**Sambhuñjeyya** — shares **material things** (giving / receiving food) or **Dhamma** (giving / receiving recitation).
**Saṃvaseyya** — performs **Uposatha** and other Saṅgha acts with him.
**Saha vā seyyaṃ kappeyya** — lies down **under one roof**, even in **separate sections**.
## (B) Counting the offences
|! Act | Measure of offence
| **material sharing** — giving / receiving much **in one effort** | **one** PC
| material sharing — **with breaks** | PC **for each effort**
| **Dhamma sharing** — teaching / having taught by lines etc. | PC **for each line**
| **communion** | **at the end of the act**
| **sharing a sleeping place** | by **the effort of lying down** of one when the other is lying down
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of associating with the monk Ariṭṭha. It is a **common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Knowing he is suspended, sharing, communing or sleeping under one roof | PC
| **In doubt** about a suspended monk | DK
| One not suspended, perceived as suspended or in doubt | DK
## (E) No offence
# In either case perceiving him **as not suspended**.
# Knowing “**he has been reinstated**.”
# Knowing “**he has given up that view**.”
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# A suspended monk **not reinstated**.
# **Knowing** it.
# **Associating** etc.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Three origins, by doing, release by perception, with intention, an offence by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of consciousness, three feelings**.

**Samaṇuddeso** — **a novice**.
**Cara pire vinassa** — “**Go away, stranger; be gone** — go where we won't see you.”
**Tathānāsitaṃ** — there are three kinds of expulsion:
|! Expulsion | Meaning
| **from communion** | **suspension** for not seeing an offence etc.
| **from the robes** | “**The corrupter must be expelled**,” “**Expel the nun Mettiyā**” — **disrobing and expulsion**
| **as a penalty** | “**From today, friend novice, you may not claim the Blessed One as your teacher; nor do you have the two or three nights of sharing a sleeping place with monks that other novices have; go, be gone**” — ==**this is what is meant here**==
**Upalāpeyya** — **entices** him: “**I'll give you a bowl, robe, recitation, explanation**.”
**Upaṭṭhāpeyya** — has him **serve** him, accepting **bath powder, clay** etc.
**Sambhuñjeyya** — shares **material things** (giving / receiving food) or **Dhamma** (giving / receiving recitation).
**Saha vā seyyaṃ kappeyya** — lies down **under the same roof**, even where the precincts are separate.
## (B) Counting the offences
|! Act | Measure of offences
| **material sharing** — giving / receiving much **in one effort** | **one** PC
| material sharing — **stopping and starting** | PC **for each effort**
| **Dhamma sharing** — teaching / having taught by lines etc. | PC **for each line**
| **sharing a sleeping place** | by **each effort of lying down** when the other is lying down
| **enticing**, **having him serve** | PC

## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of enticing the novice Kaṇṭaka. It is a **common rule**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Knowing he is an expelled novice, enticing, having him serve, associating, sleeping under one roof | PC
| **In doubt** about an expelled novice | DK
| One not expelled, perceived as expelled or in doubt | DK
## (E) No offence
# In either case perceiving him **as not expelled**.
# Knowing “**he has given up that view**.”
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# A novice **expelled as a penalty**.
# **Knowing** it.
# **Enticing, having him serve, associating or sleeping under one roof**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
According to the commentaries: **samanubhāsana origin, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.
