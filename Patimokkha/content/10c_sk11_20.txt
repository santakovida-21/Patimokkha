## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==went among the houses **laughing loudly**==. People complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Ujjagghikāya** — **laughing loudly** (instrumental of manner).
**Hasanīyasmiṃ vatthusmiṃ mihitamattaṃ** — ==merely **smiling** at **something funny** is **no offence**==.
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (C) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Going among the houses laughing loudly **out of disrespect** | DK
## (D) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# Merely **smiling** at **something funny**.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Going among the houses laughing loudly**.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Samanubhāsana origin** (body–speech–mind), **by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling** (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī summary).

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==went among the houses **swaying the body**==. People complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Kāyappacālakaṃ** — **swaying** the body again and again (the same pattern in every such term).
**Kāyaṃ paggahetvā** — one should go ==keeping the body **still**, **upright**, in **an even posture**==. The Pāli also says “**letting the body droop (olambento)**.”
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.

**පඨමපාරාජික සමුට්ඨානයි, කිරියයි, සඤ්ඤාවිමොක්ඛයි, සචිත්තකයි, ලෝකවජ්ජයි, කායකම්මයි, අකුසලචිත්තයි, දුක්ඛවේදනයි**.
#en
## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==went among the houses **swinging the arms**==. People complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Bāhuppacālakaṃ** — **swinging** the arms again and again (the same pattern in every such term).
**Bāhuṃ paggahetvā** — one should go ==keeping the arms **still**==. The Pāli also says “**letting the arms droop (olambento)**.”
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (C) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Going among the houses swinging, letting the arms dangle **out of disrespect** | DK
## (D) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Going among the houses swinging the arms**.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**First-pārājika origin, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

**සීසප්පචාලකං** — හිස **සොලවමින් සොලවමින්** ("සීසප්පචාලකං" ආදී සෑම පදයක ම මේ ක්‍රමය ය).
**සීසං පග්ගහෙත්වා** — ==හිස **නොසොල්වා, සෘජුව** තබා== හිඳිය යුතුය. පාළියේ "**හිස එල්ලා (ඔලම්බෙන්තො)**" යි ද කියයි.
**වාසූපගතස්ස** — ==හිඳීම සම්බන්ධ (කාය, බාහු, සීස) ශික්ෂාපද **තුනෙහි** **වාසය සඳහා පැමිණ සිටින්නාට අනාපත්ති**== (කඞ්ඛාවිතරණී).

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==sat among the houses **shaking the head**==. People complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Sīsappacālakaṃ** — **shaking** the head again and again (the same pattern in every such term).
**Sīsaṃ paggahetvā** — one should sit ==keeping the head **still and upright**==. The Pāli also says “**letting the head droop (olambento)**.”
**Vāsūpagatassa** — ==in **the three** sitting rules (body, arms, head), **no offence for one staying there**== (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī).
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (C) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Sitting among the houses shaking, letting the head droop **out of disrespect** | DK
## (D) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# ==When **staying there** (vāsūpagata)==.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Sitting among the houses shaking the head**.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**First-pārājika origin, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.
