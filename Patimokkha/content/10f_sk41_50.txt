**Anāhaṭe** — ==**not yet brought**, i.e. **not yet reached the mouth**== (the mouthful).
**Mukhadvāraṃ vivarati** — **opens the mouth**.
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.

**සමනුභාසන සමුට්ඨානයි** (කාය-වාචා-චිත්ත), **කිරියයි, සඤ්ඤාවිමොක්ඛයි, සචිත්තකයි, ලෝකවජ්ජයි, කාය-වචීකම්මයි, අකුසලචිත්තයි, දුක්ඛවේදනයි** (කඞ්ඛාවිතරණී සාරාංශය).
#en
## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==spoke **with food in their mouths**==. People complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Sakabaḷena mukhena** — ==the offence is for speaking **with so much food in the mouth that the words are incomplete**==.
If, **while teaching Dhamma**, one has **myrobalan** or **liquorice** in the mouth, it is **allowable to speak** as long as **there is not so much that the words become unclear** (Samantapāsādikā, Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī).
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (C) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Speaking with so much food in the mouth that the words are unclear **out of disrespect** | DK
## (D) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Speaking with food in the mouth**.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Samanubhāsana origin** (body–speech–mind), **by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling** (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī summary).
