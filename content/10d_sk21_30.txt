## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==received almsfood **heaped up**==. People complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Samatittikaṃ** — ==**evenly filled, evenly full**== — filled in **a bowl fit for determination** without going past **the inner rim line**.
**Thūpīkataṃ** — put in the bowl and **heaped up past the inner rim line**.
**Piṇḍapātaṃ** — any **food allowed until noon**.
!dgm: sk-tittika **Level and heaped** — against the inner rim line of the bowl.
## (B) “Heaped” — the Elders' views
|! Elder | View
| Elder **Abhaya** | what is made of **the five foods**
| Elder **Cūḷanāga**, master of the Tipiṭaka | quoting “almsfood means porridge, rice, snacks, powder-balls, **tooth sticks, fringe threads**,” said **even a fringe thread** may not be heaped
| Elder **Cūḷasumana** (in Rohaṇa) | said “**Cūḷanāga has strayed from the teaching**,” and ruled that it is limited **by food allowed until noon** — the accepted view
## (C) What is allowed even if heaped
|! Situation | Ruling
| in a vessel **not fit for determination** | allowable even heaped
| **juice drinks, seven-day tonics, lifetime medicines** | allowable even in the determined bowl
| receiving in **two bowls**, filling one to send to the monastery | allowable (Mahāpaccarī)
| cakes, sugarcane pieces, fruit that **sink down** as they are put in | not “heaped”
| **takkola, vaṭaṃsaka** etc. **placed on top** | not “heaped”
| put **on a leaf or plate** set on the bowl | not “heaped”
## (D) No exemption even for the sick
==The no-offence list here **does not mention the sick**; so **even one who is ill must receive it level**==. Though in all sekhiyas there is **no offence on account of illness**, in **three** — **heaped almsfood** (30), **covering curry with rice** (36), **looking critically** (38) — **there is no “ill” exemption**.
> The fault is only **in receiving**; **what is received is properly received** — it may be eaten.
## (E) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.

**First-pārājika origin, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.
