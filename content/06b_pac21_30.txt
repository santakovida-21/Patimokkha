## කථා වස්තුව
@ සැවැත් නුවර · ජේතවනාරාමය
භික්ෂුණීන්ට අවවාද කරන ස්ථවිරයන්ට **සිවුරු, පිණ්ඩපාත, සෙනසුන්, ගිලන්පස** බොහෝ සෙයින් ලැබෙන බව දුටු **ෂඩ්වර්ගීය භික්ෂූහු** "අපි ද අවවාද කරමු" යි භික්ෂුණීන් කැඳවා ==සුළු ධර්ම කථාවක් පමණක් කොට, දවස ම තිරශ්චීන කථාවෙන් ගෙවා== පිටත් කළහ. භික්ෂුණීන් භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේට "අවවාදය සඵල නොවීය" යි සැළ කළහ. භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ **අංග අටකින් යුත් භික්ෂුවක් ඤත්තිචතුත්ථ කර්මයෙන් "භික්ඛුනොවාදක" ලෙස සම්මත කිරීම** අනුදැන "අසම්මත භික්ෂුවක් භික්ෂුණීන්ට අවවාද කරයි නම් පාචිත්තිය" යි පැනවූහ.
ඉන්පසු ෂඩ්වර්ගීයයෝ ==**සීමාවෙන් පිටතට ගොස් එකිනෙකා සම්මත කොට**== "අපි ද සම්මත වූවෝ ය" යි නැවත එසේ ම කළහ. එවිට භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ සම්මත කළ යුතු භික්ෂුවගේ **අංග අට** නියම කළහ.
## (අ) පද විවරණය
**අසම්මතො** — භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ **ඤත්තිචතුත්ථ කර්මයෙන්** අනුදත් **භික්ඛුනොවාදක සම්මුතිය** නොලැබූ.
**භික්ඛුනියො** — **උභතෝ සංඝයා** කෙරෙහි උපසම්පදාව ලද භික්ෂුණීන්.
**ඔවදෙය්‍ය** — භික්ෂුණී සංඝයාට, බොහෝ දෙනෙකුට හෝ එක් භික්ෂුණියකට **අෂ්ට ගරුධර්ම** අවවාද වශයෙන් කියයි. **අවවාදය අවසානයේ** පාචිත්තිය වේ.
## (ආ) භික්ඛුනොවාදකගේ අංග අට හා ගරුධර්ම අට

# **බහුශ්‍රැත** වීම.
# **උභය ප්‍රාතිමෝක්ෂය** විස්තර වශයෙන් මනාව ප්‍රගුණ, සූත්‍ර හා අනුව්‍යඤ්ජන වශයෙන් විනිශ්චය කළ හැකි වීම.
# **කල්‍යාණ වාක්** — පැහැදිලි, නිදොස් කථා ඇති වීම.

# භික්ෂුණීන්ට අවවාද කිරීමට **සමර්ථ** වීම.

## (ඇ) ප්‍රඥප්තිය හා ආණත්තිය

## (උ) සමුට්ඨානාදිය
**පදසෝධම්ම සමුට්ඨානයි** (වාචා, වාචා-චිත්ත), **කිරියයි, නෝසඤ්ඤාවිමොක්ඛයි, අචිත්තකයි, පණ්ණත්තිවජ්ජයි, වචීකම්මයි, තිචිත්තයි, තිවේදනයි**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
Seeing that the elders who exhorted the nuns received plenty of **robes, alms-food, lodgings and medicines**, **the group-of-six** said, “Let's exhort them too,” called the nuns, ==gave only a short Dhamma talk and spent the whole day in pointless talk==, then sent them away. The nuns told the Blessed One the exhortation had been fruitless. The Blessed One allowed **a monk with eight qualities to be authorised as exhorter of nuns by a motion-and-three-announcements act**, and laid down: “Should an unauthorised monk exhort the nuns — pācittiya.”
Then the group-of-six ==**went outside the boundary and authorised one another**==, saying, “We too are authorised,” and did the same again. So the Blessed One specified **the eight qualities** of the monk to be authorised.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Asammato** — not granted the **authorisation as exhorter of nuns** that the Blessed One allowed by a **motion-and-three-announcements act**.
**Bhikkhuniyo** — nuns fully ordained **in both Saṅghas**.
**Ovadeyya** — speaks **the eight heavy rules** by way of exhortation to the Saṅgha of nuns, to several, or to one nun. The pācittiya falls **at the end of the exhortation**.
## (B) The eight qualities and the eight heavy rules
**The eight qualities of a monk who may be authorised:**
# **Virtuous** — restrained by the Pātimokkha, seeing danger in the slightest fault.
# **Learned**.
# Has **both Pātimokkhas** well learnt in detail, able to analyse them by rule and detail.
# **Of good speech** — clear and faultless.
# **Liked and agreeable** to most of the nuns.
# **Able** to exhort the nuns.
# Has **never before committed a heavy offence (intercourse)** with a woman gone forth in the Blessed One's name and wearing the ochre robe.
# Of **twenty rains** or more.
**How to exhort** — the authorised monk should sweep the cell, set out water, prepare seats, and sit **with a companion**. When the nuns have come, bowed and sat down —
|! The monk asks | Reply | Then
| “Samaggāttha, bhaginiyo?” | “Samaggāmhāyyā” | he asks about the heavy rules
| “Vattanti, bhaginiyo, aṭṭha garudhammā?” | “Vattantāyyā” | he hands over the exhortation: “**Eso, bhaginiyo, ovādo**”
| — | “Na vattantāyyā” | he **recites the eight heavy rules**
**The eight heavy rules:**
|! # | Heavy rule
| 1 | A nun ordained a hundred years must bow to, rise for, salute and pay respect to **a monk ordained that day**.
| 2 | She must not spend the rains **in a residence without monks**.
| 3 | Every half-month she should look to the monks' Saṅgha for two things: **asking the Uposatha date** and **coming for exhortation**.
| 4 | After the rains she must hold the **Pavāraṇā before both Saṅghas** on what was seen, heard or suspected.
| 5 | A nun who has committed a heavy offence must undergo **half a month's mānatta** before both Saṅghas.
| 6 | A **probationer** trained two years in the six rules should seek ordination from both Saṅghas.
| 7 | A monk must not be **abused or reviled** in any way.
| 8 | From today **admonition of monks by nuns** is barred; admonition of nuns by monks is not barred.
Each heavy rule is one “**to be honoured, respected, revered and venerated, not to be transgressed as long as life lasts**.”
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of exhorting. The specification of the eight qualities is a **supplementary rule**. It is a **rule not shared** (monks only), and **anāṇattika**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| An unauthorised monk exhorting with the eight heavy rules — at the end of the exhortation | PC
| Exhorting with **some other Dhamma**; exhorting a nun **ordained on one side only** (by the nuns' Saṅgha) | DK
| If the authorising act was **not legitimate** — perceived as illegitimate, in doubt, or as legitimate; to an **incomplete** Saṅgha of nuns, three triads = 9; to a **complete** one, another 9 = **18 pācittiyas** | PC
| If the authorising act was **legitimate** — the same pattern except the last case (“legitimate act perceived as legitimate, complete Saṅgha perceived as complete”) = **17 dukkaṭas** | DK
| When told “We are complete,” speaking another Dhamma; when told “We are incomplete,” reciting the eight rules; speaking another Dhamma without handing over the exhortation | DK
## (E) No offence
# Authorised by a legitimate act, perceiving it as such, exhorting a **complete** Saṅgha perceived as complete.
# Giving the text of the heavy rules as a **recitation lesson**; giving an **explanation**.
# Reciting when told “**Osārehi ayyā**” (please recite, venerable sir); **answering a question** put to him.
# Nuns listening while he speaks **for another's benefit**.
# Speaking to a **probationer** or **novice nun**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Not authorised**.
# The nun **fully ordained** (by both Saṅghas).
# **Speaking the eight heavy rules by way of exhortation**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Padasodhamma origin** (speech; speech–mind), **by doing, no release by perception, without intention, an offence by convention, verbal action, three kinds of consciousness, three feelings**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
The elder monks took turns exhorting the nuns, and Ven. **Cūḷapanthaka**'s turn came. The nuns said to one another, “Today's exhortation won't be fruitful; Ven. Cūḷapanthaka will **repeat the same inspired verse over and over**.” When he recited the verse “**Adhicetaso appamajjato…**” again and again, they said just that. Then the elder ==rose into the air, walked, stood, sat and lay down in the sky, emitted smoke, blazed and vanished==, displaying many psychic wonders, and spoke that verse and much other Dhamma. “**Never before has there been so fruitful an exhortation**,” the nuns rejoiced.
But because he ==exhorted them **until the sun set**==, by the time the nuns returned **the city gates were shut**, and they **spent the night outside the city**. Seeing them enter at dawn, people complained, “**These nuns have spent the night in the monastery with the monks and are only now coming back!**” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Sammatopi** — even one authorised by a motion-and-three-announcements act.
**Atthaṅgate sūriye** — **after the sun has set** (until dawn).
**Ovadeyya** — exhorting **with the heavy rules or any other Dhamma** — even an authorised monk incurs ==**a pācittiya**==. (In the previous rule another Dhamma was only dukkaṭa; here the time makes it pācittiya.)
## (B) Compared with the previous rule
|! Point | Exhortation (Pāc. 21) | After sunset (Pāc. 22)
| Fault | exhorting **without authorisation** | exhorting **after sunset**
| Applies to | an unauthorised monk | **an authorised monk too**
| Content | heavy rules → PC; other Dhamma → DK | heavy rules **or any Dhamma** → PC
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning Ven. **Cūḷapanthaka**, on the matter of exhorting after sunset. It is a **rule not shared**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Exhorting after sunset — perceiving it as set, in doubt, or as not set (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| The sun not yet set, perceived as set or in doubt | DK
| Exhorting a nun **ordained on one side only** | DK
## (E) No offence
# Giving the heavy rules as a **recitation** or **explanation**.
# Reciting when asked; **answering a question**.
# Nuns listening to talk **meant for another**.
# Speaking to a **probationer** or **novice nun**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **The sun having set**.
# The nun **fully ordained**.
# **Exhorting**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Padasodhamma origin, by doing, no release by perception, without intention, an offence by convention, verbal action, three kinds of consciousness, three feelings**.

## The Story
@ Kapilavatthu · Nigrodhārāma
**The group-of-six monks** ==went to the nuns' quarters and exhorted **the group-of-six nuns**==. When other nuns told them, “Come to us for exhortation,” they replied, “The venerables come right here and exhort the group-of-six nuns.” The monks criticised this, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
Later, when the nun **Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī** was **ill**, the elders, scrupulous about going to the nuns' quarters to exhort, did not go. Since she wished for a Dhamma talk, the Blessed One added the supplementary rule “**aññatra samayā — except on the proper occasion, when a nun is ill**.”
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Bhikkhunupassayaṃ** — even a place where a nun stays **a single night** counts as nuns' quarters.
**Ovadeyya** — here only exhorting **with the heavy rules** is pācittiya.
**Samayo** — **when a nun is ill**, i.e. unable to go for the exhortation or for communion (Uposatha).
## (B) How offences add up
|! The monk | Exhorting with the heavy rules at the nuns' quarters | Offences
| **unauthorised** — by day | this rule + Exhortation (Pāc. 21) | **2 pācittiyas**
| **unauthorised** — after sunset | + After sunset (Pāc. 22) | **3 pācittiyas**
| **authorised** — at night | this rule + After sunset | **2 pācittiyas** (being authorised, no pācittiya for the heavy-rule exhortation)
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down **among the Sakyans** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of going to the nuns' quarters to exhort. “**Aññatra samayā**” is a supplementary rule. It is a **rule not shared**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Without the occasion, going to the quarters and exhorting a fully ordained nun with the heavy rules — perceiving her as ordained, in doubt, or as unordained (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| An unordained woman perceived as ordained, or in doubt | DK
| Exhorting a nun **ordained on one side only** with anything; a fully ordained nun with **another Dhamma** | DK
## (E) No offence
# **On the occasion** — a sick nun.
# **To an unordained woman**.
# Giving the heavy rules as **recitation** or **explanation**; reciting when asked; answering a question; nuns overhearing talk meant for another.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# **Going to the quarters**.
# A **fully ordained** nun.
# **No proper occasion**.
# **Exhorting with the heavy rules**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Kathina origin** (body–speech, body–speech–mind), **by doing, no release by perception, without intention, an offence by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of consciousness, three feelings**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
After the eight qualities were specified, **the group-of-six**, ==no longer authorised==, saw that the exhorting elders received robes and other gains, and said, “**The elder monks exhort the nuns for the sake of material gain.**” The monks of few wishes criticised them, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Āmisahetu** — for the sake of one of these: **robes, alms-food, lodgings, medicines, hospitality, respect, honour, veneration**.
**Bhikkhū** — here meaning **authorised monks** — those the Saṅgha has authorised to exhort the nuns.
**Evaṃ vadeyya** — saying so ==**wishing to disparage**== is pācittiya.
## (B) “The authorised unordained”
Only saying it of an ordained monk is pācittiya. One who **received authorisation at the proper time but afterwards (through an offence) stands at the novice's level** is called “authorised but unordained”; saying it of him, or of an **unauthorised** person, is **dukkaṭa**.
|! Of whom it is said | Offence
| an ordained monk authorised **by a legitimate act** | PC
| a monk authorised **by an illegitimate act** | DK
| an **unauthorised** ordained monk | DK
| an authorised or unauthorised **unordained** person | DK
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of saying “they exhort for gain.” It is a **rule not shared**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Saying so, wishing to disparage, of a monk authorised by a legitimate act — perceiving it as legitimate, in doubt, or as illegitimate (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| For an illegitimate act — in all three perceptions (**triple dukkaṭa**) | DK
| Saying so of an unauthorised ordained monk, or of an authorised or unauthorised unordained person | DK
## (E) No offence
# Saying so of one who **really does** exhort for the sake of robes and the like (speaking the truth).
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# The monk being **ordained**.
# **Authorised by a legitimate act**.
# **Not one who aims at gain**.
# Saying so **wishing to disparage**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Three origins, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

## (උ) සමුට්ඨානාදිය
**සඤ්චරිත්ත සමුට්ඨානයි** (සමුට්ඨාන සය), **කිරියාකිරියයි** (දීම කිරියාව, හුවමාරුව නොගැනීම අකිරියාව), **නෝසඤ්ඤාවිමොක්ඛයි, අචිත්තකයි, පණ්ණත්තිවජ්ජයි, කාය-වචීකම්මයි, තිචිත්තයි, තිවේදනයි**.
#en
## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
In a certain street of Sāvatthī a monk and a nun both went for alms. Telling each other, “Go, sister, alms are being given at such-and-such place,” “Go, venerable sir, alms are being given there,” ==they became acquainted through seeing each other often==. When robes were being distributed to the Saṅgha, he asked the nun, who had come for the exhortation, “Sister, this is my share of robe-cloth; will you accept it?” “Yes, venerable sir, my robes are worn out.” ==He gave her his robe==. Since his own robe was worn too, the monks told him, “Now make yourself a robe.” Hearing what had happened, the Blessed One asked, “Is she a relative?” and rebuked him: “**An unrelated man does not know what is fitting or unfitting, available or unavailable, for an unrelated woman**,” and laid down the rule.
Later monks, being scrupulous, would not give robes to nuns **even in exchange**. The nuns complained, and the Blessed One allowed **exchange (pārivattaka) with the five co-religionists**, adding “aññatra pārivattakā.”
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Aññātikā** — not related on the mother's or father's side back **to the seventh generation**.
**Bhikkhunī** — ordained in both Saṅghas.
**Cīvaraṃ** — any of the six kinds — **the minimum assignable size**.
**Aññatra pārivattakā** — giving other than by exchange is pācittiya.
## (B) Compared with nissaggiya 5
Compared with **nissaggiya 5** (accepting a robe) —
|! Point | Nissaggiya 5 | Pācittiya 25
| Act | **receiving** a robe from an unrelated nun | **giving** a robe to an unrelated nun
| Recipient | **the monk** | **the nun**
| Offence | nissaggiya pācittiya (robe forfeited) | pure pācittiya
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning a certain monk, on the matter of giving a robe. “Aññatra pārivattakā” is a **supplementary rule**. It is a **rule not shared**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Giving a robe, not in exchange, to an unrelated nun — perceiving her as unrelated, in doubt, or as related (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| Giving to a nun **ordained on one side only** | DK
| A related nun perceived as unrelated, or in doubt | DK
## (E) No offence
# To a **related** nun.
# **In exchange** — even something small for something large, or large for small.
# The nun taking it **on trust**; taking it **temporarily**.
# Giving **some requisite other than a robe**.
# Giving to a **probationer** or **novice nun**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **A robe of assignable size**.
# **Not an exchange**.
# Giving **to an unrelated nun**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Go-between origin** (six origins), **by doing and not doing** (giving; not taking an exchange), **no release by perception, without intention, an offence by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of consciousness, three feelings**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
Ven. **Udāyī** was **skilled at robe-making**. A nun asked him, “Venerable sir, please sew a robe for me.” He sewed it, dyed it well, ==drew **an erotic picture** in the middle==, folded it and put it away, telling her, “Keep it folded, and when the nuns' Saṅgha comes for exhortation, wear it and **walk behind** the Saṅgha.” When she did so, people complained, “**How shameless these nuns are — drawing pictures on their robes!**” Asked whose work it was, she said, “Ven. Udāyī's.” The nuns said, “**It wouldn't suit even shameless rogues — how much less Ven. Udāyī!**” The Blessed One asked, “Is she a relative?”, rebuked him and laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Cīvaraṃ** — a robe **fit for wearing as lower or upper robe**.
**Sibbeyya** — he sews it himself: ==**each time he pushes the needle in and draws it out**== is a pācittiya. If he pierces a hundred times and draws it out **once**, it is **one** pācittiya.
**Sibbāpeyya** — has another sew it.
|! How he orders | Offence of the one who orders
| “**Sew it**,” once — even if the other finishes all the needlework | **one** pācittiya
| “**Everything to be done on this robe is your job**” — when the other finishes | a pācittiya **for each run of stitching (ārāpatha)**
| many things in one word / ordering again and again | by that count — nothing more to say
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning Ven. **Udāyī**, on the matter of sewing a robe. It is a **rule not shared**, **sāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (C) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Sewing or having sewn a robe of an unrelated nun — perceiving her as unrelated, in doubt, or as related (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| A related nun perceived as unrelated, or in doubt | DK
| Sewing a robe of a nun **ordained on one side only** | DK
## (D) No offence
# Sewing **some requisite other than a robe** — a bag, sandals etc.
# For a **related** nun.
# Sewing even a robe for a **probationer** or **novice nun**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Belonging to an unrelated nun**.
# A robe **fit for wearing as lower or upper robe**.
# **Sewing or having it sewn** in the way described.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Go-between origin** (six origins), **by doing, no release by perception, without intention, an offence by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of consciousness, three feelings**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==travelled the same long road with nuns by arrangement==. People complained, “**Just as we go about with our wives, these Sakyan ascetics go about with nuns by arrangement!**” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
Later, as many monks were going **from Sāketa to Sāvatthī**, some nuns said, “We'll come with you.” Being scrupulous, the monks said it was not allowed and told them to go ahead or behind. After the monks had gone, ==the nuns following were **robbed and raped by thieves** on the road==. Hearing this, the Blessed One allowed the occasion of “**a road that is risky, dangerous, to be travelled with a caravan**,” adding a supplementary rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Saṃvidhāya** — having arranged, i.e. **made an appointment about the time of going** (“Let's go today / tomorrow”).
**Ekaddhānamaggaṃ** — one and the same long road — or a long road **together**.
**Antamaso gāmantarampi** — **even from one village to the next**.
**Satthagamanīyo** — to be travelled **with a caravan** — dangerous to travel alone.
## (B) The arrangement and counting village precincts
**Where it is arranged** — except **the nuns' quarters, the monastery, the assembly hall and sectarians' lodgings**, any place is **unsuitable ground**; arranging there is itself **dukkaṭa**.
**The journey** — having arranged, if he goes with the nun **without changing the agreed time** (“today or tomorrow”) — even by another gate or another road —
|! Situation | Offence
| until he enters the **village precinct** that people have marked for the next village | no offence
| the **first foot** into the precinct | DK
| the **second foot** — and so for **each village precinct** | PC
| a **road without villages** — for **every half-yojana** | PC
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of travelling the same road. “**Aññatra samayā**” is a supplementary rule. It is a **rule not shared** (the nuns have their own), **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Arranging on unsuitable ground | DK
| Having arranged, travelling without the occasion into a village precinct / over a half-yojana — perceiving it as arranged, in doubt, or as not (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| Not arranged, perceived as arranged or in doubt | DK
| Arranging **by himself alone while the nun does not** | DK
| With a woman ordained on one side only etc. — **under the woman rule (Pāc. 67)** | PC

# **On the occasion** — a risky, dangerous road to be travelled with a caravan.
# **He himself does not arrange** (only the nun does).
# **The time is changed** — going on a day other than the one agreed.
# **In emergencies** (dangers from kings, thieves, fire etc.).

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==boarded the same boat with nuns by arrangement and amused themselves==. People complained, “**Just as we amuse ourselves in boats with our wives, so do these!**” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
Later, as many monks were going **from Sāketa to Sāvatthī**, they had to cross a river. Some nuns said, “We'll cross with you.” Being scrupulous, the monks declined. Crossing separately, ==the nuns were **robbed and raped by thieves**==. The Blessed One added the supplementary rule “**except for crossing over**.”
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Saṃvidhāya** — **for the sake of amusement**, making an appointment about the time of boarding.
**Uddhaṃgāminiṃ** — going **up-river (against the current)** for amusement.
**Adhogāminiṃ** — likewise going **down-river (with the current)**.
==Going up or down only to reach a landing== is no offence.
**Aññatra tiriyaṃ taraṇāya** — **except for crossing over**.
## (B) Counting the offences
|! How one travels | Offence
| along **a bank with villages** | PC for **each village precinct**
| along **a bank without villages**, or **mid-stream on a river a yojana wide** | PC for **each half-yojana**
| **at sea** | one may go as one likes
| **crossing** the river | no offence
Making the arrangement is **dukkaṭa**. Here **only a change of time** gives exemption — not a change of **landing or boat**.
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of boarding a boat. “**Aññatra tiriyaṃ taraṇāya**” is a supplementary rule. It is a **rule not shared**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Having arranged, going up- or down-river for amusement — perceiving it as arranged, in doubt, or as not (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| Not arranged, perceived as arranged or in doubt | DK
| Arranging by himself alone while the nun does not | DK
## (E) No offence
# **Crossing over**.
# **Not arranging himself**.
# **The time changed**.
# **In emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# **Boarding by mutual arrangement**.
# **The time not changed**.
# **Not crossing over** and **no emergency**.
# **Passing a village precinct or a half-yojana**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Journey origin, by doing, no release by perception, without intention, an offence by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of consciousness, three feelings**.

**ථුල්ලනන්දා** භික්ෂුණිය එක් පවුලක **නිත්‍ය දානය ලබන කුලූපිකාවක්** වූවාය. ඒ ගෘහපතියා **ස්ථවිර භික්ෂූන්** — සාරිපුත්ත, මහාමොග්ගල්ලාන, මහාකච්චාන, මහාකොට්ඨිත, මහාකප්පින, මහාචුන්ද, අනුරුද්ධ, රේවත, උපාලි, ආනන්ද, රාහුල — දානයට ආරාධනා කළේය. ථුල්ලනන්දා "**මහා නාගයන් සිටියදී මේ චේටකයන් (කොලුවන්) ආරාධනා කළේ ඇයි?**" යි අසා, "**ආර්‍ය දේවදත්ත, කෝකාලික, කටමෝදකතිස්සක, ඛණ්ඩදේවියාගේ පුත්‍රයා, සමුද්දදත්ත**" යන්නෝ මහා නාගයෝ යැයි කීවාය. ඇය කථා කරමින් සිටියදී ම ස්ථවිරයෝ පැමිණියහ; එවිට ඇය "**ඇත්තෙන් ම ඔබ ආරාධනා කළේ මහා නාගයන් ය**" යි කීවාය. "**දැන් ම ඔබ මොවුන් චේටකයන් කළා ය, දැන් මහා නාගයන් කරන්නී ද?**" යි ගෘහපතියා ඇය ==නිවසින් පිටත් කොට, නිත්‍ය දානය ද නතර කළේය==.
**දේවදත්ත** ==භික්ෂුණිය පොළඹවා (පරිපාචනය කොට) ලබා දුන් පිණ්ඩපාතය දැන දැන වැළඳූ බව== අසා භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ ගරහා ශික්ෂාපදය පැනවූහ. පසුව, භික්ෂුවකට පෙරාතුව ම සූදානම් කළ දානය ගැන කුකුස් ඇති වූ කල්හි "**අඤ්ඤත්‍ර පුබ්බෙ ගිහිසමාරම්භා**" අනුප්‍රඥප්තිය පැනවූහ.
## (අ) පද විවරණය
**භික්ඛුනිපරිපාචිතං** — භික්ෂුණිය විසින් — තමාගේ හෝ ඒ භික්ෂුවගේ **ඥාති, පවාරණය කළ අය නොවන** ගිහියන් වෙත — භික්ෂුවගේ **ගුණ ප්‍රකාශ කොට**, "**ආර්‍යයන්ට දෙන්න, ආර්‍යයන්ට කරන්න**" යි නිපදවූ.
**ජානං** — තමා දැනගෙන, හෝ අනුන් කියා, හෝ ඒ භික්ෂුණිය ම කියා.
**පිණ්ඩපාතො** — **භෝජන පහෙන්** (බත, කොමු, සත්තු, මාළු, මස්) එකක්.
**පුබ්බෙ ගිහිසමාරම්භා** — භික්ෂුණිය පොළඹවන්නට **පෙරාතුව ම** ගිහියන් භික්ෂූන් සඳහා **සූදානම් කළ** දෙය (සමාරම්භ = සූදානම් කිරීම); හෝ ඥාතීන්, පවාරණය කළ අයගේ දෙය. එය හැර අන් දෙයක් දැන දැන අනුභව කිරීම පාචිත්තියයි.

## The Story
@ Rājagaha · Veḷuvana, the squirrels' feeding place
The nun **Thullanandā** was a family-visitor at a certain household, **receiving a regular meal** there. The householder invited **the elder monks** — Sāriputta, Mahāmoggallāna, Mahākaccāna, Mahākoṭṭhita, Mahākappina, Mahācunda, Anuruddha, Revata, Upāli, Ānanda and Rāhula. Thullanandā asked, “**Why invite these small fry when there are great nāgas?**” — meaning “**Ven. Devadatta, Kokālika, Kaṭamodakatissaka, the son of Khaṇḍadevī, and Samuddadatta**.” While she was still speaking, the elders arrived, and she said, “**Indeed you have invited great nāgas!**” “**Just now you made them small fry, and now great nāgas?**” — the householder ==put her out of the house and stopped her regular meal==.
Hearing that **Devadatta** ==knowingly ate alms procured through a nun's prompting==, the Blessed One rebuked him and laid down the rule. Later, when a monk was scrupulous about food already prepared before, he added “**aññatra pubbe gihisamārambhā**.”
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Bhikkhunīparipācitaṃ** — brought about by a nun, who **speaks of the monk's virtues** to householders who are **not relatives or inviters** of hers or his, saying, “**Give to the venerable, do for the venerable**.”
**Jānaṃ** — knowing it himself, or others tell him, or the nun herself tells him.
**Piṇḍapāto** — any of **the five staple foods** (rice, porridge, flour-cakes, fish, meat).
**Pubbe gihisamārambhā** — what householders had **already prepared** for monks **before** the nun's prompting (samārambha = preparation), or what belongs to relatives or inviters. Knowingly eating other than that is pācittiya.
## (B) Counting and details
==**Dukkaṭa on receiving**; a pācittiya **for every mouthful** swallowed.==
|! Food | Ruling
| one of **the five staple foods** procured by a nun | PC
| **gruel, snacks, fruit** and anything other than the five | no offence
| procured by **probationers, novices, novice nuns** etc. | no offence
| procured by a nun **ordained on one side only** | DK
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Rājagaha** concerning **Devadatta**, on the matter of eating alms procured by a nun. “**Aññatra pubbe gihisamārambhā**” is a supplementary rule. It is a **rule not shared**, and **anāṇattika**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Receiving | DK
| Knowing it was procured, swallowing it — for each mouthful | PC
| Eating what was procured by a nun ordained on one side only | DK
| Not procured, perceived as procured; in doubt in either case | DK
## (E) No offence
# In either case **perceiving it as not procured**.
# What **householders had already prepared**.
# What was procured by **probationers, novice nuns** and the like.
# **Anything other than the five staple foods**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= pañca — five)
# **Procured by a nun**.
# **Knowing** it was procured.
# **Not already prepared by householders**.
# **One of the five staple foods**, rice etc.
# **Swallowing** it.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**First-pārājika origin** (body–mind), **by doing, release by perception, with intention, an offence by convention, bodily action, three kinds of consciousness, three feelings**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
Ven. **Udāyī**'s **former wife** had gone forth among the nuns. She often came to him, and he to her. Then he ==sat down **alone with that nun in a private place**==. The monks of few wishes criticised him, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Bhikkhuniyā saddhiṃ** — with a nun ordained **in both Saṅghas**.
**Eko ekāya** — just the monk and the nun — **with no second person**.
**Raho** — **private to the eye** (screened by a wall, door, curtain, trees etc.) and **private to the ear** (where ordinary talk cannot be heard).
**Nisajjaṃ kappeyya** — when the nun is seated the monk sits or lies down close by, or when the monk is seated the nun does — or **both** do.
## (B) With the second undetermined rule
This rule forms **one section** with the second Aniyata and with **Upananda's fourth rule further on (Pāc. 45 — sitting with a woman in a screened place)**; it was laid down separately **because of the incident**.
|! Rule | With whom
| Second Aniyata | a woman — the offence undetermined (SD / PC)
| **Pācittiya 30** | **a nun**
| Pācittiya 45 | **a woman** — in a screened place
**A discerning man** — a **second person** who can break the privacy, **neither blind nor deaf**, able to understand lewd and decent talk — present **within twelve cubits** means there is no privacy.
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning Ven. **Udāyī**, on the matter of sitting in private with a nun. It is a **rule not shared**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Sitting or lying down alone with a nun in a private place — perceiving her as a nun, in doubt, or as not (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| With a nun **ordained on one side only** | DK
| Preliminary efforts in going to her out of desire for privacy | DK
## (E) No offence
# A **discerning second person** (neither blind nor deaf) is present.
# For one **standing**.
# For one **not seeking privacy**.
# For one sitting **with his mind on other business**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= pañca — five)
# A **fully ordained nun**.
# A place **private to eye and ear**.
# **Seeking privacy**.
# **No discerning second person**.
# **Sitting or lying down**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**First-pārājika origin** (body–mind), **by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily action, unwholesome mind, two feelings** (pleasant / neutral).
