## The Story
@ Vesālī · the Gabled Hall in the Great Wood
The Saṅgha had received plenty of **cakes**, and some were left over. The Blessed One said, “**Ānanda, give the cakes to those who eat scraps.**” Ven. Ānanda seated them in a row and gave **one each**. To a certain **female wanderer** he gave ==**two cakes stuck together**, thinking it was one==. The wanderers nearby asked her, “**Is this ascetic your lover?**” “No — he gave me two thinking it was one.”
Again, an **Ājīvaka** who got rice at a food distribution said, “**Householders, I got this rice from that shaven-headed householder,**” ==speaking scornfully==. On both incidents the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Acelako** — a **naked** ascetic (Ājīvakas etc.).
**Paribbājako** — **anyone gone forth** (a sectarian) other than a monk or novice.
**Paribbājikā** — any **woman gone forth** other than a nun, probationer or novice nun.
**Khādanīyaṃ vā bhojanīyaṃ vā** — **anything that can be swallowed**, except water and tooth-sticks.
**Sahatthā dadeyya** — gives **by body, by something attached to the body, or by tossing**.
## (B) Counting the offences
|! How given | Offence
| giving food **in one effort** | **one** PC
| giving **with breaks** (separate acts) | PC **for each effort**
| giving **water or tooth-sticks** | DK
| one not a sectarian perceived as one, or in doubt | DK
The rule was laid down not to deprive them of food but because **the world gossips about relations between those gone forth** and because they **disparage the teaching**; hence having **an unordained person** give is no offence.
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Vesālī** concerning Ven. **Ānanda**, on the matter of giving two cakes to a female wanderer. It is a **rule not shared**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Giving food with one's own hand to a naked ascetic or male / female wanderer — perceiving them as sectarian, in doubt, or as not (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| Giving water or tooth-sticks | DK
| One not a sectarian perceived as one, or in doubt | DK
## (E) No offence
# Perceiving one not a sectarian as not.
# **Having an unordained person give**.
# **Setting the vessel near them** and saying “Take it.”
# Giving **into a vessel they have set down**.
# Giving **ointment for external use**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **A member of another sect**.
# Something **not allowed** (not water or tooth-stick).
# Giving something swallowable **to be swallowed**, **with one's own hand**, **not into a vessel they set down**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Eḷakaloma origin** (body; body–mind), **by doing, no release by perception, without intention, an offence by convention, bodily action, three kinds of consciousness, three feelings**.

**උපනන්ද** තෙරුන් සිය සහෝදරයාගේ සද්ධිවිහාරික භික්ෂුවකට "**එන්න ඇවැත්නි, ගමට පිඬු සිඟා යමු**" යි කැඳවාගෙන ගියහ. එහෙත් ඔහුට කිසිවක් **නොදෙවා**, "**යන්න ඇවැත්නි, ඔබ සමඟ කථා කිරීම, හිඳීම මට පහසු නැත; මම තනිව කථා කරමි, හිඳිමි**" යි ==පිටත් කර හැරියහ==. ඔහු දන් වේලාවට ආපසු එන්නට නොහැකිව **දානය මඟ හැරී, දුර්වල** විය. භික්ෂූහු දොස් කීහ; භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ ශික්ෂාපදය පැනවූහ.
## (අ) පද විවරණය
**දාපෙත්වා වා අදාපෙත්වා වා** — යම් ආමිසයක් **දෙවා හෝ නොදෙවා**.
**උය්‍යොජෙය්‍ය** — **මාතුගාමයා සමඟ සිනාසීම, ක්‍රීඩා කිරීම, රහසින් හිඳීම** ආදිය කිරීමට කැමතිව "යන්න" ආදිය කියා පිටත් කරයි.
**එතදෙව** — **මේ අනාචාරය ම** හේතු කොට — වෙනත් සුදුසු හේතුවක් නිසා නොවේ.

## (ඇ) ප්‍රඥප්තිය හා ආණත්තිය
**සැවැත් නුවරදී** **උපනන්ද** තෙරුන් අරබයා පිටත් කර හැරීමේ වස්තුවෙහි පැනවිණි. **සාධාරණ ප්‍රඥප්තියකි**. "**පිටත් කරන්න**" යි අණ කිරීමෙන් ද වන බැවින් **සාණත්තිකයි**. **තික පාචිත්තියකි**.

## (ඉ) අනාපත්ති
# "**අපි දෙදෙනා එකට යැපිය නොහැක**" ආදී **සුදුසු හේතුවකින්** පිටත් කිරීම — උදා: මිල අධික බඩුවක් දැක ලෝභ ඇති වේ යැයි, ස්ත්‍රියක දැක කලකිරේ යැයි, ගිලනෙකුට හෝ ආරාමයට කැඳ, බත ගෙනයාමට.

## (උ) සමුට්ඨානාදිය
**තිසමුට්ඨානයි** (කාය-චිත්ත, වාචා-චිත්ත, කාය-වාචා-චිත්ත), **කිරියයි, සඤ්ඤාවිමොක්ඛයි, සචිත්තකයි, ලෝකවජ්ජයි, කාය-වචීකම්මයි, අකුසලචිත්තයි, තිවේදනයි**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
Ven. **Upananda** said to a co-resident pupil of his brother, “**Come, friend, let's go to the village for alms,**” and took him along. But **without having anything given to him**, he ==sent him away==: “**Go, friend; talking or sitting with you isn't comfortable for me; I talk and sit better alone.**” The monk could not get back in time for the meal, **missed it and grew weak**. The monks criticised Upananda, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Dāpetvā vā adāpetvā vā** — **having had food given to him or not**.
**Uyyojeyya** — wishing to **laugh, play, sit in private** etc. with a woman, he sends him off with “Go” or the like.
**Etadeva** — **for that misconduct alone** — not for any other proper reason.
## (B) How the offences fall
|! Stage | Offence
| merely sending him away | DK
| the monk sent away passing **the range of sight** or **of hearing** **with the first foot** | DK
| **with the second foot** | PC
**The range** — in the open, **twelve cubits** (for both sight and hearing). If **walls, doors, ramparts** etc. intervene, **being screened by them** is itself passing the range.
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning **Upananda**, on the matter of sending a monk away. It is a **common rule**. Since it also happens by ordering “Send him away,” it is **sāṇattika**. A **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Sending an ordained person away for misconduct and his passing the range — perceiving him as ordained, in doubt, or as unordained (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| Sending away **an unordained person** — in all three perceptions (**triple dukkaṭa**) | DK
| **Saddling both with a quarrel** | DK
## (E) No offence
# Sending him away for **a proper reason** such as “**The two of us can't get by together**” — e.g. lest greed arise on seeing costly goods, lest discontent arise on seeing a woman; to take gruel or rice to a sick monk or to the monastery.
# Sending him away **on business, without wishing to misbehave**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Wishing to misbehave**.
# Sending an ordained person away **for that very purpose**.
# The one sent away **passing the range**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Three origins** (body–mind, speech–mind, body–speech–mind), **by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily and verbal action, unwholesome mind, three feelings**.

## කථා වස්තුව
@ සැවැත් නුවර · ජේතවනාරාමය

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
Ven. **Upananda** went to a friend's house and ==sat down in the **bedroom** with his wife==. The husband came, bowed, sat down and told his wife, “**Give the venerable alms**”; she did. Then he said, “**Venerable sir, you have eaten; now please go.**” The wife, **overcome by passion**, said, “Sit, venerable sir; don't go.” A second and a third time the same happened. Going out, the husband told the monks, “**Ven. Upananda is sitting in the bedroom with my wife; told to go, he won't; we have much to do.**” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Sabhojane** — a house “**with both people**” (saha ubhohi janehi); or “**sabhoga**” — **to a man overcome by passion the woman is an object of enjoyment, and the man to the woman**. Hence the word-analysis says: “**There is a woman and a man; both have not gone out; both are not free of passion.**”
**Anupakhajja nisajjaṃ kappeyya** — **intrudes and sits down**.
## (B) The limit — the bedroom
In that family's **bedroom (sayanighara)** —
|! Room | Offence limit
| a **large** room, as in a great four-sided hall | sitting **beyond arm's reach from the back wall**, near the bed inside
| a **small** room | sitting **past the middle**
Sitting thus is pācittiya.
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning **Upananda**, on the matter of intruding and sitting down. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Sitting past the limit in a bedroom — perceiving it as a bedroom, in doubt, or as not (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| Not a bedroom, perceived as one or in doubt | DK
## (E) No offence
# Perceiving it **as not a bedroom**.
# Sitting **without passing the limit**.
# **A second monk** is present.
# **Both have gone out**.
# **Both are free of passion**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# A husband and wife **not free of passion** being **present**.
# **A bedroom**.
# **No second monk**.
# **Intruding and sitting down**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**First-pārājika origin** (body–mind), **by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily action, unwholesome mind, two feelings** (pleasant / neutral).

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
After the previous rule was laid down, Ven. **Upananda** went to the same family and ==sat **in private on a screened seat**== with that woman. The husband complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Mātugāmena** — **a human female** — **even a girl born that day**; not a yakkhinī, peta-woman or animal.
**Raho** — **private to the eye** — a place where one **cannot be seen** when the eyes blink, the brows are raised or the head is lifted.
**Paṭicchannaṃ āsanaṃ** — a seat **screened** by anything — wall, door, mat, curtain, tree, pillar, sack.
**Nisajjaṃ kappeyya** — when the woman is seated the monk sits or lies close by, when the monk is seated the woman does — or **both** do.
## (B) With the Aniyata rules
This rule corresponds to the pācittiya part of **the first Aniyata**.
|! Rule | Place | Who breaks the privacy
| **Pācittiya 44** | a **screened** seat (private to the eye) | a **discerning man** with eyes, within 12 cubits
| **Pācittiya 45** | alone in an **open** place (private to the ear) | a discerning **woman or man**, not blind, **not deaf**
| **Pācittiya 30** | with **a nun** | a discerning **woman or man**, not blind and **not deaf**
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning **Upananda**, on the matter of sitting on a screened seat. It is a **common rule** (for nuns, with a man), **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Sitting or lying in private on a screened seat with a human woman — perceiving her as a woman, in doubt, or as not (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| With **a yakkhinī, peta-woman, paṇḍaka, or a female animal in human form** | DK
| Preliminary efforts in going there desiring privacy | DK
## (E) No offence
# A **discerning man** with eyes is present within twelve cubits.
# For one **standing**.
# For one **not seeking privacy**.
# For one sitting **with his mind on other business**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# **A woman**.
# **A screened (private) place**.
# **No discerning second person** — seeking privacy.
# **Sitting or lying down**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**First-pārājika origin, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily action, unwholesome mind, two feelings**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
Thinking, “Sitting on a screened seat is forbidden,” Ven. **Upananda** went to the same family and ==sat **alone with the woman, private to the ear**==. The husband complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Eko ekāya** — just the monk and the woman.
**Raho** — here **private to the ear** — where **ordinary talk cannot be heard**, even if not private to the eye.
**Mātugāmo** — **a living human woman**, even one born that day. **Nisajjaṃ kappeyya** — sits; **lying down** is included.
## (B) With the second Aniyata
This rule corresponds to its **pācittiya** part and forms one section with **Pācittiya 30**.
|! Person present | Privacy broken?
| a **discerning woman or man**, neither blind nor deaf — within 12 cubits | ✓ broken — no offence
| **a deaf person with eyes** | ✕ not broken
| **a blind person who can hear** | ✕ not broken
| one **asleep**, **a small child**, one not discerning | ✕ not broken
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning **Upananda**, on the matter of sitting in private. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Sitting or lying alone in private with a human woman — perceiving her as a woman, in doubt, or as not (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| With a yakkhinī, peta-woman, paṇḍaka, or a female animal in human form | DK
| Preliminary efforts in going there desiring privacy | DK
## (E) No offence
# A **discerning second person**, neither blind nor deaf, is present.
# For one **standing**.
# For one **not seeking privacy**.
# For one sitting **with his mind on other business**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# **A woman**.
# **A place private to the ear**.
# **No discerning second person** — seeking privacy.
# **Sitting or lying down**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**First-pārājika origin, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily action, unwholesome mind, two feelings**.

## කථා වස්තුව
@ රජගහ නුවර · වේළුවනය කලන්දක නිවාපය
**උපනන්ද** තෙරුන්ට උපස්ථාන කරන පවුලක් ඔහු ද සමඟ භික්ෂූන් **දානයට** ආරාධනා කළේය. උපනන්ද තෙරුන් ==දානයට පෙර **වෙනත් පවුල්වලට ගොස්**== බොහෝ වේලා ගත කළහ. භික්ෂූහු "ආර්‍ය උපනන්ද ආ පසු දෙමු" යි දායකයා නැවැත්වූ හෙයින් ප්‍රමාද වී පැමිණි තෙරුන් නිසා ==භික්ෂූන්ට **ප්‍රමාණවත් ලෙස වැළඳීමට නොහැකි** විය==. භාග්‍යවතුන් වහන්සේ ශික්ෂාපදය පැනවූහ.
පසුව සිවුරු දෙන කාලයේ, සිවුරු කරන කාලයේ, සහ **දානයට පසු** යාම ගැන ද, **භික්ෂුවක් නැති විට** ද භික්ෂූන් කුකුස් ඇති කරගත් හෙයින් **අනුප්‍රඥප්ති සතරක්** — "**සන්තං භික්ඛුං, අනාපුච්ඡා, පුරෙභත්තං පච්ඡාභත්තං, අඤ්ඤත්‍ර සමයා**" — පැනවූහ.
## (අ) පද විවරණය
**නිමන්තිතො** — **භෝජන පහෙන්** එකකට ආරාධිත.
**සභත්තො සමානො** — ඒ **ආරාධනා භත්තයෙන් යුක්ත** ව සිටියදී.
**සන්තං භික්ඛුං අනාපුච්ඡා** — **උපචාර සීමාව ඇතුළත, දර්ශන උපචාරයේ** සිටින, **සාමාන්‍ය කටහඬින් දැනුම් දිය හැකි** භික්ෂුවකට "**මම අසවලාගේ නිවසට යමි**" හෝ "**චාරිත්තයට පිළිපදිමි**" යි **නොදන්වා**.
**පුරෙභත්තං වා පච්ඡාභත්තං වා** — ආරාධිත භත්තය **වැළඳීමට පෙර හෝ පසු**.
**කුලෙසු චාරිත්තං ආපජ්ජෙය්‍ය** — ආරාධිත පවුල හැර **අන් පවුල්වලට** ඇතුළු වෙයි.
**සමයො** — **චීවරදාන සමය** (කඨින නැති විට කත්තික මාසය, ඇති විට මාස පහ) හා **චීවරකාර සමය**.

**කථින සමුට්ඨානයි, කිරියාකිරියයි** (ඇතුළු වීම කිරියාව; නොදැන්වීම අකිරියාව), **නෝසඤ්ඤාවිමොක්ඛයි, අචිත්තකයි, පණ්ණත්තිවජ්ජයි, කාය-වචීකම්මයි, තිචිත්තයි, තිවේදනයි**.

## The Story
@ Rājagaha · Veḷuvana, the squirrels' feeding place
A family that supported Ven. **Upananda** invited the monks, him included, to **a meal**. Upananda ==went **visiting other families** before the meal== and spent a long time. The monks held the donor back, saying, “Let's give when Ven. Upananda comes,” and because he arrived late ==the monks **could not eat their fill**==. The Blessed One laid down the rule.
Later, as monks became scrupulous at robe-giving time, robe-making time, about going **after the meal**, and when **no monk was present** to inform, he added **four supplementary rules** — “**santaṃ bhikkhuṃ, anāpucchā, purebhattaṃ pacchābhattaṃ, aññatra samayā**.”
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Nimantito** — invited to **one of the five foods**.
**Sabhatto samāno** — while **provided with that invited meal**.
**Santaṃ bhikkhuṃ anāpucchā** — **without informing** a monk who is **within the precinct boundary, in sight**, and **can be told in an ordinary voice**: “**I am going to so-and-so's house**” or “**I am going visiting**.”
**Purebhattaṃ vā pacchābhattaṃ vā** — **before or after** eating the invited meal.
**Kulesu cārittaṃ āpajjeyya** — enters **families other than** the one that invited him.
**Samayo** — **robe-giving time** (Kattika without kaṭhina, five months with it) and **robe-making time**.
## (B) How the offences fall
Apart from the two occasions, entering another family's house **before midday** —
|! Step | Offence
| entering **the house precinct** | DK
| crossing **the threshold with the first foot** | DK
| **with the second foot** | PC
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Rājagaha** concerning **Upananda**, on the matter of visiting families. “**Santaṃ bhikkhuṃ, anāpucchā, purebhattaṃ pacchābhattaṃ, aññatra samayā**” — a **fourfold supplementary rule**. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Entering the house precinct; the first foot | DK
| Without an occasion, without informing a monk, entering another family's house — perceiving himself as invited, in doubt, or as not (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| Not invited, perceiving himself as invited, or in doubt | DK
## (E) No offence
# **On an occasion** (robe-giving, robe-making).
# **Having informed a monk present**; entering without informing **when no monk is present**.
# Going by **a path that runs through another's house or house precinct**.
# Going to **a monastery, nuns' quarters, sectarian lodging, assembly hall, or the meal-giver's house**.
# **In emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= pañca — five)
# **Accepting an invitation** to one of the five foods.
# **Not informing a monk present**.
# Entering **a house other than the meal-giver's**.
# **Before midday has passed**.
# **No occasion or emergency**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Kathina origin, by doing and not doing** (entering; not informing), **no release by perception, without intention, an offence by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of consciousness, three feelings**.

## The Story
@ Kapilavatthu · Nigrodhārāma
When **Mahānāma the Sakyan** had received plenty of **ghee**, with the Blessed One's permission he ==invited the Saṅgha to ask for **medicines for four months**==. The monks asked for little, so medicine was left over; he invited them **for another four months** (a renewed invitation), and later **for life** (a permanent invitation).
**The group-of-six** ==asked for ghee at an unsuitable time==. When told, “**Venerable sirs, there's no ghee today; please wait till it is brought**,” they reproached him — “**You invited us and won't give?**” — and asked for what was hard to get. People complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule (the renewed and permanent invitations being **two supplementary rules**).
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Catumāsappaccayapavāraṇā** — a four-month invitation to medicines — said **because of the incident**. The meaning: **a four-month, renewed or permanent invitation** — ==all **should be accepted**==; one should not refuse saying, “**I have no illness now**,” but accept it thinking, “**When I am ill I will ask**.”
**Tato ce uttari sādiyeyya** — asking **beyond the limit** of the invitation.
## (B) Kinds of limit
|! Kind of invitation | How the offence arises
| limited **by nights** (“for so many days”) | asking **after those nights**
| limited **by medicines** (“so much medicine”) | asking for **other medicines** or **more**
| limited by both | exceeding either
| **unlimited** | no offence
| — | asking for medicine **when there is no need**, or **one medicine when another is needed**
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down **among the Sakyans** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of asking for medicines. “**Punapavāraṇā, niccapavāraṇā**” are two supplementary rules. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| When not ill, asking for medicine beyond the limit of a Saṅgha invitation — perceiving it as beyond, in doubt, or as within (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| Within the limit, perceived as beyond or in doubt | DK
## (E) No offence
# Asking for **the invited medicines within the invited period**.
# Asking for **other** or **more** medicines, or **after the period**, **stating the facts when there is need** (“We need this; though the days have passed, I am ill”).
# From **relatives**; from those who gave **a personal invitation**; from those who gave **an unlimited invitation**.
# **For another**; with **one's own funds**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# **A Saṅgha invitation**.
# **Asking for medicine** under it.
# **Not ill** (no need).
# **Exceeding the limit**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Go-between origin** (six origins), **by doing, no release by perception, without intention, an offence by convention, bodily and verbal action, three kinds of consciousness, three feelings**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**King Pasenadi of Kosala** set out with his army for battle. **The group-of-six** ==went to **watch the army**==. Seeing them, the king asked, “**Venerable sirs, why have you come?**” “**To see you, great king.**” “**What is the use of seeing me going to fight? Shouldn't you see the Blessed One?**” — and he sent molasses to the Blessed One. People complained, “**We came for our livelihood; why have these come?**” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
Later, a monk called to visit **his uncle**, who had fallen ill in the army, was scrupulous, and the Blessed One added “**aññatra tathārūpappaccayā**.”
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Uyyuttaṃ** — **ready to march** — **having left** the village.
**Senaṃ** — **fourfold**: **elephants, horses, chariots, infantry**.
**Aññatra tathārūpappaccayā** — going **only to see the army**, **without such a reason** (a relative or a devotee being ill, etc.).
## (B) How the offences fall
|! Stage | Offence
| **each step** in going to see it | DK
| seeing it **from within the range of sight** | PC
| leaving the range and seeing it **again and again** | PC **for each effort**
| going to see **a single division** — elephants, horses etc. | DK in the same way
**Range of sight** — the place from where he sees it.
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of seeing an army. “**Aññatra tathārūpappaccayā**” is a supplementary rule. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Each step in going | DK
| Seeing an army that has set out from within the range of sight — perceiving it as set out, in doubt, or as not (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| Going to see a single division, elephants etc. | DK
| An army not set out, perceived as set out or in doubt | DK
## (E) No offence
# Perceiving it **as not set out**.
# Seeing it **while staying in the monastery**; seeing it **come to where one stands**; seeing it **coming the opposite way** on the road.
# With **such a reason**.
# **In emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# **Going to see an army that has set out**.
# Seeing it **other than from an allowed place**.
# **No such reason**.
# **No emergency**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Eḷakaloma origin, by doing, no release by perception, without intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily action, unwholesome mind, three feelings**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six**, having gone to the army for some reason, ==stayed with the army **for more than three nights**==. People complained, “**Why are these staying with the army?**” The Blessed One laid down: “**A monk who goes to an army for some reason may stay with it two or three nights; if he stays longer — pācittiya.**”
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Siyā ca tassa bhikkhuno koci deva paccayo** — when he has gone to the army for **such a reason** (a relative being ill etc.).
**Dirattatirattaṃ** — **two or three nights**.
**Tato ce uttari** — **beyond three nights** — if **at sunset on the fourth day** he is in the army, **standing, sitting or lying** — ==even keeping some posture **in the air by psychic power**== — it is pācittiya.
## (B) Counting the nights
|! Situation | Ruling
| **one, two or three nights** with the army | no offence
| **sunset on the fourth day** in the army | PC
| **leaving before dawn of the third night** and coming back to stay | no offence — three nights count afresh
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of staying with an army more than three nights. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Staying with an army more than three nights — perceiving it as more, in doubt, or as fewer (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| Fewer than three nights, perceived as more or in doubt | DK
## (E) No offence
# Staying **two or three nights** or fewer.
# **Leaving before dawn of the third night** and staying again.
# Staying **because of illness** or **to look after a sick person**.
# When the army is **besieged by an enemy army**.
# For one **obstructed** by something.
# **In emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Exceeding three nights**.
# **The sun setting** while in the army.
# **No illness etc.**
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Eḷakaloma origin, by doing, no release by perception, without intention, an offence by convention, bodily action, three kinds of consciousness, three feelings**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
While staying two or three nights with an army, **the group-of-six** ==went to watch **battle drills, roll-calls, the army's formation and its divisions**==. One of them was **struck by an arrow**. People mocked him: “**Venerable sir, did you fight well? How many did you get?**” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Uyyodhikaṃ** — the place where they advance again and again **to fight** — **a battlefield or drill ground**.
**Balaggaṃ** — the place where **the strength of the force is known** — **where the army is counted**.
**Senābyūhaṃ** — the **arrangement** of the army — **its encampment**.
**Anīkadassanaṃ** — seeing **a division of the army**.
|! Division (minimum unit) | Reckoning
| **elephant division** | “**an elephant with twelve men**” — **three** such elephants
| **cavalry division** | “**a horse with three men**” — **three** such horses
| **chariot division** | “**a chariot with four men**” — **three** such chariots
| **infantry division** | **four men bearing weapons**
## (B) How the offences fall
|! Stage | Offence
| **each step** in going to see any of these | DK
| seeing it **from within the range of sight** | PC
| leaving the range and seeing it **again and again** | PC **for each effort**
==here there are **no further divisions of offence**==.
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six, on the matter of going to battle drills. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, and a **triple pācittiya**.
## (D) Division of offences — according to the Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī
|! Case | Offence
| Each step in going | DK
| Seeing a drill, roll-call, formation or division from within the range of sight (**triple pācittiya**) | PC
| Leaving the range and seeing it again — each effort | PC
## (E) No offence
# Seeing it **while staying in the monastery**; seeing fighting that **comes to where one stands**; seeing what one meets **on the road**.
# With **such a reason**.
# **In emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= cattāri — four)
# **Going to see** a battle drill or the like.
# Seeing it **other than from an allowed place**.
# **No such reason**.
# **No emergency**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Eḷakaloma origin, by doing, no release by perception, without intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily action, unwholesome mind, three feelings**.
