## The Story
@ Kosambī · Ghositārāma
A **brahmin** had prepared **a milk drink** for the Saṅgha. The monks ==drank the milk **with a slurping sound**==. A monk **who had been an actor** said ==“**I think the whole Saṅgha has caught a chill**”== — **a joke about the Saṅgha**. Monks of few wishes criticised him. The Blessed One rebuked him, said “**Monks, one should not joke about the Buddha, the Dhamma or the Saṅgha; whoever does so: dukkaṭa**,” and laid down the rule on slurping as well.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Surusurukārakaṃ** — ==eating or drinking **making the sound “suru suru”**==.
## (B) Joking about the Triple Gem (dava)
**Davo** — **words of mockery**. ==**One should not joke about the Three Jewels in any way at all**== (Samantapāsādikā), for example:
|! Jewel | Forms of mockery
| **Buddha** | “What Buddha? **A silaka-Buddha**? **A paṭibuddha**?”
| **Dhamma** | “What Dhamma? **A cow-dhamma**? **A goat-dhamma**?”
| **Saṅgha** | “What Saṅgha? **A herd of deer**? **A herd of cattle**?”
Doing so is **dukkaṭa**.
## (C) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Kosambī** concerning a number of monks. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (D) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Eating or drinking with a slurping sound **out of disrespect** | DK
## (E) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (F) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Eating with a slurping sound**.
## (G) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**First-pārājika origin, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

#en
## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==ate **licking their bowls**==. People complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Pattanillehakaṃ** — ==eating **licking the bowl again and again**==. ==The bowl should not be licked **even with one finger**==.
**Parittake sese** — when **only a little is left**, **gathering it together** and licking it up is **no offence**.
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (C) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Eating while licking the bowl **out of disrespect** | DK
## (D) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# Gathering together and licking up **a small remainder**.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Eating while licking the bowl**.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**First-pārājika origin, by doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, bodily action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling**.

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==taught Dhamma to **people holding an umbrella**==. Monks of few wishes complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Chattaṃ** — **three kinds** of umbrella:
|! Umbrella | Meaning
| **setacchatta** | **a white umbrella covered with cloth**
| **kilañjacchatta** | **a wicker (split-cane) umbrella**
| **paṇṇacchatta** | one made of **palm leaves** or other leaves
**Maṇḍalabaddha, salākabaddha** — said to show that all three are **bound with rings and ribs**. **A single-leaf umbrella** with **its own stalk** is also an umbrella.
**Chattapāṇi** — ==as long as he **does not let go of it with his hand** — **holding it up, resting it on the shoulder or on the thigh**== — one may not teach him. ==If **someone else holds it**, or it stands on an umbrella stand, **as soon as it leaves his hand**== he is not “holding an umbrella” — one may teach him.
**Dhammo** — ==what is **spoken by the Buddha, by disciples, by sages or by deities**, **connected with the meaning and with the Dhamma**==.
|! Way of teaching | Offence
| teaching **word by word** | DK **for each word**
| teaching **syllable by syllable** | DK **for each syllable**
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. There is **one amendment on account of illness** — ==when monks hesitated to teach someone ill, people complained, “**How can they not teach Dhamma to one who is ill?**”, and “**not ill**” was added==. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (C) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Teaching Dhamma to one holding an umbrella who is not ill **out of disrespect** | DK
## (D) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Teaching one holding an umbrella who is not ill**.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Dhamma-teaching origin** (speech–mind), **by doing and not doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling** (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī summary).

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==taught Dhamma to **people holding a staff**==. Monks of few wishes complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Daṇḍo** — ==a staff **four cubits long by a medium-sized man's measure**==. **One longer** or **one shorter** is “**not a staff**.”
**Daṇḍapāṇi** — as long as he **does not let go of it with his hand**, one may not teach him; **as soon as it leaves his hand**, one may.
**Dhammo** — ==what is **spoken by the Buddha, by disciples, by sages or by deities**, **connected with the meaning and with the Dhamma**==.
|! Way of teaching | Offence
| teaching **word by word** | DK **for each word**
| teaching **syllable by syllable** | DK **for each syllable**
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. There is **one amendment on account of illness** — ==when monks hesitated to teach someone ill, people complained, “**How can they not teach Dhamma to one who is ill?**”, and “**not ill**” was added==. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (C) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Teaching Dhamma to one holding a staff who is not ill **out of disrespect** | DK
## (D) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Teaching one holding a staff who is not ill**.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Dhamma-teaching origin** (speech–mind), **by doing and not doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling** (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī summary).

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana monastery
**The group-of-six** ==taught Dhamma to **people holding a weapon (bow)**==. Monks of few wishes complained, and the Blessed One laid down the rule.
## (A) Explanation of terms
**Āvudhaṃ** — though the Pāli says “**a bow, a cross-bow**,” ==**every kind of bow together with every kind of arrow** is to be understood as a weapon== (Samantapāsādikā). So —
|! In his hand | Ruling
| **a bow with an arrow** | not allowable to teach
| **a bow alone**, or **an arrow alone** | not allowable
| a bow **strung** or **unstrung** | not allowable
| a bow **hung on the neck (Samantapāsādikā) / shoulder (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī)** — **as long as he does not take it in hand** | allowable
The same whether he is standing or sitting.
**Dhammo** — ==what is **spoken by the Buddha, by disciples, by sages or by deities**, **connected with the meaning and with the Dhamma**==.
|! Way of teaching | Offence
| teaching **word by word** | DK **for each word**
| teaching **syllable by syllable** | DK **for each syllable**
## (B) The laying down of the rule and the command
It was laid down at **Sāvatthī** concerning the group-of-six. There is **one amendment on account of illness** — ==when monks hesitated to teach someone ill, people complained, “**How can they not teach Dhamma to one who is ill?**”, and “**not ill**” was added==. It is a **common rule**, **anāṇattika**, **dukkaṭa only**.
## (C) Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Teaching Dhamma to one holding a weapon (bow) who is not ill **out of disrespect** | DK
## (D) No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**; **not knowing**.
# When **ill**.
# In **emergencies**.
# For the insane and so on, and the first offender.
## (E) Factors (= tīṇi — three)
# **Disrespect**.
# **No reason for exemption**.
# **Teaching one holding a weapon who is not ill**.
## (F) Origin and the rest (samuṭṭhāna)
**Dhamma-teaching origin** (speech–mind), **by doing and not doing, release by perception, with intention, blameworthy by nature, verbal action, unwholesome mind, painful feeling** (Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī summary).
