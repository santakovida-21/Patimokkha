## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana
**The group-of-six monks** ==wore their lower robes hanging down in front and behind==. People criticised this: “Like householders who enjoy sensual pleasures.” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## Explanation of terms
**Parimaṇḍalaṃ** — covering the navel above and both knees below, hanging no lower than about an eighth of the shin below the knee.
## Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Out of disrespect — wore their lower robes hanging down in front and behind | DK
| Unintentionally, unmindfully, not knowing, or when ill | N
## No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**;
# **Not knowing**; when **ill**; in **emergencies**;
# When there are sores on the legs;
# For the insane and so on, and for the first offenders.
## Factors
# **Doing (or not doing)** what the rule describes.
# **Disrespect** (*anādariya*).
# **No exemption** applies (illness and so on).

# ශික්ෂාපදයේ දැක්වෙන **ක්‍රියාව කිරීම / නොකිරීම**.
# **අනාදරය** (අගෞරවය).
# **අනාපත්ති කරුණක් නොවීම** (ගිලන් බව ආදිය නොමැති වීම).
#en
## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana
**The group-of-six monks** ==went about in inhabited areas with their bodies exposed==. People criticised this: “Like householders who enjoy sensual pleasures.” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## Explanation of terms
**Supaṭicchanno** — with the body well covered up to the neck and the wrists. **Antaragharaṃ** — among the houses of a village.
## Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Out of disrespect — went about in inhabited areas with their bodies exposed | DK
| Unintentionally, unmindfully, not knowing, or when ill | N
## No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**;
# **Not knowing**; when **ill**; in **emergencies**;
# When in a dwelling;
# For the insane and so on, and for the first offenders.
## Factors
# **Doing (or not doing)** what the rule describes.
# **Disrespect** (*anādariya*).
# **No exemption** applies (illness and so on).

#en
## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana
**The group-of-six monks** ==sat with their heads covered==. People criticised this: “Like householders who enjoy sensual pleasures.” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Out of disrespect — sat with their heads covered | DK
| Unintentionally, unmindfully, not knowing, or when ill | N
## No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**;
# **Not knowing**; when **ill**; in **emergencies**;
# Because of cold or illness;
# For the insane and so on, and for the first offenders.
## Factors
# **Doing (or not doing)** what the rule describes.
# **Disrespect** (*anādariya*).
# **No exemption** applies (illness and so on).

#en
## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana
**The group-of-six monks** ==taught the Dhamma to people holding umbrellas==. Monks of few wishes criticised this. The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## Explanation of terms
These rules are laid down out of respect for the Dhamma.
## Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Out of disrespect — taught the Dhamma to people holding umbrellas | DK
| Unintentionally, unmindfully, not knowing, or when ill | N
## No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**;
# **Not knowing**; when **ill**; in **emergencies**;
# When the listener is ill;
# For the insane and so on, and for the first offenders.
## Factors
# **Doing (or not doing)** what the rule describes.
# **Disrespect** (*anādariya*).
# **No exemption** applies (illness and so on).

#en
## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana
**The group-of-six monks** ==taught the Dhamma to people with their heads wrapped==. Monks of few wishes criticised this. The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## Explanation of terms
There is no offence when the hair is visible.
## Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Out of disrespect — taught the Dhamma to people with their heads wrapped | DK
| Unintentionally, unmindfully, not knowing, or when ill | N
## No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**;
# **Not knowing**; when **ill**; in **emergencies**;
# When the listener is ill; when the hair is exposed;
# For the insane and so on, and for the first offenders.
## Factors
# **Doing (or not doing)** what the rule describes.
# **Disrespect** (*anādariya*).
# **No exemption** applies (illness and so on).

## The Story
@ Sāvatthī · Jetavana
**The group-of-six monks** ==defecated and urinated in water==. People criticised this: “Like householders who enjoy sensual pleasures.” The Blessed One laid down the rule.
## Division of offences
|! Case | Offence
| Out of disrespect — defecated and urinated in water | DK
| Unintentionally, unmindfully, not knowing, or when ill | N
## No offence
# **Unintentionally**; **unmindfully**;
# **Not knowing**; when **ill**; in **emergencies**;
# When ill; when done on land and water flows over it;
# For the insane and so on, and for the first offenders.
## Factors
# **Doing (or not doing)** what the rule describes.
# **Disrespect** (*anādariya*).
# **No exemption** applies (illness and so on).
